---

**NARRATIVE:**

The problem BioMate solves is not new — it has been the defining bottleneck in life science for decades. What is new is that AI now makes it solvable.

Computationally demanding biomedical work — ADMET profiling, genomic variant analysis, PBPK simulation, cryo-EM data processing, IND filing computational sections — sits at the center of every drug development program, every clinical study, every regulatory submission. It is also the stage most likely to become the rate-limiting step.

The cost of the status quo is concrete. A bioinformatics consultant charges $150 to $300 per hour. The analytical component of a CRO engagement can reach $10,000 to $100,000 and take weeks to deliver. An in-house computational team costs $150,000 to $200,000 per head per year — beyond reach for most biotech companies and academic labs. And when results finally arrive, there is a deeper problem: for regulatory submissions and peer review, every computational decision must be traceable. Generic AI tools cannot provide this. They hallucinate tool configurations, invent parameters, and produce outputs that cannot be cited or defended.

The result: programs move slower than they should, decisions get made on incomplete data, and computational bottlenecks erode the return on every upstream wet-lab investment.

**NARRATIVE:**
