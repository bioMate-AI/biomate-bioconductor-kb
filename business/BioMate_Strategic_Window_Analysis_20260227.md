## Framework Summary

The Strategic Window Analysis evaluates whether a business opportunity is worth pursuing by scoring two independent dimensions:

- **Product/Company Fit** = Weighted Strength Score − Weighted Weakness Score (max 100)
- **Product/Market Fit** = Weighted Opportunity Score − Weighted Threat Score (max 100)

**Strategic Window threshold: both scores must reach 70 or above.**

Scoring scale: 1–100 (1 = very low, 100 = very high). Each dimension is classified as a Strength OR a Weakness (not both); weights across all internal dimensions sum to 100%, as do all external dimensions.

## IV. Gap Analysis

The table below shows the five weakness dimensions and what score improvement is needed to open the strategic window.

| Dimension | Current Status | Current Score | Target Score | Points Gained | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Financial resources** | Bootstrapped, limited runway | 18 | 65 (break-even + seed) | +3.76 | 1 — Critical |
| **Customer relationship** | Closed beta, no sustained users | 15 | 65 (active beta cohort) | +2.50 | 1 — Critical |
| **Patent / IP** | Patent-pending, no grants | 30 | 60 (provisional filed) | +1.80 | 2 — High |
| **Brand image** | Unknown publicly | 12 | 55 (public launch) | +1.72 | 2 — High |
| **Sales force strength** | No function | 10 | 50 (channel identified) | +1.60 | 3 — Medium |

**Points needed to reach 70:** 70 − 52.74 = **17.26 points**

**Achievable by:** Fixing Financial Resources + Customer Relationship + Patent/IP alone adds ~8 points. Combined with Brand Image + Sales, total gain would be ~11.38 points. To cross 70, we also need incremental improvements on the strength side (see To-Do section).

**Fastest path to opening the strategic window:**
1. Achieve break-even (78 Pro users) → validates unit economics, improves Financial score
2. Convert 50+ beta users to active, engaged users → builds Customer Relationship score
3. File provisional patents → moves Patent/IP from weakness to managed risk
4. Public launch (Reddit, nf-core, GitHub, Twitter/X) → starts building Brand
5. Strengthen distribution clout (formal Galaxy partnership) → lifts an existing strength

Combined, these would increase Product/Company Fit from **52.74 → ~72**, opening the strategic window.

## IX. Summary for Mentor Discussion

**The honest finding:** BioMate has exceptional product and market positioning, but the strategic window is not yet open for the Individual Researchers segment because the go-to-market capabilities are not yet developed. This is expected and fixable for a pre-launch company.

**What is already strong and requires no major new investment:**
- Technology and R&D depth (scores 85–90)
- Product differentiation (score 90)
- Product-mission fit (score 95)
- Market opportunity (nearly at the 70 threshold on its own)

**What must be built to open the window:**
- Financial stability (break-even or seed round)
- Customer relationships (beta → paying users, NPS data)
- Patent/IP protection (provisional filings)
- Brand presence (public launch, community)

**The path:** If these four actions are executed in the next 60–90 days, the Product/Company Fit score should rise from 52.74 to approximately **70–72**, opening the strategic window.

**The Beta Stabilization and Evidence-Based Model frameworks** (from the spreadsheet) further confirm: the company must not scale marketing, sales hiring, or enterprise outreach until Steps 4–6 of the evidence model are validated. The current 90-day priority is to collect real user evidence, not to grow.

**Segment recommendation:** Begin with Individual Researchers (easiest buyer, fastest evidence collection), then use validated evidence to open the Academic Labs window, and ultimately build toward Pharma/Enterprise once brand, sales, and IP are in place.
