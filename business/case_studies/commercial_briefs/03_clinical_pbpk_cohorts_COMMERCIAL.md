## CASE BRIEF
# A Dosing Answer for Every Patient Population — Not Just the Average 70 kg Adult

### THE PROBLEM
Drugs behave differently in a neonate, a pregnant patient, someone in kidney failure, or a 90-year-old — and regulators require you to justify the dose for each of these populations. Today that means six-figure specialist software (Simcyp, PK-Sim), a scarce PBPK modeller, and two-to-four weeks of work per cohort. Most teams simply can't cover every population in time.

### THE BIOMATE AI WORKFLOW
BioMate provides a physiologically based pharmacokinetics (PBPK) workflow tuned to each specific patient cohort. From a plain-language request it selects the right model — pediatric (across ten age bands), pregnancy (by trimester), kidney or liver impairment, elderly, high body weight, or first-in-human cross-species scaling — adjusts the body's physiology for that population, predicts drug exposure, recommends a cohort-matched dose, and drafts the regulatory "Use in Specific Populations" label text.

### OUTCOME
A drug-development team can produce a dosing rationale and label-ready text for every special population in a single session, instead of triaging which cohorts they have time to model. One validated example — semaglutide — reproduces the real Phase III trial's body-weight result, showing the models track observed clinical outcomes, not just theory.

### TECHNICAL VALIDATION NOTE
The adult PBPK engine is benchmarked against 30+ approved drugs' published human pharmacokinetics using the EMA regulatory qualification criterion, and a full case study is validated against the SUSTAIN-1 Phase III trial. Cohort-specific physiology is parameterized directly from FDA and ICH guidance (ICH E11 pediatric, FDA 2010 renal, FDA 2003 hepatic, FDA 2005 first-in-human).
