## CASE BRIEF
# More Accurate Than a Frontier AI — Because It Uses Trusted Tools, Not Guesswork

### THE PROBLEM
Vendors claim general-purpose AI can "do science" on its own. In practice, an AI that writes and runs its own analysis code produces answers that look plausible but are frequently wrong — a serious risk when the output drives a research or regulatory decision.

### THE BIOMATE AI WORKFLOW
BioMate does not ask an AI to invent analyses. It orchestrates validated, community-trusted tools, applies quality gates, and returns an auditable answer. On independent public benchmarks, this orchestration approach outperforms both leading general-purpose AIs and a specially fine-tuned science model.

### TIME, COST & QUALITY COMPARISON

| Metric | Frontier AI (autonomous) | BioMate AI |
|---|---|---|
| Open-answer bioinformatics accuracy (BixBench) | 18–29% | ≈48% |
| Biomedical reasoning accuracy (BiomniEval1) | ≈65% (fine-tuned model) | 71.3% |
| Answer traceability | None | Full audit trail |
| Failure mode | Confident but wrong / invented tools | Uses validated tools only |

### OUTCOME
Decision-makers get answers they can actually trust and defend, because every result comes from a validated tool with a documented trail — not from an AI's memory. BioMate's measured advantage over both frontier and fine-tuned models is the evidence behind the positioning that orchestration beats autonomous code generation.
