## CASE BRIEF
# Turn a Published Method Into a Runnable Pipeline in Seconds

### THE PROBLEM
Reproducing an analysis from a paper means a scientist reads the Methods section and hand-builds the matching pipeline — 2 to 8 hours of translation work per paper, before a single result is produced.

### THE BIOMATE AI WORKFLOW
BioMate matches a published method to the correct executable pipeline in its catalog of thousands of workflows, so instead of rebuilding the analysis by hand, the researcher starts from the right pipeline immediately.

### TIME, COST & QUALITY COMPARISON

| Metric | Manual Pipeline | BioMate AI |
|---|---|---|
| Time to find the right pipeline | 2–8 hours | Under a second |
| Correct pipeline in top 5 results | Manual searching | 100% (benchmark) |
| Catalog searched | Memory / web search | 2,455 indexed workflows |

### OUTCOME
Researchers can reproduce and extend published work in a fraction of the time, and the catalog grows with each paper — compounding the platform's coverage. *(Today this covers finding the right pipeline; automatically filling its parameters and running the full reproduction end-to-end is on the near-term roadmap.)*

### TECHNICAL VALIDATION NOTE
On a 14-paper curated benchmark using keyword retrieval alone (a conservative lower bound), BioMate placed the correct pipeline in the top five results 100% of the time (Hit@5 = 1.00, MRR = 0.63). Production retrieval adds semantic search and LLM re-ranking on the full catalog. Full technical validation report available on request.

### COMMERCIAL TIER & HOSTING MOAT
Large-DB retrieval tier. The moat is the hosted, executable catalog: 2,455 runnable pipelines indexed for instant matching, versus a static list a competitor would have to rebuild.
