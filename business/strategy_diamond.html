Staging — Speed and Sequence
The right move at the right time. Each phase creates the conditions for the next.
Phase 1
Prove
Months 1–3
  • Free public access — no monetization pressure
  • Goal: demonstrate researchers use BioMate and return to it
  • Collect usage data, workflows run, domains accessed
  • Gather early testimonials and use cases
▸ 1,000 active researchers
Phase 2
Convert
Months 4–9
  • First paying users — Pro and Team tiers launched
  • First institutional pilots from Phase 1 usage patterns
  • First public benchmark data published (speed, cost, reproducibility)
  • Phase 1 data becomes the conversion argument
▸ 10,000 users · 5 institutional partnerships
Phase 3
Scale
Months 10–18
  • Enterprise contracts with formal procurement processes
  • Outcome-based pricing tier introduced alongside subscription
  • Direct pharma and biotech outreach with proven institutional references
  • Geographic expansion begins
▸ First $1M ARR
Phase 4
Compound
Year 2+
  • Outcome-based pricing becomes the default model
  • Platform flywheel engages — usage improves AI, which attracts usage
  • Asia-Pacific institutional expansion
  • Strategic partnerships: CROs, publishers, data repositories
▸ Self-sustaining platform growth
Trust precedes payment  ·  Usage data precedes institutional sales  ·  Institutional credibility precedes enterprise deals
