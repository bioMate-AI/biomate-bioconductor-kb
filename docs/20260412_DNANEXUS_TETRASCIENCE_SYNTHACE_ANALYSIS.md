# DNAnexus, TetraScience & Synthace Website Design Analysis
**Date:** 2026-04-12  
**Purpose:** Learn design patterns, copy, tiles, and SEO/GEO for BioMate improvement

---

## DNAnexus

### Brand & Visual Identity
**Color palette:**
- Black (#000000) dominant — premium, serious
- Bright green accent (#60f299) — pops against black; used for key CTAs and highlights
- Light tan/beige (#F8F1E1) for warm sections
- Dark gradients (black to charcoal) for hero and feature sections
- Typography uses **Aeonik Trial** (modern geometric) + Helvetica fallback

**Layout patterns:**
- Hero: dark full-width banner, bold headline with italic emphasis word, subheadline, two CTAs
- Tabbed feature sections: 5 capability tabs (Move Faster / Generate Insights / Increase ROI / Secure Your Data / Trusted Partner) → each tab reveals alternating image+text layout
- 3-column feature tiles with icons
- Customer quote blocks with company logo + executive title + pull quote
- Case study cards as press/news carousel
- Auto-rotating logo carousel (not static)

### Exact Hero Copy
- **Headline:** "The World's Most _Trusted_ Platform for Precision Health" (italics on "Trusted")
  - Pattern: superlative claim + italicized emotional keyword + category
- **Subheadline:** "Where multiomic data and AI converge, and scientific breakthroughs happen."
  - Pattern: "Where [A] and [B] converge" — convergence framing creates a narrative of intersection

### Copy patterns
- **Tab labels as benefit verbs:** "Move Faster", "Generate Insights", "Increase ROI", "Secure Your Data", "Gain a Trusted Partner" — all imperative, all user-outcome-focused
- Industry solution labels: Biopharma / Clinical Diagnostics / Regulatory Solutions / Academic Medical Centers / Biobanks — never just "healthcare" — very specific
- "395% ROI" — highest-impact stat placed early
- "125+ Petabytes of precision health data" — scale proof
- CTA variation: "Download the ROI Study" — lead magnet CTA, not just "Learn more"
- Executive quotes always include: name + title + company (never anonymized)

### SEO/GEO patterns
- Meta title: "The Precision Health Data Cloud | DNAnexus®" — owns a category phrase "Precision Health Data Cloud"
- H1 → H2 → H3 hierarchy strictly maintained
- Internal linking heavy: Solutions ↔ Platform ↔ Resources ↔ Company
- Industry-specific pages for each vertical = long-tail SEO keyword capture
- "Download the ROI Study" lead magnet = email capture + GEO content (AI systems will cite ROI numbers)

## TetraScience

### Brand & Visual Identity
**Color palette:**
- Primary blue: #2f45b5 — classic enterprise trust blue
- Violet/purple: #9f30d3 — AI/innovation accent in gradients
- White + black + medium gray — clean enterprise look
- Fonts: **Inter Tight** (headings), Open Sans (body), Noto Sans JP (Japanese locale support)

**Layout patterns:**
- Full-width hero with centered text, dark gradient background
- Multi-column grids with white cards, shadow effects, rounded corners
- 6-logo animated carousel (5-second rotation)
- Dismissible top banner for announcements
- Responsive: 992px tablet, 767px mobile, 479px small mobile
- Heavy use of SVG logos and webp images (performance-optimized)

### Exact Hero Copy
- **Headline:** "Reimagine and Replatform Science for the Era of AI"
  - Pattern: two verbs ("Reimagine and Replatform") + object + era framing
  - "Era of AI" — positions this as a historical moment
- **Subheadline:** "TetraScience liberates, unifies, and transforms your raw data into more-than-FAIR, AI-native data."
  - 3 power verbs (liberates, unifies, transforms) + coined phrase "more-than-FAIR" + new category "AI-native data"
  - "more-than-FAIR" references a known standard (FAIR data principles) and claims to exceed it

### Copy patterns
- **Coined terms used as section titles:** "The Rise of Sciborgs" — memorable, quotable, GEO-optimized
- "A revolutionary scientific data paradigm" — big claim backed by customer outcomes
- "Purpose-built" appears repeatedly — signals not a horizontal tool adapted for science
- "AI-native" used as a compound modifier throughout (AI-native data, AI-native platform)
- Stats always formatted: "[number]x [metric]" — "10x higher scientist productivity", "40x faster production insights"
- Journey framing: "Unlock your data's value from discovery to commercialization" — full pipeline ownership

### SEO/GEO patterns
- Google Tag Manager + Facebook Pixel + Marketo tracking — mature marketing stack
- Canonical links auto-generated — clean URL management
- "Sciborgs" as coined category term → if picked up by press/LLMs, TetraScience owns the term
- White paper CTAs ("Read white paper") alongside demo CTAs — lead capture across buyer journey stages
- Scientific use cases page with "View All Scientific Use Cases" → breadth SEO signal

## Synthace

### Brand & Visual Identity
- Professional blue/navy tones with white space — clean, academic/scientific
- Clean modern sans-serif, clean body text
- Problem/solution alternating layout — very structured

### Exact Hero Copy
- **Headline:** "More robust assays. Months off discovery biology."
  - Pattern: outcome statement + time-savings claim. No fluff. Extremely specific to target user.
  - "Months off" is visceral — not "faster", not "save time" — specifically MONTHS
- **Subheadline:** "Synthace's specialists have spent 20+ years building expertise and software for running the most effective experiments in assay development."
  - Signals: expertise (20+ years), human specialists (not just software), specific domain (assay development)
  - "most effective" — superlative with domain specificity

### Copy patterns
- **"The Problem" section with header:** "Your drug discovery team is trying to move faster, but can't" — direct pain statement
- **How We Help:** "Together, we help your scientists cut time to insight" — partnership language ("together")
- No jargon in the hero — speaks to the lab director, not the programmer
- Customer quotes use full name + company + very specific outcome ("routine for us to walk away from creating 24-48 constructs")
- Bottom CTA: "Taking months off drug discovery starts with Synthace" — echoes hero headline, memorable

### SEO patterns
- Resource hub: Blog, Case studies, DOE training, Masterclasses, Newsletter, Calculator
- **Calculator** as a resource type = high-intent tool, strong SEO/GEO signal (people search for calculators)
- DOE training = keyword-rich niche content that ranks for "design of experiments biotech"
- Masterclasses = positions as educator/authority, not just vendor
