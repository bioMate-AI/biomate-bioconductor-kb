# Blog Notes: FDA AI-Enabled Early-Phase Clinical Trial Optimization

Source: `FDA_AI_clinical_drug_window.jpg`

## Core Insight

FDA issued a Request for Information (RFI 2026-08281) specifically soliciting AI approaches for **early-phase clinical trial optimization**. This is a direct regulatory signal that AI-assisted trial design is moving from research to policy.

## Key Application Areas

- **Patient recruitment optimization** — AI screening of EHR, genomics, biomarkers to identify eligible patients faster
- **Adaptive dose escalation** — AI-guided BOIN/mTPI-style decisions replacing static 3+3 rules
- **Go/no-go decision support** — early biomarker signals → futility analysis → pipeline triage
- **Safety signal detection** — multimodal early warning systems combining AE reports + lab trends

## Industry Status

- AstraZeneca and Amgen already piloting AI-enabled Phase I/II trial optimization
- Regulatory acceptance is the bottleneck — FDA RFI signals they are building the framework
- Trial sponsors want AI decision audit trails that can withstand FDA inspection

## Implications

The window is open: FDA is actively soliciting, not just tolerating, AI in early-phase trial design. Whoever builds the validated AI trial optimization stack first gains regulatory credibility and first-mover lock-in with pharma sponsors.
