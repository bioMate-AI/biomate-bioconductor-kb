# Blog Notes: Recent Innovation Directions in Pharma/Drug Development

Source: `recent_innovation_drug_development.jpg`

## 10 Active Innovation Directions (2025-2026)

1. **Early drug risk prediction + multimodal early warning** — multimodal deep learning × alerting models; predict adverse signals before Phase I
2. **Personalized drug response prediction + regimen optimization** — GNN × pharmacogenomics; individual-level dosing from genomic + clinical features
3. **Hard-to-treat disease scenario acceleration + solution optimization** — NLP × knowledge graph construction + reasoning; rare/undruggable indication discovery
4. **Drug-related biological signal discovery + efficacy optimization** — deep learning × multidimensional data integration; biomarker-driven trial enrichment
5. **Drug component and health/adverse response causal inference** — causal inference × ML; separating real mechanism from confounding in real-world evidence
6. **AI platform for expanding existing drug indication scenarios** — knowledge graph embedding × collaborative filtering; drug repurposing at scale
7. **Defect auto-identification + quantitative analysis in drug quality imaging** — attention mechanism × deep learning; QC automation for manufacturing
8. **Drug component image analysis + quantitative quality model** — federated learning × transfer learning; decentralized quality modeling across sites
9. **Drug-related protein-protein interaction network prediction + functional module mining** — GNN × sequence-structure co-modeling; target interaction network mapping
10. **Drug optimization tool efficacy prediction + adaptive tuning** — LSTM × multi-dimensional data joint analysis; continuous optimization models

## Pattern

Most cutting-edge pharma AI work is moving toward: (a) multimodal data fusion, (b) causal reasoning not just correlation, (c) adaptive/agentic loops not one-shot predictions.
