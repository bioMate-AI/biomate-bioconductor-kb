### Front
- Left edge: thin navy accent bar (6mm wide, full height)
- Top-left: BioMate AI logo (use actual logo asset, not text placeholder)
- Below logo: tagline — *"AI Platform for Biomedical Research"* in Open Sans, muted color
- Thin divider line (cream border color)
- Name: **Yaoyun Zhang** — Open Sans Bold, 10–11pt, deep navy
- Title: *Founder & CEO* — Open Sans Regular, 7.5pt, muted
- Contact rows (icon + text):
  - ✉ contact@biomate.ai
  - ⊕ biomate.ai

### Back
- Full navy background (`#14283a`)
- Subtle large circles (white, 8–12% opacity) as decorative background elements
- Centered: **BioMate AI** in large Crimson Text serif, white
- Below: *"AI Platform for Biomedical Research"* — Open Sans, white, 80% opacity
- Bottom: `biomate.ai` — small, white, 65% opacity

### Notes for designer
- Replace the logo text block with the actual PNG logo asset
- The envelope and globe icons should use a consistent icon set (e.g. Feather or Heroicons)
- Consider foil or emboss on the "BioMate AI" text on the back for premium feel

## 2. Flyer

**Size:** A4 (210 × 297mm) or US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) · **Use:** Conference handout, lab bulletin boards, event tables

### Structure (top to bottom)

**Header band** — full-width navy (`#14283a`), ~35% of page height
- Large BioMate AI wordmark (Crimson Text, white, ~34pt)
- Tagline: *"AI Platform for Biomedical Research"* (Open Sans, white, 80% opacity)
- Subtle decorative circles (white, low opacity) in top-right and bottom-left corners
- Thin accent stripe at bottom of header band (`#2a5582`)

**Hero statement**
- *"Describe your experiment. Get your results."* — bold, deep navy, 15pt
- *"No code. No installation. No infrastructure headaches."* — muted, 11pt

**4-feature grid** (2×2 cards, white fill, cream border)
Each card has a light navy tinted header bar:
1. **34 Domains** — Transcriptomics · Genomics · Drug Discovery · Cryo-EM · Proteomics · and 29 more
2. **2,455+ Workflows** — Validated analysis pipelines tied to published research
3. **Auto-loop QC** — Evidence-graded quality control; automatically flags and remediates issues
4. **Cloud GPU Compute** — AWS infrastructure on demand. Zero setup, zero maintenance.

**Audience line** — *"Built for researchers, biotech teams, and pharma organizations"* italic, muted

**CTA button** — full-width navy rounded rectangle: *"Try free — no credit card required"*

**URL block**
- `biomate.ai` — large, accent navy, bold
- `dev-public.biomate.ai/register` — smaller, muted
- QR code (generate to point to `dev-public.biomate.ai/register`)

**Footer band** — full-width navy
- `contact@biomate.ai · biomate.ai · Houston, TX`
- `© 2026 BioMate AI LLC`

### Notes for designer
- Replace `[ QR Code ]` placeholder with an actual QR code
- Replace logo text placeholder in header with actual logo PNG
- For print: ensure all navy areas are CMYK-converted; check cream `#f6efde` renders correctly
- A4 bleed: add 3mm bleed on all edges

## 3. T-Shirt

**Recommended fabric:** 100% cotton or 50/50 blend · **Print method:** Screen print or DTG
**Color options:** Navy shirt (front design) + Cream/natural shirt (back design) as a matched pair, or single colorway

### Front — Navy shirt
**Placement:** Chest center, ~25cm wide

Design elements:
- **BioMate AI** — wordmark in white, Crimson Text Bold, large (~22pt equivalent at print scale)
- Below: *"AI Platform for Biomedical Research"* — Open Sans Regular, white, 75% opacity, smaller
- Optional: subtle rectangular container (white, 8% opacity) behind the text block

Bottom hem (optional, small): `biomate.ai` in white, 45% opacity

### Back — Cream/natural shirt
**Placement:** Upper back, centered, ~28cm wide

Design elements:
- **BioMate AI** — accent navy (`#2a5582`), Crimson Text Bold, large
- *"AI Platform for Biomedical Research"* — Open Sans, muted navy
- Thin accent line below tagline
- Domain tag pills (6 tags in 2 rows of 3):
  `Transcriptomics` · `Drug Discovery` · `Genomics`
  `Cryo-EM` · `Single Cell` · `Proteomics`
  Each pill: cream fill, border `#d3c7a8`, accent navy text, rounded corners
- *"Zero code. Cloud-native. Science-first."* — italic, muted
- `biomate.ai` — accent navy, bottom

### Alternative colorway
- All-black shirt with cream/cream-tinted text (works well for conferences)
- All-white shirt with full navy print

### Notes for designer
- Provide artwork as vector (AI or SVG) for clean print scaling
- Domain pills need enough padding — minimum 6px internal padding at print scale
- For DTG printing on cream fabric, the cream elements may need a slight off-white rather than pure white to avoid disappearing into the shirt
