# BioMate — Brand Flyer (content + structure spec for Claude design)

**Design brief:** single-page flyer. Tone: clean, scientific, credible — evidence-first, not hypey. Audience: biomedical researchers, life-science R&D, biotech/pharma, clinical/translational teams. Palette: trustworthy blue/teal + white, one accent. BioMate logo top-left.

---

## SECTION 1 — Hero (top)
**Brand name:** **BioMate**
**Tagline (large):** Your AI thinking lab for biomedical research — every answer grounded in scientific evidence.
**One-liner (sub):** BioMate reasons over biomedical databases and the literature, designs and runs the analysis, lets you explore the results interactively, and delivers audited, source-cited reports.

*(Visual: question → evidence/databases + literature → analysis & interactive charts → cited report.)*

---

## SECTION 2 — What it is (short paragraph)
BioMate is an **evidence-grounded AI research environment for biomedicine**. It connects to a large set of biomedical databases, searches the literature, generates analysis protocols, runs the analysis, lets you visualize and re-interrogate the output interactively, applies QC and autonomous research loops, and produces **auditable reports with citations back to the source**.

---

## SECTION 3 — Key features (icon grid, 4 tiles)
1. **Evidence-grounded** — answers backed by biomedical databases and the published literature, with traceable sources.
2. **Autonomous research** — QC-gated auto-research loops that iterate toward a defensible answer.
3. **Interactive by design** — visualize, filter, and re-analyze results in the loop, not just read them.
4. **Audit-ready** — every result and report carries source citations and a full audit trail.

---

## SECTION 4 — What it can do (by research domain, grouped)
**Omics & molecular analysis**
- Bulk & single-cell RNA-seq · differential expression · variant calling & annotation · ChIP/ATAC & epigenomics · proteomics · metabolomics · methylation arrays · spatial.

**Structural & imaging**
- Cryo-EM reconstruction · bio-imaging · flow cytometry.

**Drug discovery & translational**
- ADMET screening · PBPK / PK modeling · clinical-trial dose simulation · target & pathway analysis.

**Regulatory & evidence**
- IND §2.6.1 narrative generation · 21 CFR Part 11-ready audit packages · CRO campaign evidence.
