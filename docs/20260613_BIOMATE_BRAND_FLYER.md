# BioMate — Brand Flyer (content + structure spec for Claude design)

**Design brief:** single-page flyer (front, optional back). Tone: clean, scientific, confident — not hypey. Audience: computational biologists, bench scientists, biotech/pharma R&D, CROs. Palette: trustworthy blue/teal + white, one accent. Use the BioMate logo top-left.

---

## SECTION 1 — Hero (top)
**Brand name:** **BioMate**
**Tagline (large):** From a plain-English question to a real, reproducible result.
**One-liner (sub):** The AI bioinformatics engine — describe your analysis in chat, and BioMate finds the right pipeline, runs it on the cloud, and returns publication-ready outputs.

*(Visual: chat bubble → workflow steps → result chart/report.)*

---

## SECTION 2 — What it is (short paragraph)
BioMate turns natural-language requests into **executed** bioinformatics workflows. No scripting, no pipeline plumbing. It routes your intent to a validated workflow from a curated library, runs it in real Bioconductor/nf-core containers on managed cloud compute, and hands back results, figures, QC, and a methods report you can cite.

---

## SECTION 3 — Key features (icon grid, 6 tiles)
1. **Chat-to-pipeline** — natural language → the correct workflow, auto-selected.
2. **Huge validated library** — 3,300+ Bioconductor workflows (1,800+ packages) + nf-core + drug-discovery pipelines, searchable by intent (semantic + keyword).
3. **Real cloud execution** — runs in genuine R/Bioconductor containers on managed batch compute; no local setup.
4. **Auto-QC + self-correction** — built-in quality gates that flag issues and auto-remediate parameters.
5. **AI findings + methods report** — plain-language interpretation of results plus a citable, reproducible methods write-up.
6. **Step-by-step transparency** — live per-step status, input/output previews, downloadable artifacts.

---

## SECTION 4 — What it can do (services, grouped)
**Omics analysis**
- RNA-seq & differential expression · single-cell (QC → clustering → markers) · variant calling & annotation · ChIP/ATAC & epigenomics · proteomics & metabolomics · methylation arrays · spatial.

**Structural & imaging**
- Cryo-EM reconstruction · bio-imaging & flow cytometry.

**Drug discovery & translational**
- ADMET screening · PBPK / PK modeling · clinical-trial dose simulation · target & pathway analysis.

**Regulatory & CRO**
- IND §2.6.1 narrative generation · 21 CFR Part 11-ready compliance packages · CRO campaign management & exportable evidence.

---

## SECTION 5 — How it works (3 steps, horizontal)
1. **Ask** — "Run differential expression on these counts," paste your data.
2. **BioMate runs it** — picks the validated pipeline, executes on the cloud, QC-checks the output.
3. **Get results** — figures, tables, AI interpretation, and a methods report — reproducible and shareable.

---

## SECTION 6 — Why BioMate (3 short proof points)
- **Validated, not improvised** — workflows are real, input-consuming pipelines run in their native environments — not throwaway snippets.
- **Coverage that matters** — workflows span ~83% of analysis-package download traffic across Bioconductor, including the fastest-growing "rising-star" packages (100% covered).
- **Open foundation** — the underlying Bioconductor knowledge is published openly (BioMate-KB), so the science is inspectable.
