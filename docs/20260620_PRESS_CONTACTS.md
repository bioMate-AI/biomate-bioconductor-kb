you with researcher users, or provide benchmark data. Also available for

## Notes

- **Wikipedia priority:** Bits in Bio is the fastest path — a tool feature or guest post there would constitute the independent secondary source needed to satisfy WP:GNG and unblock the AfC submission.
- **STAT News priority:** Higher credibility for notability but harder to get placed; requires a strong news hook. Best angle: "agentic AI removes the bioinformatics staffing bottleneck" with VirBench/Robin as independent validation of the problem.
- **COI disclosure:** When pitching, always disclose founder role upfront. Do not pitch as a third party.
- **Demo offer:** Always include a demo offer — journalists covering AI tools often want to see it run live before writing.
