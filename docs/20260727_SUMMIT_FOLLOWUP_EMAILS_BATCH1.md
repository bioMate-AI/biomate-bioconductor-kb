## 1. BioMate — what it is, in one paragraph (talking points)
BioMate is a **virtual AI research workforce for translational & biomarker science**: a Bioconductor-native orchestration platform that runs the full multi-omics stack — single-cell (scRNA/CyTOF), multi-omic integration & endotyping (MOFA+), TCR/BCR repertoire (AIRR), spatial, genetics, methylation, proteomics, autoantibody/isotype analysis, and target prioritisation — **from natural language, end-to-end, without the client standing up a bioinformatics team.** Every result is **closed-system, human-in-the-loop, and citable** (no open/generative black box). We sell **capability to drug developers** (trial enrichment, responder/biomarker stratification, target discovery) — not a per-test clinical product.

**Positioning that matters to this audience:**
- **Predictive, not just prognostic** — we frame stratification as a **biomarker × treatment interaction** (the "gate-3" test that actually changes a trial), and report the fraction of decisions that change. This is the language pharma-development buyers respect.
- **Closed & IP-safe** — mirrors what UCB's own team said on stage (closed systems, human governance, no hallucinated biology).
- **Fast & reproducible** — orchestrated, harmonized, re-runnable pipelines vs. bespoke one-off scripts.

**What we can run *today* that's relevant here:** autoantibody-isotype/subclass stratification (built+tested); scRNA-seq & CyTOF immune profiling; multi-omic integration/endotyping; FcRn/complement pathway expression profiling; Open-Targets-method target ranking (MG demo independently surfaced **C5 + FcRn** — the validated MG target classes).

## 2. Why this room, why now (market context)
- MG/CIDP/GBS/MMN is one of the most commercially active corners of autoimmune drug development. argenx's Vyvgart (efgartigimod) alone did **$1.5B in Q2 2026** (+60% YoY); generalized-MG market ~$5.9B (2025) → ~$15.6B (2036).
- Mechanistically it's **FcRn blockade + complement inhibition + B-/plasma-cell depletion** — and every one of those has an open **patient-stratification / non-responder** question. That question is exactly what BioMate sells into.
- BioMate does **not** compete for drug revenue — it competes for the informatics/biomarker budget *inside* these programs. The buyer is the **translational/biomarker team or the CMO**, not HQ BD.
