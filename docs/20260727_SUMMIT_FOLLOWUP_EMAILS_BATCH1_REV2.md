## 0. BioMate — one paragraph (talking points for the call)
BioMate is a **virtual AI research workforce for translational & biomarker science**: a Bioconductor-native orchestration platform that runs the full multi-omics stack — single-cell (scRNA/CyTOF), multi-omic integration & endotyping (MOFA+), TCR/BCR repertoire (AIRR), spatial, genetics, methylation, proteomics, autoantibody/isotype analysis, and target prioritisation — **from natural language, end-to-end, without the client standing up a bioinformatics team.** Every result is **closed-system, human-in-the-loop, and citable** (no open/generative black box). We sell **capability to drug developers** (trial enrichment, responder/biomarker stratification, target discovery) — not a per-test clinical product. The positioning that lands with this room: **predictive, not just prognostic** (biomarker × treatment interaction — the "gate-3" test that changes a trial); **closed & IP-safe** (mirrors UCB's on-stage philosophy); **fast & reproducible**.
