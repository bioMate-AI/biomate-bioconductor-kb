# Dr. Chandra Mohan — ALCAM Target: Landmark Publication + Current Work

*Reference note, 2026-09-11. All facts below traced to the cited sources; nothing reconstructed. Prepared to anchor the pilot's "reproduce the Lab's published primary analysis" step and to brief the team on the Lab's current direction.*

---

## 1 · Who / what target

- **PI:** Chandra Mohan, Hugh Roy & Lillie Cranz Cullen Endowed Professor of Biomedical Engineering, **University of Houston**. A long-standing lupus-nephritis (LN) biomarker group.
- **Target:** **ALCAM** (Activated Leukocyte Cell Adhesion Molecule / **CD166**) in **lupus nephritis**.
- **Status of the target:** established by the Lab as a **urinary biomarker of active LN**, now being advanced toward a **therapeutic target** under an LRA **"Target Identification in Lupus"** grant. Mechanistic rationale ties to the **ALCAM–CD6 axis** (the anti-CD6 antibody itolizumab is the therapeutic arc referenced in our reply doc — an external asset, not the Lab's own molecule).

## 2 · The landmark publication (data + method)

**Stanley S, et al.; Chandra Mohan (corresponding). *Comprehensive aptamer-based screening identifies a spectrum of urinary biomarkers of lupus nephritis across ethnicities.* Nature Communications 2020; 11:2197.**
DOI **10.1038/s41467-020-15986-3** · open full text **PMC7198599** · (Author Correction: *Nat Commun* 2020, s41467-020-16944-9 / PMC7297716).

### Method — two-stage, ethnicity-stratified
- **Discovery:** **SOMAscan** aptamer-based targeted proteomics, screening **1,129 distinct human proteins** directly in **urine**.
- **Validation:** **ELISA** on independent cohorts, panels **stratified by ethnicity** (the paper's central design point — the best-discriminating panel differs by group).

### Data — cohorts
| Stage | N | Composition | Population |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | 23 | 7 active LN / 8 inactive SLE / 8 HC | — |
| Validation 1 (primary) | 95 | 27 active LN / 47 inactive SLE / 21 HC | African-American + Caucasian |
| Validation 2 (secondary) | 280 | 80 active LN / 80 inactive SLE / 67 active non-renal lupus / 53 HC | Asian |

*(The abstract pools these into slightly different per-group totals — 127 inactive / 107 active LN / 67 non-renal / 74 HC — depending on aggregation; the staged breakdown is from the article body.)*

### Ethnicity-specific panels (active LN vs inactive)
- **African-American:** ALCAM, PF-4, properdin, VCAM-1
- **Caucasian:** sE-selectin, VCAM-1, BFL-1, Hemopexin
- **Asian:** ALCAM, VCAM-1, TFPI, PF-4

### ALCAM performance
- **AUC 0.84** (African-American); **AUC 0.93** (Asian; sensitivity 0.90 / specificity 0.91) for active vs inactive LN.
- Correlated with **PGA, SLEDAI, renal-SLEDAI, proteinuria**; **distinguished active LN from active non-renal lupus** (renal specificity). Concurrent biopsy/histology correlation was limited in this paper.

> Honesty flag: in the 2020 paper ALCAM is a **biomarker**; the **pathogenic/target** role is developed in the LRA grant + follow-up work below — "drug target" is the program direction, not a claim this one paper closes.

## 3 · What the Lab is doing now (recent direction)

1. **Biomarker → drug target.** LRA **Target Identification in Lupus** grant to advance **ALCAM as a therapeutic target** for LN treatment — moving beyond diagnostics.
2. **Non-invasive / point-of-care diagnostics.**
   - **Analytically validated urine ALCAM ELISA** as an LN test — *Expert Rev Mol Diagn* 2023; 23(8) (DOI 10.1080/14737159.2023.2236022).
   - **Home-monitoring device** that tracks "the pathogenic urine biomarker ALCAM" — *Front Immunol* 2022; 13:1044743 (PMC9780296), with a **2025 correction/update** (PMC12679908); collaborators include **Johns Hopkins** School of Medicine BME. Framing ALCAM as *pathogenic*, not merely correlative.
3. **Expanded / independently validated urine panels.** 2024 work (reported via UH; *Journal of Autoimmunity*) independently validated and extended the active-renal-lupus panel — **ALCAM, CD163, MCP1, SELL, ICAM1, VCAM1, NGAL, TWEAK**; urine ALCAM/KIM-1/PF4/VCAM-1 elevated in active LN vs active non-renal SLE, inactive SLE and HC.
4. **Adjacent cohorts.** Prior extensions into **pediatric LN** and **lupus-related cardiovascular** biomarkers (UH, 2022).
5. **Method continuity for us:** aptamer (**SOMAscan**) discovery → **ELISA** validation → **home/point-of-care** device, on **cross-ethnic** cohorts. This is exactly the "published primary analysis" the pilot proposes to **reproduce for parity** before layering causal-confidence + responder-stratification on the Lab's own cohorts.
