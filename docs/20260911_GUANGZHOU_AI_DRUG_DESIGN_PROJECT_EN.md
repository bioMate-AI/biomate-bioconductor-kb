## Requirement background and description

### Context

The enterprise has a forward-looking exploratory need in innovative-drug R&D. Through the platform's formal call for use cases, it invites service providers with AI drug-design and deep-learning capabilities to accelerate lead-compound discovery and molecular optimization jointly.

### Business overview

The project focuses on the early discovery stage of drug R&D. It aims to use deep-learning models to systematically learn the complex relationships between molecular structure and biological activity, build intelligent capabilities for drug-molecule generation and optimization, and efficiently screen chemical space for candidate compounds with potential.

### Core need

Using AI molecular-generation models, rapidly generate candidate compounds with chemically reasonable structures and potential activity. Optimize candidates comprehensively across three core dimensions:

1. Synthetic accessibility
2. Target affinity
3. Safety

The purpose is to improve the overall quality of lead compounds and create a sound basis for subsequent preclinical research.

### Desired exploration direction

Build an integrated AI drug-design workflow covering molecular generation, activity prediction, property optimization, and candidate ranking. The goal is to substantially accelerate the traditionally long and expensive molecular-screening step, shifting discovery from empirical trial-and-error toward intelligent, precise design.
