## Cross-cutting requirements (read first)

For a for-profit buyer using **de-identified** human specimens/data, the standard stack is:

1. **IRB / human-subjects determination** — De-identified samples/data almost always qualify as
   **"Not Human Subjects Research" (NHSR)** at the buyer's IRB, so BioMate typically needs an
   NHSR determination letter rather than full IRB review. Exceptions: prospective/custom
   collection (the *vendor's* IRB covers consent), re-contact studies, and any request that
   returns a HIPAA Limited Data Set (e.g. **IBD Plexus biosamples require IRB approval before
   release**).
2. **Agreement type** — Commercial specimens: **purchase order** (catalog) or **master supply /
   custom-collection agreement + MTA** (prospective). Biobanks: **DUA** (data) and/or **MTA**
   (physical samples); dbGaP adds a **Data Use Certification** with an institutional Signing
   Official.
3. **Commercial-use / consent** — Confirm the donor consent explicitly permits **commercial
   research use** per lot. Most vendors are RUO (research-use-only); a few (notably **DLS**)
   require a **separate commercial license**. BioIVT and Sanguine have the most explicit
   commercial-use-permitted consent language.

**Cost reality:** No commercial vendor publishes per-sample list prices — all are quote-required.
Consortium "modest cost" resources (USIDNET, ACP) and cost-recovery repositories (NIDDK) are far
cheaper per sample than commercial vendors, but slower and narrower.

stratification; fresh-PBMC logistics/lead time; per-sample price; explicit for-profit consent scope.

## Recommended fastest route for a for-profit (no collaborator)

**Single fastest no-collaborator route → NIH All of Us Researcher Workbench.** For-profit
companies already hold DURAs (Amgen, BMS, 23andMe), the Workbench is **free** to access (pay only
pay-as-you-go cloud beyond initial credits), it needs **no academic collaborator and no biosample
MTA**, the central IRB covers de-identified use, and it delivers **SLE + IBD (and all immune
diseases) as EHR phenotypes plus WGS** in **days-to-weeks** once the institutional DURA is signed.
Limitation: **data only, no physical samples**.

**If physical samples are required, fastest paths by need:**
- **Fresh functional PBMC / longitudinal, SLE + LN + UC + Crohn's:** **Sanguine Biosciences** (only
  vendor confirming all four incl. LN; best fresh/same-day + EMR-longitudinal), or **Boca
  Biolistics** / **Precision for Medicine**. Purchase-order/MTA, weeks; quote required.
- **Quick retrospective serum/plasma:** **BioIVT eCommerce portal** or **Central BioHub / iSpecimen
  marketplace** — in-stock in days; quote required.
- **Cheapest large IBD genetics samples:** **NIDDK IBDGC (Study 26)** — published per-specimen fees
  ($2.92–$24.81) via DUA+MTA; direct industry eligibility CNV (confirm).
- **Deep longitudinal IBD data+biosamples:** **IBD Plexus industry membership/CDA** (request pricing;
  IRB required for biosamples).
- **Population-scale multi-omic data (incl. Olink/WGS) + optional samples:** **UK Biobank** Tier 3
  £9,000/3yr — but **confirm intake is open** (possible pause until late 2026).
