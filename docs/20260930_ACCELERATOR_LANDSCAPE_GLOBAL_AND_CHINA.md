## E2 · China non-dilutive grants + entity structure (the decision-relevant lever)

The **biggest China grants split by who can apply** — this shapes how we structure the Chinese entity:
- **Entity-based (a foreign-founded China entity / WFOE can access directly):** **高企/HNTE** status → **15% CIT + 100% R&D super-deduction** (caveat: **core IP must be domiciled in the China entity**); most **local talent-entrepreneurship** packages (Suzhou 姑苏, Guangdong 珠江); Torch/Qiming enterprise-applicant tracks.
- **Host-institution-based (need a university PI / academic host):** **NSFC** competitive grants; **lead roles on 科技部 National Key R&D (国家重点研发计划)** 重点专项 — e.g. the **¥1.31B 2025 innovative-drug mega-project** — where a company realistically enters **as a partner in a university-led consortium**.
- **Foreign-scientist route:** NSFC **外国学者研究基金** (¥0.2M/0.4M/0.8M per yr, 3 tiers; 9-months-on-site rule).
- **National platforms that de-risk the wet half:** **国家生物药技术创新中心** (Suzhou; China's first national biopharma tech-innovation center — shared nucleic-acid/cell preclinical platforms).
- **AI-for-Science 专项** is a policy tailwind flowing through NKP/NSFC, not a standalone fund.

**Structural implication:** a pure foreign entity gets tax + local talent money; the **Soochow/SYSU academic-spinout route adds NSFC + National-Key-R&D lead access on top.** Sequence accordingly (entity for tax/talent + a university-host consortium for the mega-grants). *(All figures ⚠️ — confirm against primary sources.)*
