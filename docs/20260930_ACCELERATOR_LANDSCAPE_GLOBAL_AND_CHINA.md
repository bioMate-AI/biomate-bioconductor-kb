## 1b · AION Labs (Israel) — the strategic special case
Not a normal "apply" accelerator — a **venture-creation studio** (AstraZeneca / Merck / Pfizer / Teva + Israel Biotech Fund + AWS) that co-founds **one new Israeli NewCo per challenge**. You **cannot** enroll your existing entity. Terms per challenge: ≥$1M over 2yr + $100K AWS credits + facilities; founders take equity in the NewCo.
- **Why it matters:** its **"Generative-AI for Novel Target Combinations"** challenge (closed May 2025) explicitly sought *ranked target-pair lists for multi-specific biologics* including **immune-mediated disease** — **our exact wedge**, validated as a pharma-priority problem.
- **VERDICT: EMULATE + selectively PARTNER — do NOT compete / do NOT join.** Cite it as external de-risking of our thesis; engage the **member pharma (AZ/Merck/Pfizer) directly** (via MDSS / A.Catalyst) where we keep our IP; don't spin a separate Israeli NewCo. Monitor BioMed X + aionlabs.com for the next on-topic challenge (2–3/yr; ⚠️ none open on-topic as of 2026-09-30).
