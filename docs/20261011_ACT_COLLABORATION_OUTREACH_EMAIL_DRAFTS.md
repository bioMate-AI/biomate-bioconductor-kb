  plan* — never our stack, pipeline stages, algorithms, data sources, or "model in development."
- **No over-claim.** The lead αvβ6 candidates are described honestly as **designed and in synthesis /
  binding characterization — not yet confirmed binders.** No measured affinity/selectivity is asserted.
- **Warm/cold preserved exactly** from the survey; no cold→warm upgrade. No invented relationships.
- **Formal academic register;** ~4 short paragraphs each; intro → clinical white-space → their specific
  asset/data → one simple ask (a 20–30 min call).
- **Email addresses are NOT fabricated.** Where the survey did not carry a verified personal address, the
  `To:` line says `[confirm address]`. **Re-confirm every address before sending.**
- **IP boundaries** flagged where a collaboration crosses one (peptide/macrocycle co-design; competitor).
- Signature per the standing convention: *Yaoyun Zhang — Founder & CEO, BioMate AI · Houston, TX*
  (the "former UTHealth SBMI faculty" line is kept out of the signature; a light Houston/TMC nod in the body
  is fine, and for Dr. Zhao the former-department tie is the warm opener).
