# Moderna design & tone — deep study, and a re-review of biomate.ai

**Date:** 2026-10-16 · Per feedback that our hero is "still too technical at the top level" and that technical
detail "should be elaborated in separate pages with diagrams and numbers." Studied Moderna page-by-page (home,
pipeline, about; science pages 404'd but the homepage states the layering rule explicitly). Notes, then re-review.

---

## Part 1 — Moderna's design & tone (interpretation)

**The one principle: strategic RESTRAINT.** The top level leads with *mission and humanity, not technology.*
Earn trust through clarity and purpose, not density.

1. **Hero = mission, not mechanism.** "Pioneering mRNA technology" + *"deliver the greatest possible impact to
   people through mRNA medicines."* Zero jargon — no "lipid nanoparticles," no "nucleotide." The **what** (medicines)
   and the **why** (impact on people), never the **how**.
2. **Voice:** *purposeful, audacious, rigorous, inclusive, patient-centric.* "Aspirational scientism" — confident
   enough to simplify, human enough to center impact. Owns the category by **creation, not boasting**: *"We are
   creating a new category of medicines."* Earnest, not cocky.
3. **Ruthless layering.** ALL mechanism is deferred behind CTAs. "So, what is mRNA?" → a **one-sentence** layperson
   answer ("carries a message … instructions that direct your cells"). Depth lives on inner pages.
4. **Proof = scale, not mechanism.** "13+ years," "45 development programs," "36 ongoing trials." Concrete scale.
   **No** efficacy %, **no** mechanism, **no** feature claims at the top.
5. **Pipeline page = clinical & factual.** Regulatory taxonomy (phases, NSCLC), **one row per program**,
   categorization over storytelling, "as of <date>." Reads as a biotech because it embraces regulatory language,
   not feature-speak.
6. **Visuals:** human/lifestyle photography at the top (scientists, people) — **not** molecule diagrams. Diagrams
   and data belong on the deep science pages.
7. **What they deliberately DON'T put at the top:** mechanism/nomenclature, competitive positioning, pricing, fear.

## Part 2 — Re-review of biomate.ai against this

Our site inverts Moderna's order: we lead with the **how** (the engine), in **feature-speak**.

| Moderna | biomate.ai (current, incl. my last edit) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Hero = mission + people | Hero sub = *"closed loop of generative design, honest multi-constraint verification, and experimental feedback … AI and automation beneath"* | **Too technical.** This is a "how it works" paragraph, not a hero. |
| Mechanism deferred to inner pages | The two sections I added (**"hard gates," "many rounds"**) sit on the homepage | **Wrong altitude.** These belong on a deep *How we design* page with diagrams + numbers. |
| Proof = scale (programs, years) | Proof = **BixBench 92.7%, routing 90.8%** — AI-capability benchmarks | **Feature-speak.** Reads as a tool, not a biotech. Biotech proof = data scale, targets, validated science. |
| Human/aspirational voice | Technical/product voice ("workbench," "platform," "verification") | **Missing the human register.** |
| Leads with WHAT/WHY | Leads with HOW (AI, loop, engine) | **Inverted.** |

**Bottom line:** the repositioning's *structure* is right (realm + therapeutic areas), but the **altitude and
tone are still a tool vendor's.** The hero and homepage must drop the mechanism vocabulary; the rigor I wrote
(selectivity, FTO, multi-constraint verification, the loop) is good content — **in the wrong place.**

## Part 3 — Direction (the revision)

1. **Hero → mission-led, human, zero jargon.** Name the realm + the human stakes; defer the engine. Drafts:
   - **A.** "New medicines for when the immune system turns against the body." · *sub:* "We focus on the diseases
     where the immune system attacks the body, fails to clear infection, or is evaded by cancer — and design the
     medicines to set it right."
   - **B.** "A new generation of medicines for immune-mediated disease." · *sub (human):* "Millions live with
     diseases driven by an immune system gone wrong. We design the medicines to change that."
   - **C.** "Medicines designed for the immune system." · *sub:* "Autoimmune, chronic infection, and cancer —
     three failures of immunity. One focused mission."
   > None mention AI, loops, verification, or "molecules." Those are the **how**, moved down.
2. **Create a deep "How we design" / "Our Science" page** — the home for the technical detail, **with diagrams and
   numbers**: the design→make→test→learn loop (as a diagram), the hard gates (selectivity / developability / FTO /
   mechanism-correct physics / multi-objective), multi-round optimization, wet-lab validation. **Move the two
   rigor sections I put on the homepage here.** This is where "honest multi-constraint verification" belongs.
3. **Simplify the homepage flow** (Moderna cadence): hero (mission) → the three therapeutic areas (simple, human) →
   a ONE-LINE "our approach" + a link to the science page → credibility (scale) → a human/mission close.
4. **Reframe proof as biotech scale** (not AI benchmarks on the homepage): e.g. *136,730 real binding measurements ·
   16 immune targets mapped · a validated multi-constraint design loop.* Keep BixBench/routing on the Evidence/
   platform page, where feature metrics belong.
5. **Visuals:** lean human/aspirational at the top; put diagrams (the loop, the gates) on the science page.

**Honesty holds throughout:** pre-clinical stage stated; "designed to set it right" is mission language, not an
efficacy claim; scale numbers are ground-truth.
