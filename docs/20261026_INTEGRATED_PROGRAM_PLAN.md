# Integrated program plan — one calibrated verification system, built from what we already have (2026-10-26)

**Why this document exists.** The user's critique (2026-10-26): the program keeps restarting with small standalone studies instead of reusing and improving what exists, and restarts do not produce a deep contribution for a high-end journal. Correct. Much of this session retired wrong claims (necessary) but did not harvest what survived. This plan fixes that: ONE system, ONE claim, ONE evaluation, every existing asset assigned a role.

## 1. The claim (proposed — NOT yet validated)
A generator proposes cyclic-peptide candidates; a **ladder of verification tiers** checks each one; every tier is **calibrated against measured data** and **abstains when out of its domain**; the loop learns only from **verified** rewards. Claim to test: *the ladder yields shortlists with higher, calibrated precision at a stated abstention rate than the standard confidence-only pipeline, on held-out documents and receptors.*
**REVISED after the prior-art check (`docs/20261026_PRIOR_ART_INTEGRATED_CLAIM.md`): the architectural claim is NOT novel** — multi-fidelity oracles (MFBind, MF-LAL), verifiable-reward molecular generation (LLMol), LLM peptide agents (Pepti-Agent, PepThink-R1) and integrated cyclic-binder pipelines (UnibindMD, posted 2026-10-23) all exist. The contribution must be EVIDENTIAL: measured, held-out reliability of each tier for cyclic peptides (reliability diagrams, coverage–precision/abstention), plus the physics/data we uniquely have (MIDAS-metal integrin FEP, 542 αvβ6 compounds / 35 documents). Novelty of those pieces remains UNVERIFIED (abstract-level reading only). Closest known pieces: sample selection/reranking for co-folding, AfCycDesign/CyclicBoltz1 (ring closure inside the model), multi-fidelity BO. "LLM + rules + computation" alone is not novel (SynCraft).

## 3. Evaluation (all retrospective, pre-registered, cluster/document-held-out)
- **E1 structure tier:** Stage B one-shot (running). Claim rule already fixed (A2/A3 in `PREREG.md`).
- **E2 affinity tier:** leave-document-out on the 35 αvβ6 documents: does the calibrated tier rank actives and say "unsure" correctly? Metrics: precision/enrichment at fixed coverage, calibration error, abstention curve. Nulls: per-document mean, promiscuity, 1-NN (already implemented in `integrin_chembl`).
- **E3 developability tiers:** applicability-domain checks on cyclic/ncAA peptides; permeability on source-held-out splits.
- **E4 loop:** DPO on the integrated verified reward vs matched best-of-N and shuffled-reward placebo, scored by an INDEPENDENT oracle.
- **E5 end-to-end:** shortlist precision vs abstention for {confidence-only pipeline, + T1, + T1–T3, full ladder}. This is the headline figure.

## 6. What gets written, by outcome (decided now, not after seeing results)
- **E1 positive AND E5 positive:** one method + evidence paper: "calibrated, abstaining verification for cyclic-peptide design" (structure tier + affinity tier + ladder), with the measured reliability maps as the core result.
- **E1 negative, E2/E5 positive:** same paper without the selector claim; the finding that co-folding confidence ≈ random for cyclic complexes is reported as a measured limitation that motivates independent tiers.
- **E5 not better than confidence-only:** no method paper. A short evidence report only if the reliability measurements are themselves new after W7; otherwise stop.
- **Preprint v1 / v2 split:** v1 = W1, W2, W3, W5, W6/E5 (retrospective, held-out) + the 1 ns metal structural screen as a stated limitation. v2 = wet-lab measurements of a pre-registered shortlist (W8), which also provides the anchors to calibrate FEP (replacing the expensive retrospective FEP campaign).
- Never written: any αvβ6 binding/FEP claim before W4's gate; availability as a design objective; "best method" without the pre-registered rule.

## 9. W8 — pre-registered wet-lab predictions (written into v1 so v2 can falsify it)
Goal: v1 ends with a dated, hashed `predictions.json` for the v2 experiment, so the later wet-lab result is a genuine test. Content: the shortlist the full ladder emits at its stated abstention rate; per-candidate predicted αvβ6 rank/IC50 band with the tier uncertainties; predicted selectivity direction vs αvβ3; the specific falsification criteria (e.g. rank correlation with the measured plate below a pre-set threshold, or precision at the stated coverage below the retrospective value minus its CI). Experiment design principles (committee G): same ELISA format as the Kapp/Quigley anchors so values are directly comparable; include deliberately predicted-weak compounds and known controls (FF, YY, A20FMDV2); an αvβ3 counter-screen on the same plate; buffer composition matched to the anchors (1 mM Ca/Mg/Mn per Kapp 2017). Cost/CRO details NOT estimated here (unverified). Gate: the shortlist is generated only after W1–W3 and W5 are final, and each candidate must pass the hard gates (RGD pharmacophore, single chelator handle H5, FTO flag with `patents_checked` stated).
