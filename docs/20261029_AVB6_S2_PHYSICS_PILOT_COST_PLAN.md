# S2 — αvβ6 physics-chain pilot: cost-bounded plan (NOT EXECUTED; awaiting owner approval) — 2026-10-29
Part of `docs/20261028_AVB6_RETROSPECTIVE_RECOVERY_PREREG.md` (A3 §11.4). Nothing in this document has been run.

## 1. Question and why only αvβ6
Does the chained physics tier (Boltz-2 co-fold with the three β6 Mn2+ retained → pose gate → MM-GBSA ΔG) rank known αvβ6 binders above property-matched cross-target decoys? αvβ6 is the only target with a prepared receptor + metal configuration (`target_kb/avb6.json`: `9xmm`, Mn ×3 at MIDAS/ADMIDAS/SyMBS; `scorer_config.config_for('avb6')` marks metal-free runs mechanism-non-compliant and their ΔG void). One target, 10 positives: this is a pilot that tests the tier's discrimination, not a field-level claim.

## 2. Frozen subset (before any scoring)
`retro_recovery/data/s2_pilot_subset.csv`, sha256 `f2189a2b30d093602ac19f184a25bbe72ad5359bcaf069cb55356c69c86c4522`: target P18564 (ITGB6), 10 positives drawn uniformly (seed 20261030) from the flat-distinct positives with ≤ 120 heavy atoms, each with 5 of its matched decoys from analysis M (50) = **60 compounds** (heavy atoms median 66.5, max 78). The KB active avebehexin is excluded from the 60 and used only as a **calibration control** (with the Trivehexin monomer): their known poses (9xmm-derived) must be reproduced (DockQ against the reference pose ≥ 0.23 and Asp–Mn distance ≤ 2.6 Å, the KN-2 engagement criterion) before any of the 60 is interpreted. M decoys are cross-target binders (so B3/B4 memorisation shortcuts are irrelevant to a physics score, but the positive/decoy contrast is "αvβ6 binder vs binder of other targets").

## 3. Pipeline and BioMate components
`cyclic_llm/smiles_cofold_adapter.py` (SMILES → Boltz-2 input with Mn ×3; untested at this scale) → `physics_batch.py` (AWS Batch, queue `biomate-gpu-spot-queue`, spot only) → `execution_grader.py` contract (`complex.cif`, `mmgbsa_result.json{dG_bind_kcal_mol,target}`) → `score.py` (ΔG as the expensive tier). Component status to be reported per compound: ran / failed / skipped, with failure causes. Read-out: AUROC and EF at top 17 % (10 of 60) of −ΔG, bootstrap over compounds (not over families: n = 1 family), versus the strongest cheap baseline on the same 60 (B1_rgd, B3, size).

## 4. Cost bound (all prices ASSUMED, not measured)
Measured Boltz-2 timings on the GB10 (`20261026_INTEGRATED_PROGRAM_PLAN`): 3.5 min (≈250 tokens), 18 min (621), 43 min (1,069). The αvβ6 assembly (α + β head-domains + 66-atom peptide) is expected in the ≈ 1,000-token class — **to be measured, not assumed, on the calibration fold**. Estimates: co-fold 60 × ~45 min ≈ 45 GPU-h; + 2 calibration controls ≈ 1.5 GPU-h; MM-GBSA per pose ≈ 0.25 GPU-h × 60 = 15 GPU-h (assumed; the avb6 MM-GBSA pipeline exists, `MMGBSA_RESULTS.md`). Total ≈ 60 GPU-h. At an assumed spot 0.4–1.0 USD/GPU-h: **≈ 24–60 USD; hard cap 80 USD** (includes retries).
## 5. Gates and aborts (pre-registered)
(1) Run **one** control fold first; record wall-clock, peak memory, tokens; recompute the estimate; stop if the projected total > 80 USD or per-fold > 90 min. (2) Calibration controls must pass the engagement check or the run stops (a metal-incompliant or pose-failing pipeline yields void ΔG). (3) Per-job instance-state monitor + per-task checkpointing (lessons from the 2026-10-27 spot reclaim); retries ≤ 3. (4) Tag every instance `project=cyclic-selector`; terminate on completion; no always-on resources. (5) A null here is a diagnosis task (§8): audit inputs (Mn retained in every input yaml, ligand charge/protonation, SMILES→CCD mapping for ncAAs) before any retuning; a changed pipeline is a new registered version.
## 6. What would be learned / not learned
Learned: whether the physics tier separates αvβ6 binders from matched decoys on this panel, and its failure modes. Not learned: generality (1 target), selectivity, residence. Decision requested from the owner: approve (cap 80 USD), approve a smaller panel, or defer.
