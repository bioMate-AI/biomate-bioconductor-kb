## Scientific wording updated

The abstract now reports the held-out-source ranking result alongside acquisition yields. Regime L evaluates ranking on held-out literature sources; acquisition occurs in training sources. Literature sources proxy laboratories. Positive framing now explains how these retrospective analyses inform prospective validation without relabelling them as prospective experiments or independently identified cross-laboratory trials. The positive frozen-model contrast is accompanied by the unresolved shuffled-feedback contrast.
