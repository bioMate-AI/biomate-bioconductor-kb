# Review of the 32 proposed knowledge priors — 2026-10-29

This is an AI evidence-and-mapping review, **not human adjudication, biological validation, or an executable eligibility manifest**. All 32 entries marked `weight_eligible` in `knowledge/knowledge_kb_v1.json` were read with their stored quotes, feature mappings, notes and group-A direction-check statuses. Public full-text contexts were opened where described below. No KB entries, eligibility counts, pin files or published artifacts were changed.

## Decision and scope

For the **current unconditional, monotone token potential**, the review recommends **0 allowed, 10 provisional, 22 excluded**. Provisional means that a real, scoped observation can motivate a specified hypothesis or conditional instruction, but the current feature mapping or generalization is not established. Excluded means unsuitable for this particular potential; it does not mean the source is false or useless for retrieval.

The 10 provisional entries are `LIT-HBD-002`, `LIT-IMHB-001`, `LIT-IMHB-005`, `LIT-IMHB-006`, `LIT-NH-002`, `LIT-NME-001`, `LIT-NME-003`, `LIT-NME-004`, `LIT-NME-005`, and `LIT-NMEPOS-001`.

The older 14-rule selection should not be restored by relabelling all quote-verified records eligible. Agreement in two train-source descriptor associations does not validate a causal rule, fix an unsupported feature mapping, or qualify a rule for other assays. No held-out outcomes were used to select these decisions.

Three distinctions drive the result:

1. **Exposure versus count:** burying an NH group through intramolecular interactions can improve permeability while leaving the molecule's total HBD count unchanged. It does not establish a monotone penalty on all HBDs, still less all N/O tokens.
2. **Position versus count:** N-methylation can help at one position and hurt at another. The primary contexts explicitly contain such counterexamples. A positive global N-methyl count reward discards this information.
3. **Endpoint and domain:** oral dosing, solubility, protease stability, linear-lipopeptide half-life and cyclic-peptide passive permeability are different outcomes. A literature window is not an unbounded monotone gradient.

## A defensible method study that can proceed without inventing approval

A **proposed**, not yet registered or launched, minimal ablation can use `LIT-HBD-002` alone as motivation for a negative-HBD hypothesis. Keep the hard-rule baseline; compare a fixed weak intervention with zero weight; freeze magnitude, budget, seeds and endpoints before execution. Prefer an actual completed-molecule HBD descriptor. If the existing polar-token proxy is used, label it explicitly as an unvalidated N/O-token heuristic and report measured HBD/TPSA shifts alongside validity and outcome metrics. It must not be represented as an audited literature law or final L1.

For a contextual knowledge arm, the 10 provisional entries can inform scaffold-specific or exposure-aware instructions after their conditions are carried into the prompt. Collapse same-paper duplicates: IMHB-005/006 are one evidence family; HBD-002/NME-004 share a paper; IMHB-004/NME-003 share a paper, though the former is excluded as a signed rule. Do not infer independence from entry count. Keep the global N-methyl reward off; interventions require site/context eligibility and binding tradeoffs.

These proposed arms ask whether an explicitly limited heuristic helps generation. They do not predeclare biological effectiveness. No audit/pin file should be generated from this table automatically, no full-scale extraction is approved, and public release remains paused.
