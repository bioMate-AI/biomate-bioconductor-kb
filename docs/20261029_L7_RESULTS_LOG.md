## 6. Diagnostic (pre-registered in PREREG_LOOP.md L7m, commit 5ff3e7ae8): why does random feedback rank held-out sources better than the greedy loop for the physchem learner?
Status 2026-10-30: **mechanism not identified yet.** Scope: physchem-descriptor GBM and plain GBM, PAMPA permeability, development seeds 0–9, full-pool arms `loop` and `random_fb`. (Plain-GBM regimes A and B have no full-pool arms in the existing logs; only regime L does.) Files: `results/diag_loop_vs_random.json`, `results/diag_label_range_refit.json`; scripts `diag_loop_vs_random.py`, `diag_label_range_refit.py`.
**H-art (evaluation artefact) — not supported:** 160/160 assertions pass (E identical across arms and aligned with `E_scores_final`, same init, equal-sized revealed sets, revealed sets disjoint from E).
**H-div (diversity) — partly consistent, not decided.** Physchem learner, paired loop − random over 10 seeds (mean, 95 % CI of the mean): scaffold entropy of revealed rows A −1.04 [−1.12, −0.96], B −0.29 [−0.36, −0.24], L −0.59 [−0.70, −0.50]; source entropy A −0.363 [−0.444, −0.289], L −0.248 [−0.342, −0.163] but **B −0.023 [−0.080, 0.038] (no difference)**; distinct sources fewer in all (A −7.6, B −5.1, L −6.3); largest-source share higher in A (+0.136) and L (+0.128), not B. Coverage of E chemistry (mean max cosine to the revealed set) differs by only −0.003 to −0.008. The B gap (−0.132, largest) occurs where source entropy is equal, so source concentration alone cannot explain it; scaffold concentration could. The decisive test is the diversity-matched random draw (`random_srcmatch`, running on CPU spot, instance i-07d23f1e6c9401f47): until it returns, no diversity claim.
**H-range (label range restriction) — not supported by the pre-registered rule.** The loop's revealed labels are much higher and narrower (mean +0.67 vs +0.005 in A, +0.37 vs −0.05 in B, +0.78 vs 0.00 in L; SD 0.85/0.76/0.87 vs 1.00/0.99/1.02; top-decile share 0.30/0.09/0.38 vs 0.09/0.06/0.13). But refitting the physchem GBM on the loop's rows re-weighted to the random arm's label histogram recovers only 21 % (A; weighted − loop +0.036 [0.018, 0.053]), 12 % (B; +0.011 [−0.015, 0.033]) and −7 % (L) of the refit gap (random − loop E source-rho +0.168, +0.103, +0.076), below the 50 % threshold; the weights keep 52–76 % effective sample size, so the test is not starved. Selection bias in labels is real but does not account for most of the gap.
**Per-source view:** the gap is concentrated in a few sources (largest negative per-source differences: 2013_CHUGAI, 2016_Furukawa, 2024_Faris, 2020_Le Roux, 2020_Townsend); a few sources move the other way (2021_Comeau +0.73 in B, 2022_Bhardwaj +0.06 to +0.09), so the effect is source-specific.
**For T2 (provenance-aware acquisition), as information only:** loop-selected training data are less scaffold- and source-diverse and label-shifted, and the physchem learner trained on them ranks held-out sources worse than a random draw; whether diversity control (T2's caps) would remove the gap is exactly what `random_srcmatch` and T2's own pre-registered arms will test; nothing here is a result about T2.
