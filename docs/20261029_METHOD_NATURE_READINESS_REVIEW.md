# Method manuscript: Nature readiness review

Reviewed 29 October 2026. This is an editorial and evidence-readiness assessment, not independent peer review or a certification of all numerical results and references.

## Verdict

Not ready for submission as a completed original-research method article. The draft is a method specification with measured baseline, component and simulation results. The central integrated-method comparison, knowledge intervention and major ablations are unmeasured. Writing improvements cannot supply that evidence. Nature acceptance or journal fit cannot be inferred from compliance with formatting guidance.

## Priority scientific issues

1. **Central claim:** establish real-model integration, masked-training diagnostics and matched budget comparisons before claiming a method advantage. Tables 8–12 and the main method figure have no verified results. The scoped mined catalogue and knowledge-on/off comparison remain unmeasured.
2. **Independent evaluation:** preserve the failed F1 floor and VOID replacement. The existing registered two-family gate cannot establish GO. F2 is an independent fitted-model comparison, not measured biological improvement. A different future study would require a prospectively declared evaluation; do not redefine the current gate after its result.
3. **Metric mismatch:** the protocol records train SD-AUC and final top-10 held-out scores, not held-out trajectories. The abstract, primary hypothesis and margin equations still need a prospectively resolved common estimand. The draft figure description and ablation heading were corrected to final scores; this does not resolve the registered primary-contrast inconsistency. Do not compare a final-score effect to an AUC-derived margin without a justified frozen protocol.
4. **Knowledge validity:** quotation matching does not establish applicable chemical evidence. Qualify scope and source exclusions, freeze the accepted catalogue and require traced interventions with matched knowledge-off and inapplicable-evidence controls. Historical flawed structure annotations are not valid priors.
5. **Novelty and positioning:** distinguish the particular rule compiler and scoped evidence intervention from established constrained decoding, DPO and chemical retrieval. Broad negative claims about existing methods need source-by-source verification. Atom-level SMILES modelling does not mean three-dimensional all-atom generation.
6. **Inference:** retain source-aware limits and no biological validation claim. Three objective families would still be too few for reliable broad family-cluster inference. The proposed linear attenuation by verifier correlation is an assumption, not an empirical calibration.

## Corrections made before author review

- Title shortened; architecture explicitly distinguished from 3D coordinate generation.
- Tuning count corrected to 99 runs per objective family and 198 total.
- Shuffled-control rule stated as the paired difference t-interval containing zero.
- Prospective held-out trajectory/ablation claims corrected to final top-10 scores.
- Linear verifier attenuation identified as a modelling assumption rather than a demonstrated relation.

## Manuscript and submission requirements

The current draft remains long and protocol-heavy, with a separate Related work section and internal stage terminology. Once the study is complete, organise the main narrative around the tested question, measured contribution and implications; move implementation detail and prespecified designs into Methods and supplementary material. Keep this readiness review outside the manuscript. Preserve the temporary study-status marker until missing results are resolved so the draft cannot be mistaken for a completed study.

Authors, affiliations, contributions, funding, competing interests and corresponding-author details are absent. The method requires its own accessible code/data package; the public Paper 1 figure repository is not a method reproducibility release. Corpus manifests, checkpoints, source exclusions, complete hyperparameters, random seeds, software environment and minimum data supporting the method claims need traceable access. Applicable life-sciences reporting documentation must accompany submission. All 47 references and comparative claims have not been independently reverified in this review.
