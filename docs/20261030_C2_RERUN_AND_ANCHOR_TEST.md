## 0. What was found before running (read-only audit of code and logs)
Sources: `research/guangzhou_all_atom/c2_retro_loop.py` (S3 copy identical), `c2_launch.sh`, S3 `cyclic_peptide/c2_loop/` (`c2_staged.npz` identical to `results/c2_staged.npz`, `out/c2_stdout.txt`, `out/bootstrap.log`; no `c2_result.json`, no `DONE` flag).
1. **Design.** Within-target active learning: 5 random seed compounds, batch 5, 12 rounds (65 compounds acquired), pool capped at 600 per target, hit = top 20 % of pAff within the target; metric = recall of those hits after 65 acquisitions; "novel" = hits in Tanimoto-0.5 clusters absent from the seed set. 2. **The logged run is not the current code.** `out/c2_stdout.txt` (2026-10-21 17:14Z, 3,092 s) shows 10 targets and modes learn/fixed/random; the committed v2 (commit 008226f89 / 270543219, 2026-10-22) defaults to all 106 targets and adds UCB/Thompson. The v2 launcher configuration (all targets, 3 seeds) has no recorded result. The documented result is therefore from an earlier script version; whether the earlier version equals the current script restricted to 10 targets is tested in A-repro.
3. **What the logged numbers say.** FINAL raw: learn 0.299, fixed 0.249, random 0.118, 10 targets; learn − random +0.181 (Wilcoxon p = 0.00195, n = 10 targets); **learn − fixed = +0.050**. Most of the margin over random is the fixed cross-target transfer model (0.249), not learning from in-loop feedback.
4. **Target composition (families from `retro_recovery/data/targets.csv`, Pfam-Jaccard families):** 7 of the 10 evaluated targets are GPCRs of the single family F997e29c2 (SSTR2, SSTR3, SSTR5, MC1R, MC3R, MC5R; plus MC4R/other family members), KRAS and NRAS share family F693c7bf0, CDK2 is alone. The background for every evaluated target therefore contains close paralogs/sister receptors and often the same or analogue peptides measured on them (selectivity panels). This is the cross-family leakage pattern that our family-disjoint benchmark was built to remove. Hypothesis H-leak: `fixed`/`learn` margins over random shrink substantially when the background is family-disjoint.
5. **Other design notes (no change made):** pAff pools assay types and labs (IC50/Ki/Kd); staged features are 2,048-bit Morgan bits (no chirality information is recoverable: the npz has no SMILES) + 16 unnamed descriptors; N_BG fixed at 3,000; 10 targets span only 4 families, so n = 10 targets is about 3 independent clusters; 106 eligible targets in total (>= 60 rows).

## 2. Part B pre-registration (lab-offset anchor test)
Data: CycPeptMPDB loader set (6,960 peptides, within-source labels per PREREG_LOOP L3; raw labels = the PAMPA column). Anchors: peptides measured in >= 2 sources (via `Same_Peptides_ID/Same_Peptides_Source/Same_Peptides_Permeability/Same_Peptides_Assay`; PAMPA values only; 434 peptides in >1 row, 391 in >1 source before filtering). **Offset estimate** for source s from k seeded anchors: b_s = mean over its k anchors of (y_anchor in s − mean of y of the same peptide in the other sources), k ∈ {3, 5, 10}; sources with fewer than k anchors are excluded from that k and counted. **Models:** chirality-aware Morgan count fingerprint (radius 2, 2,048) HistGBM (the baseline of docs/20261029_BASELINE_ERROR_ANALYSIS.md), trained on all sources except the held-out one (leave-one-source-out; sources with >= 30 peptides held out). Training label variants: (a) raw pooled labels; (b) anchor-calibrated labels y − b_s (b_s from k anchors per training source; sources without k anchors keep raw labels and are flagged); (c) within-source z-scored labels (upper reference; uses all labels of each source). **Primary metric:** per held-out source, within-source Spearman between prediction and the held-out labels; gain = (b) − (a), bootstrap over held-out sources (95 % CI). **Secondary:** pooled Spearman on raw labels of held-out peptides after adding the held-out source's anchor-estimated b_s to the predictions (the practical use). **Kill criterion (owner):** median gain of (b) over (a) < 0.03 Spearman at k = 5 (also reported at 3 and 10). **Positive control:** synthetic labels y = f(x) + b_s with known offsets b_s (SD 1) on the same sources; the offset estimator must recover b_s (Pearson > 0.9 at k = 5) and (b) must beat (a) by > 0.1 Spearman; **negative control:** same with b_s = 0 must give gain ≈ 0 (CI includes 0). Anchors seeded (seed 0–4, report spread); assay mixing: PAMPA only.

## 8. Amendment P-B2 (2026-10-30, after the Part B result and an independent code review; dated BEFORE the rerun)
**Defect (verified independently):** in `anchor_test.py`, the offsets of the TRAINING sources were estimated from anchors whose partner value `y_others` is the mean over all other sources that measured the same peptide — including the held-out source. Held-out labels therefore entered the calibrated training labels. Count of training-source anchors whose partner set contains the held-out source (anchors available / affected / partner is only the held-out source): 2015_Ahlbach 51/10/5, 2015_Wang 49/19/4, **2018_Naylor 49/21/5**, 2023_Ghosh 52/11/2 (all other held-out sources 0 affected). Direction of bias: calibration used information from the evaluation labels, so the first Part B gain could only be inflated, not deflated; the registered kill criterion (median gain < 0.03) was met anyway, so the first result's verdict cannot be a leak artefact, but the numbers are superseded.
**Fix (same hypotheses, thresholds and rules):** `prep_anchor_data.py` stores per-partner-source values (`other_vals`: source → mean PAMPA of that peptide in that source). In every held-out task, anchors of training sources are rebuilt with partners restricted to training sources (the held-out source and its labels removed from `y_others`); an anchor with no remaining partner is dropped; sources then need ≥ k remaining anchors (so fewer calibrated sources at k=3/5). The held-out source's own offset for the practical pooled metric uses its anchors against training-source partners only (same rule). Synthetic worlds (planted, planted_full, null) apply the identical restriction (per-partner synthetic values), so the controls test the fixed estimator. A unit test (`test_anchor_no_leak.py`) must show that changing every held-out-source label changes neither the training offsets nor the calibrated training labels. Everything else (models, seeds, folds, metrics, kill criterion, bootstrap) unchanged. The first Part B result (§5) is retained as "v1, leaky".

## 9. Amendment D1 (2026-10-30): pre-registered DESIGN analysis — what shared anchor panel makes lab-offset calibration work? (committed BEFORE any simulation code is run)
Status: simulation + data census, CPU only. It does not test anything prospectively. Motivated by Part B (kill criterion met under sparse anchor coverage; planted full-coverage control works).
**D1-a Data-derived inputs (estimated, not assumed, in this order):** (i) lab sizes: empirical distribution of rows per source (42 sources; 15 with ≥30 rows) — labs are drawn by resampling this distribution; (ii) within-lab label SD: per-source SD of raw PAMPA; (iii) anchor/replicate measurement noise σ_m: pooled SD of repeated PAMPA measurements of the same peptide in the same source (Same_Peptides lists and duplicate rows); (iv) between-lab offset SD τ: estimated from the 57 real anchor differences d = y_self − y_others by a method-of-moments random-effects fit, τ̂² = max(0, var(d) − σ_m²·(1 + 1/m̄)) with its bootstrap CI over anchors (reported as weakly identified given 57 anchors and 6–8 sources). **External assumption, labelled:** inter-laboratory IC50 scatter of about 0.68 log units (literature, not re-verified here) is used as one τ setting by interpreting it as the SD of pairwise lab differences, τ = 0.68/√2 ≈ 0.48; the grid below also brackets it.
**D1-b Simulation world (fixed before running):** features = the first 200 principal components of the chirality-aware 2,048-bit Morgan matrix of the 7,027 CycPeptMPDB PAMPA rows (fixed seed); true peptide signal g(x) = w·PC(x) with w ~ N(0,1) scaled so that the leave-one-lab-out within-lab Spearman of the uncalibrated model equals the real value 0.28 ± 0.03 at the data-derived τ̂ and within-lab noise (calibrated once, then frozen across the grid); labels y = g(x) + b_s + ε, b_s ~ N(0, τ²), ε ~ N(0, σ_w²). Learner = ridge regression on the 200 PCs (a stand-in for the GBM: chosen for speed; a gradient-boosted check at the 6 headline cells is run on the real chirality-aware fingerprints and reported as sensitivity). L labs per simulated study (labs drawn from the empirical size distribution, minimum 30 rows).
**D1-c Anchor panel and estimator:** a SHARED panel of P compounds is added to every lab, each measured r times per lab with anchor noise σ_a (σ_a = σ_m in the base setting; a ×2 setting is also run). Offset estimate b̂_s = mean over panel compounds of (panel measurement in lab s) − (the same compound's mean over all other labs), the registered estimator generalised to a shared panel; labels of lab s are calibrated as y − b̂_s. Held-out lab: leave-one-lab-out, metric = within-held-out-lab Spearman of predictions (offset-invariant), gain = Spearman(calibrated-training model) − Spearman(raw-training model); panel measurements of the held-out lab are never used in training offsets (the P-B2 leak rule applies).
**D1-d Grid:** τ ∈ {0.25, τ̂ (data), 0.48 (external), 0.75, 1.0}; σ_w ∈ {data value, ×1.5}; σ_a ∈ {σ_m, 2σ_m}; P ∈ {3, 5, 10, 20, 40}; r ∈ {1, 2, 3}; L ∈ {8, 15, 30, 60}. Replicates: 200 simulated studies per cell (study = L labs, all held out in turn). Per study: mean gain over held-out labs and a lab-cluster bootstrap (2,000) 95 % interval.
**D1-e Outcomes and decision rules:** per cell, power_0.03 = share of studies with mean gain ≥ 0.03 AND lower 95 % bound > 0; power_0.05 likewise with ≥ 0.05. A panel design "works" in a cell if power ≥ 0.80. Report the smallest panel (P·r total measurements per lab, then P) that works for each (τ, σ_w, σ_a, L); report the median gain and its interquartile range; null checks: τ = 0 must give power ≈ 5 % of the one-sided lower-bound-above-0 test (false-positive rate reported), and a no-signal check (w = 0) must give gain ≈ 0. Planted positive control: the offset estimator must recover b_s with r ≥ 0.9 at P = 10, r = 1, base noise.
**D1-f Retrospective census (no simulation):** from the Same_Peptides tables: (1) number of peptides measured in ≥2, ≥3, ≥4 sources (PAMPA only, and any assay); (2) greedy set cover: the panel of size P (P = 3…40) maximising the number of labs with ≥ k panel members (k = 3, 5), and the resulting coverage of labs and of rows (share of the 7,027 rows in covered labs); (3) the largest such panel's coverage of the 15 held-out-eligible labs.
