## 0. What was found before running (read-only audit of code and logs)
Sources: `research/guangzhou_all_atom/c2_retro_loop.py` (S3 copy identical), `c2_launch.sh`, S3 `cyclic_peptide/c2_loop/` (`c2_staged.npz` identical to `results/c2_staged.npz`, `out/c2_stdout.txt`, `out/bootstrap.log`; no `c2_result.json`, no `DONE` flag).
1. **Design.** Within-target active learning: 5 random seed compounds, batch 5, 12 rounds (65 compounds acquired), pool capped at 600 per target, hit = top 20 % of pAff within the target; metric = recall of those hits after 65 acquisitions; "novel" = hits in Tanimoto-0.5 clusters absent from the seed set. Arms: `learn` (HistGBM retrained each round on background + acquired), `fixed` (trained once on background + seed, never updated), `random`. **Background = 3,000 rows sampled from all OTHER targets (pAff of other targets) and is used by `learn` and `fixed`.**
2. **The logged run is not the current code.** `out/c2_stdout.txt` (2026-10-21 17:14Z, 3,092 s) shows 10 targets and modes learn/fixed/random; the committed v2 (commit 008226f89 / 270543219, 2026-10-22) defaults to all 106 targets and adds UCB/Thompson. The v2 launcher configuration (all targets, 3 seeds) has no recorded result. The documented result is therefore from an earlier script version; whether the earlier version equals the current script restricted to 10 targets is tested in A-repro.
3. **What the logged numbers say.** FINAL raw: learn 0.299, fixed 0.249, random 0.118, 10 targets; learn − random +0.181 (Wilcoxon p = 0.00195, n = 10 targets); **learn − fixed = +0.050**. Most of the margin over random is the fixed cross-target transfer model (0.249), not learning from in-loop feedback.
4. **Target composition (families from `retro_recovery/data/targets.csv`, Pfam-Jaccard families):** 7 of the 10 evaluated targets are GPCRs of the single family F997e29c2 (SSTR2, SSTR3, SSTR5, MC1R, MC3R, MC5R; plus MC4R/other family members), KRAS and NRAS share family F693c7bf0, CDK2 is alone. The background for every evaluated target therefore contains close paralogs/sister receptors and often the same or analogue peptides measured on them (selectivity panels). This is the cross-family leakage pattern that our family-disjoint benchmark was built to remove. Hypothesis H-leak: `fixed`/`learn` margins over random shrink substantially when the background is family-disjoint.
5. **Other design notes (no change made):** pAff pools assay types and labs (IC50/Ki/Kd); staged features are 2,048-bit Morgan bits (no chirality information is recoverable: the npz has no SMILES) + 16 unnamed descriptors; N_BG fixed at 3,000; 10 targets span only 4 families, so n = 10 targets is about 3 independent clusters; 106 eligible targets in total (>= 60 rows).

## 1. Part A pre-registration
**A-repro.** Run the S3 script unchanged with `C2_MAX_TARGETS=10 C2_MODES=learn,fixed,random C2_SEEDS=3` on CPU spot. *Reproduces* if every per-target final raw recall (learn/fixed/random) is within ±0.02 of the logged stdout lines and the aggregate learn/fixed/random within ±0.01 of 0.299/0.249/0.118; otherwise report that the stdout came from a different code version (record which arm moved). Then run the launcher configuration (`C2_MAX_TARGETS=200`, 3 seeds, learn/fixed/random) to produce the missing `c2_result.json` for all 106 targets.
**A-ext (own script, same data, same seeds structure).** Background conditions: `full` (original), `famdisjoint` (drop every background row whose target is in the evaluated target's family, and every background row whose fingerprint is identical to a pool compound), `none` (no background; seed + acquired only). Arms: `learn`, `fixed`, `random`, `nn` (score of a pool compound = Tanimoto-weighted mean pAff of its 3 nearest revealed compounds; the analog-lookup baseline), `desc` (HistGBM on the 16 descriptors only, background + acquired). Controls: `shuffled` (permute pAff within each evaluated target pool; all arms must be compatible with random) and `planted` (replace the target pAff by a known function of 3 fingerprint bits + noise; `learn` and `nn` must beat random and `fixed` must not exceed it under `famdisjoint`). Metrics: final raw recall at 65 acquisitions and the novel-cluster hits; per-target; paired Wilcoxon; **family-cluster bootstrap (2,000 resamples over families) 95 % CIs** for learn − random, fixed − random, learn − fixed, learn − nn.
**Decision rules.** (i) *H-leak supported* if (fixed − random) under `famdisjoint` is < 50 % of its value under `full`, CI over families reported. (ii) *Loop learning claim supported* only if, under `famdisjoint`, the lower 95 % bound of learn − fixed > 0 AND of learn − nn > 0 AND the shuffled control's learn − random CI includes 0 AND the planted control passes. (iii) Otherwise the C2 result is reported as "margin over random is explained by cross-target/analog transfer and does not demonstrate in-loop learning". Typicality/validity: report `desc`-only and size-only conditions alongside, so a size shortcut is visible.
**Limits stated in advance:** no chirality-aware features (npz lacks SMILES; a rebuild from `binding_affinity_v1.csv` is out of scope for this pre-registration); retrospective replay assays only measured compounds; 106 targets cluster into few families.

## 2. Part B pre-registration (lab-offset anchor test)
Data: CycPeptMPDB loader set (6,960 peptides, within-source labels per PREREG_LOOP L3; raw labels = the PAMPA column). Anchors: peptides measured in >= 2 sources (via `Same_Peptides_ID/Same_Peptides_Source/Same_Peptides_Permeability/Same_Peptides_Assay`; PAMPA values only; 434 peptides in >1 row, 391 in >1 source before filtering). **Offset estimate** for source s from k seeded anchors: b_s = mean over its k anchors of (y_anchor in s − mean of y of the same peptide in the other sources), k ∈ {3, 5, 10}; sources with fewer than k anchors are excluded from that k and counted. **Models:** chirality-aware Morgan count fingerprint (radius 2, 2,048) HistGBM (the baseline of docs/20261029_BASELINE_ERROR_ANALYSIS.md), trained on all sources except the held-out one (leave-one-source-out; sources with >= 30 peptides held out). Training label variants: (a) raw pooled labels; (b) anchor-calibrated labels y − b_s (b_s from k anchors per training source; sources without k anchors keep raw labels and are flagged); (c) within-source z-scored labels (upper reference; uses all labels of each source). **Primary metric:** per held-out source, within-source Spearman between prediction and the held-out labels; gain = (b) − (a), bootstrap over held-out sources (95 % CI). **Secondary:** pooled Spearman on raw labels of held-out peptides after adding the held-out source's anchor-estimated b_s to the predictions (the practical use). **Kill criterion (owner):** median gain of (b) over (a) < 0.03 Spearman at k = 5 (also reported at 3 and 10). **Positive control:** synthetic labels y = f(x) + b_s with known offsets b_s (SD 1) on the same sources; the offset estimator must recover b_s (Pearson > 0.9 at k = 5) and (b) must beat (a) by > 0.1 Spearman; **negative control:** same with b_s = 0 must give gain ≈ 0 (CI includes 0). Anchors seeded (seed 0–4, report spread); assay mixing: PAMPA only.

## 3. Amendments dated before the runs (2026-10-30)
- **A-ext target set (compute-driven):** the extension arms/controls run on the 10 logged targets plus 30 further targets drawn with a fixed seed (numpy `default_rng(0)` over the other eligible targets); shuffled/planted controls on the 10 logged + the first 10 of the extra 30. Pools and backgrounds for the logged targets are identical to the original script (same RNG stream). All fits use the original `c2.model()` (HistGBM, 200 iterations) with `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`.
- **Part B coverage finding (before any model was fitted on real labels):** the data prep (`prep_anchor_data.py`) finds only **57 anchor rows** with PAMPA values from at least one other source; sources with ≥3 / ≥5 / ≥10 anchors: 8 / 6 / 0. The four sources holding ~85 % of the rows (Townsend, Kelly, CHUGAI, Furukawa) have 0 anchors. k = 10 is infeasible; calibration can only change labels of ≤8 small sources. The test is run as registered with this limit declared; a second planted world with synthetic anchors in EVERY source (`planted_full`, 8 anchors/source) is added as a control to separate "method fails" from "data lacks anchors". Offsets are defined relative to the mean of other sources' values (as registered), so even with full coverage the calibrated labels carry a residual per-source offset (reference noise); a least-squares anchor-graph estimator would be a new, separately registered version.

## 4. Audit of `replay_permeability.py` / `results/permeability_replay.json` (2026-09-12 result; audited 2026-10-30)
**What it is.** Within-publication finite-library replay on three CycPeptMPDB sources (2020_Townsend, 2021_Kelly, 2024_Faris): random permutation split per seed (20 % untouched test / 80 % candidate pool), 20 initial labels, 5 rounds x 10 selections (70 revealed), hit = top decile of the candidate pool (metric only), random vs RandomForest (64 trees, leaf 2) mean + 1 tree-SD, seeds 11/29/47, no pretrained model; chirality-aware Morgan (1,024 bits).
**Code and data handling (checked).** Source file hash identical to the stored one (`54e2be28…2e52`); exclusion count identical (247 rows: floor ≤ −9.99, `Detection_Limit_1/2` notes, non-finite) — conservative: it removes censored compounds, biasing the pool toward measurable ones (stated by the script). Molecule identity = isomeric canonical SMILES, so stereoisomers are distinct molecules (0 duplicate rows collapsed); policies are paired (same split and the same initial set per seed: verified True for all 9 source x seed pairs); metrics never enter acquisition. **Stereo/analog structure:** Townsend 2,866 molecules on 254 flat structures (100 % in groups of ≥2; max 52), Kelly 1,519 on 181 (92 %; max 116), Faris 207 on 43 (84 %): the random molecule-disjoint split leaves stereo-isomer siblings on both sides, so this is within-library interpolation of a stereo/N-methyl scan, not extrapolation.
**Reproduction (CPU, minutes, no cloud).** Random arm reproduces exactly (hits identical in 9/9 source x seed runs; held-out MAE within 0.002). The forest-UCB arm does **not** reproduce bit-for-bit in this environment (hits per seed Townsend stored [27, 34, 26] vs rerun [23, 36, 27]; Kelly seed 47 stored 5 vs rerun 9; Faris identical), mean Townsend 29.0 vs 28.7, Kelly 17.3 vs 18.7: tie-breaking/library-version sensitivity of tree predictions. Direction and magnitude reproduce; exact numbers must not be quoted to more than rounding.
**Stored effect (hits among 70 revealed):** Townsend random 9.0 vs UCB 29.0 (paired differences +19, +24, +17); Kelly 7.3 vs 17.3 (+18, +13, −1); Faris 8.0 vs 11.3 (+1, +5, +4); ratios 3.2x, 2.4x, 1.4x. Three seeds: direction only, no interval claims.
**Comparison with L3/L5.** Same direction (model-guided > random in-distribution). L5 (810/810, pre-registered): in-distribution 236.1 vs 49.8 hits (+186.3 [158.5, 209.0], 4.7x; +163.4 [128.1, 196.3] after near-duplicate removal); scaffold shift +22.2 (−1.6, 44.1) — pre-registered claim not met; a zero-learning physchem rule ties the loop under shift; 69 % of in-distribution picks come from the largest source. The replay's three campaigns are the sources that carry that concentration (Townsend 52.8 % and Kelly 22 % of within-source hits in L5), so it tests exactly where the loop works; the two analyses agree and neither tests extrapolation. Differences: replay is single-source (no between-lab confound), 70 labels vs hundreds, random-only baseline (no rule baseline), 3 seeds vs 30.
