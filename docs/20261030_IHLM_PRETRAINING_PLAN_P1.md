## 1. Corpus (target ≥ 1 M peptide/macrocycle structures of ~3.0 M; the real core is thin and this is stated up front)

|---|---|---|---|

| chemical grammar | ChEMBL (existing 2.87 M corpus) | **≤ 10 % of tokens** | grammar only |
| label-free augmentation | residue substitution from NORINE monomer tables, stereo flips, N-Me edits | ≤ 10 % of tokens (arm-controlled) | see §4 risks |
**Excluded entirely from the main corpus: every CycPeptMPDB structure** (flat-structure key, stereo-aware canonical SMILES, and Tanimoto ≥ 0.9 neighbours), because the leave-labs-out regime and the loop replay use CycPeptMPDB as the hidden-label oracle. A separately disclosed ablation variant that includes CycPeptMPDB structures (no labels) may be run later; it is never the main model.
**Fold filter (applies to the one corpus, not per fold):** one P1 serves all folds, so the corpus excludes the **union over all five folds** of held-out compounds — i.e. every compound in `retro_recovery/data/compounds.csv.gz` (16,195) — by flat-structure key and by Morgan-r2 (2048-bit) Tanimoto ≥ 0.9, with a sensitivity corpus filtered at ≥ 0.8; counts reported per source (`v4_eval/filter_corpus.py`, exact; blocked == brute force verified; 2 tests). Deduplicated flat-structure-wise and stereo-aware (isomeric canonical SMILES); duplicates of the same flat structure are retained only when stereo-distinct.
**Tokenisation:** atom-level, chirality preserved (`@`, `@@`, bracket atoms), topology tokens (macro-ring count, S–S) and an open vocabulary (`Tokenizer.fit` adds observed tokens). Topology tokens are validated against the Stage B manifest closure-pair ground truth (gate I4).

## 2. Model
Own backbone (`ihlm/model.py`), encoder or decoder configurable. Pre-registered main configuration: **d = 768, 10 layers, 12 heads, max_len 512, ≈ 94 M parameters including the (unused-in-pretraining) cross-attention blocks, ≈ 71 M without them** (inside the 60–120 M target). Objective: masked-token or causal LM on SMILES tokens (decoder arm decided by the intrinsic gates in §3; both are cheap to compare at small scale). No labels and no CycPeptMPDB in pretraining.

## 3. Pre-registered quality gates (thresholds fixed now; the loop may use P1 only if I1–I3 and E1–E2 pass)
Evaluation data: **all CycPeptMPDB** (held out of P1 entirely) with its sources split by `sha256(source) mod 2` into a **development half** (used only to choose among corpus-size/real-fraction arms) and a **test half** (gates are read on the test half, never used for selection). All CIs: 95 % bootstrap over **Source clusters** (B = 2000). P0's own numbers (`ihlm/p0_intrinsic.py`) are the baseline and are reported in §6 before P1 is trained.
| gate | metric | pass rule |
|---|---|---|
| **I1 perplexity** | mean per-token NLL on held-out cyclic peptides, P0 minus P1 | CI lower bound > 0 **and** relative perplexity reduction ≥ 20 % vs P0 |
| **I2 stereo sensitivity** | paired gap (true − variant) of per-token log-likelihood for (a) one stereocentre inverted, (b) a single N-methyl added/removed | CI lower bound > 0 for **both**, and point gap ≥ P0's |
| **I3 sampling (n = 5,000, T = 1)** | validity, uniqueness, novelty vs corpus, macrocycle fraction (ring ≥ 12), max-ring-size and N-Me-count distributions vs real peptides (total-variation distance) | validity ≥ 0.90; uniqueness ≥ 0.95; novelty ≥ 0.80; macrocycle fraction within ±50 % (relative) of the real fraction; both TV distances ≤ P0's |
| I4 topology tokens (reported; not a pass gate) | agreement of the ring-count class and S–S flag with the Stage B manifest ground truth (closure atom pairs) | ring-class agreement ≥ 0.90; S–S recall ≥ 0.95 |
| **E1 frozen-embedding probe** | ridge on mean-pooled final hidden states → within-source-z PAMPA, **leave-labs-out** (source-grouped 5-fold, same folds as the baseline) Spearman | probe ρ ≥ chirality-aware fingerprint GBM ρ − 0.05, lower CI of the difference > −0.10 |
| **E2 fine-tune** | end-to-end regression/ranking fine-tune, 3 seeds, same folds | ρ ≥ chirality-aware GBM ρ, lower CI of the difference > −0.03 |
| E3 typicality control (reported) | target-blind typicality (mean token log-likelihood) AUC on analysis I and M3 (never M2 until fixed) | reported with family-cluster CI; if the CI excludes 0.5 any loop result on those items is flagged |
Baselines for E1/E2: the chirality-aware fingerprint GBM already implemented (`stratification` analysis; `stereo_eval.chiral_gbm_scorer` protocol) on the identical folds. **Failure rule:** if any pass-gate fails, P1 is reported as a negative result and is not used in the loop; the loop continues with the fixed bases and P0 (without a foundation-model claim).

## 4. Corpus-size / real-fraction ablation (pre-registered arms, equal token budget)
Arms (total structures, real fraction): **R** real-only (~0.1–0.15 M); **M1** 0.3 M (≈ 40 % real); **M2** 1 M (≈ 12 %); **M3** 3 M (≈ 4 %); **S** 1 M synthetic-only (0 % real, control); **M2−aug** and **M2+aug** (label-free edit augmentation off/on at equal tokens). Equal training-token budget per arm (smaller corpora run more epochs). Arm choice by the **development half** only; gates read on the **test half**. Augmentation rules: label-free; every augmented molecule tagged and excluded from all evaluation; **no matched-pair structure retained and no label copied** (a variant is never stored with its parent); capped ≤ 10 % of tokens. **Recorded risks:** (1) stereo-flip/N-Me augmentation teaches the model that edited variants are as likely as the real ones and can shrink the I2 gap — hence the explicit off/on arm; (2) edit augmentation creates artificial analog series that could inflate retrieval/edit components later — hence tagging and exclusion from every evaluation; (3) generated pools may carry the generator's biases (PeptideCLM's SMILES conventions, unverified ncAA content) — measured before inclusion; (4) augmentation validity is RDKit sanitisation only.
