(ii) **Censoring dominates**: 63.6 % of I's inactives are censored ('>'), 3.1 % of positives; a censoring indicator alone gives per-target AUROC 0.206 [0.134, 0.280] — i.e. 0.794 when inverted — a stronger no-target signal than any compound-based control (C1–C3 ≈ 0.50–0.60). Analysis I largely measures "has an exact measurement vs a censored non-binder", and every I result must be read with that. (iii) M's registered C3 = 0.315 is the label-inversion artefact the reviewer described (M's decoys are other targets' positives; confirmed by the corrected 0.461 / 0.515); M and M2 are not used to judge arms. (iv) Equivalence cannot be shown on any benchmark at these family counts (14–26 families give 90 % intervals of ±0.04–0.07): even the benchmarks whose primary controls are individually unbiased are NOT_VALID_NOT_EQUIVALENT, i.e. the gate's strictest outcome is "unverifiable by power".
