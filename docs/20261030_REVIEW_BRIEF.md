## 1. One-paragraph status
The paper today is an **evaluation-instrument and baselines paper with controls**, not a model paper. The central comparison (IH-LM against larger pretrained peptide/chemistry models, both in the same loop) **has no result**: no IH-LM or larger model has been run through the loop, and IH-LM's own neural retrieval module and verifier head do not meet their own thresholds (synthetic planted-signal test world: M3 net-only rho 0.525 against 0.70 threshold, ablation gap 0.41 meets its 0.25 criterion; verifier AUC 0.529 against 0.90; marked expected-failure, thresholds not loosened). What exists is verified and mostly negative or conditional: the loop's advantage under scaffold shift is not established, a label-free physicochemical rule ties it there, no benchmark passes the validity gate, few-label gain is analog lookup, both fusion techniques failed or were null, and lab-offset anchors are not supported by public data. Everything unfinished is labelled planned.

## 1b. What the in-house model (IH-LM) is today
> About 830 lines of code (model 287, symbolic 195, tests 248, intrinsic check 102): a small standard transformer backbone with an atom-level tokenizer, an analog-attention block and heads, not trained at scale (no checkpoint). Most modules are symbolic or closed-form (fast memory for laboratory offsets, similarity retrieval, N-methyl and D-flip edit effects, update rules). Three of 15 tests are marked expected-failure with no threshold loosened: neural analog attention, net-only rho 0.525 against 0.113 without it (gap 0.41 meets its 0.25 criterion but the absolute threshold 0.70 is missed); the same module leave-laboratory-out rho 0.444 against 0.60; verifier head AUC 0.529 on new families against 0.90. The 25.5 M-parameter P0 model is a proof of pipeline and the planned 71-94 M-parameter P1 is untrained. It is not a foundation model and is not presented as one. The test results above come from a synthetic planted-signal test world, not from real data; no IH-LM module has been run on CycPeptMPDB or the binding benchmark, so no statement about which parts would add value is made. It becomes a substantial contribution only if a trained P1 passes its gates, the neural analog module and verifier are fixed or removed, an ablation ladder beats analog lookup and the information-privileged pretrained-plus-analog baseline, and a fair comparison with external pretrained models is run.

## 3. What the paper can claim today
**Can claim (with the stated limits and interval types):** a validity-gated, Pfam-family-disjoint retrospective instrument with pre-registered controls; the baseline loop behaviour above; the audit sequence (similarity, typicality, leave-one-out artefacts, estimator and interval-type corrections) as methodology; that loop-specific learning beyond analog/physicochemical lookup is not demonstrated on measured data; the anchor-coverage data-design finding. **Cannot claim:** that IH-LM or any small model matches or beats larger pretrained models; that the loop improves a model on new laboratories; that K_phys or any knowledge helps; novel generation; a first cyclic-peptide benchmark; transferable few-label learning; anything about wet-lab hit rates (no measured data from our own campaigns; wet-lab ingestion and simulation fixtures are integration tests only).

## 5. Known weaknesses
(a) The central claim is untested. (b) Inference rests on one library of 6,960 peptides in which two laboratories hold about two thirds of the peptides and most hits; seed-based intervals do not support statements about new laboratories, and cluster intervals exist only for E-metrics (11 held-out sources in regime L). (c) The gate was applied retrospectively and has low power (about ±0.06 to ±0.10 on 14 to 26 families; zero-shot analyses exclude only effects above about 0.11 AUROC on I and 0.19 on M2). (d) Several results are development data uncorrected for multiplicity. (e) Unexplained negatives (physicochemical K_phys; random feedback beating the loop in that learner). (f) Citations: only Amirahmadi et al. was read in primary text; Graber, Jablonka, CycPeptMP and PeptideCLM 2024 numbers are tagged unverified; others are fetch summaries; the survey and model-survey items (parameter counts, licences) are unverified. (g) The earlier cyclic-LLM work (v4 selector) does not support a binding claim (AUC at chance against hard negatives on 4 of 5 targets; abundance sort 0.996–1.0). (h) IH-LM components below their own thresholds. (i) Intervals have several types (spread, boot-mean, cluster-boot, meta) and are labelled; the pre-registered L5 judgements used the spread.
