# Pilot 5 pre-registration: prior-to-lookup HANDOFF (H5) on the exact-potency new-target benchmark, full family cross-validation
Written 2026-10-29 before any pilot-5 evaluation number exists (stage log in section 9). **Screening analysis (go/no-go for compute); not paper evidence by itself.**

## 1. Hypothesis and status
H5: for a new target with k in {3, 5, 10} revealed exact potencies, a Bayesian-shrinkage combination of the target-agnostic potency prior (weight n0/(n0+k)) with the Tanimoto-kernel GP posterior mean on the revealed potencies (weight k/(n0+k)) ranks the target's remaining compounds better than the best of the pure baselines. Disclosure: H5 was formed after seeing pilot 4 results (prior+correction competitive with lookup at k<=5 in the hard stratum; lookup leading from k>=5). All 47 eligible families were already scored in pilot 4 (dev half used to tune its configs). This is therefore NOT an independent confirmation; it is a nested, fully cross-validated re-test with all hyperparameters re-selected inside training families.

## 2. Data (pilot 4 data layer, unchanged)
`retro_recovery/data/pair_labels.csv.gz` exact rows (relations '='), pActivity = -log10(nM*1e-9); eligible target: n_exact >= 30 and SD(pActivity) >= 0.5 -> 141 targets, 47 families (targets_v2 families). One family holds 56 of the 141 targets (40%): every aggregate is family-equal (mean over targets within family, then over families); the giant family is always alone in its fold, so the 20 repeated fold assignments mainly vary how the other 46 families are packed (stated up front; this is a limit of the design, not a feature).

## 3. Cross-validation (all families are test families exactly once per repeat)
Outer: K = 10 balanced groups of the 47 eligible families, 20 repeats (r = 0..19). Deterministic assignment (p5common.assign_outer): families sorted by (-n_eligible_targets, family id), sizes jittered x U(0.7, 1.3) with rng seed 1000+r, greedy into the lightest group. (Pilot 4's p4common.folds breaks ties by an unstable sort; its fold map therefore depends on the platform. Audit in section 8.)
Training for outer fold f: every target (eligible or not) whose family is not in the held-out fold and n_exact >= 10, z-scored per target (SD floor 0.5), HGB prior on 2048-bit chiral Morgan fingerprints (HistGradientBoostingRegressor max_iter=200, lr=0.1, max_leaf_nodes=31, l2=1.0, early_stopping=False, random_state=0) = pilot 4's `hgb` prior exactly.
Inner (nested selection, training families only): the eligible families outside the outer fold are split into 3 balanced inner groups (seed 5000+100r+f); for each inner group j a prior is refit on training families minus the inner group; inner-validation targets are the eligible targets of group j; reveals use seeds 0-4 and k in {3,5,10}.
Potency scale of the prior: sigma_p = median SD(pActivity) over the training targets (training families only; inner stage uses the inner training families).

## 4. Methods (all scored on identical reveals: permutation seed crc32(target)+7919*seed, seeds 0-9 outer; hard stratum = bottom tercile of max-Tanimoto of the query to the revealed set; identical to pilot 4)
Baselines: **prior** (target-agnostic HGB, no revealed use); **NN** (similarity-weighted lookup, weight S^q, q in {1,3,6}); **GP** (Tanimoto kernel, posterior mean; sig2 in {1.0, 2.25} x noise variance in {0.09, 0.25, 0.64}; pilot 4's fixed GP = (2.25, 0.25)); **d** (prior + similarity-weighted residual correction, q in {1,3,6} x lambda in {0.3,1,3}); **RF** (taken from pilot 4's saved per-target results: independent of fold and prior, same reveals).
Candidates: **H5** = w0 * shifted_prior + (1-w0) * GP_mu with w0 = n0/(n0+k), shifted_prior = mean(y_S) + sigma_p * (prior_q - mean(prior_S)), n0 in {0.5,1,2,4,8,16}; **H5NN** (secondary) = same with NN lookup instead of GP. Selection (nested, inner-validation only; objective = mean over k in {3,5,10} of the family-equal mean HARD-stratum Spearman): best q for NN; best (sig2, noise) for GP; best (q, lambda) for d; best n0 for H5 given the selected GP; best n0 for H5NN given the selected NN q. Chosen configurations are frozen per (repeat, outer fold) and committed (hash) before any outer-fold scoring.

## 5. Metrics, aggregation, intervals
Spearman (primary), NDCG@10 and top-decile recall (secondary), per target averaged over seeds; hard stratum primary, overall secondary; family-equal aggregation; per-family values averaged over the 20 repeats. Intervals: **family-cluster bootstrap** (resample the 47 families, B = 2000, fixed seed) of the paired family-level difference, 95% (2.5th/97.5th percentile); the spread of the mean difference across the 20 repeats is reported separately and labelled as fold-assignment spread (not a CI).

## 6. Decision rule (hard stratum, k in {3,5,10})
Comparator at each k = the best (highest family-equal hard Spearman on the test side) of {prior, NN, GP, d, RF} (the conservative choice also used in pilot 4). PASS_k: mean paired difference H5 - comparator >= +0.03 AND bootstrap lower bound > 0. **PASS** if PASS_k at >= 2 of 3 k. **MDE first:** MDE80(k) = 2.8 * SD_between-family(difference between the best and the second-best baseline, hard Spearman) / sqrt(47), computed from the baseline-only pass (stage 4) and committed BEFORE any H5 score exists. If not PASS and MDE80 > 0.05 at >= 2 of 3 k: **UNDERPOWERED** ('no gain detectable above MDE', never 'no gain'); otherwise FAIL.

## 7. Controls (reported even if they fail)
C1 planted series (pilot 4 construction: p = 5 + 4*mean(S to 3 random compounds) + N(0, 0.3), k = 10, seeds 0-4): NN and GP >= 0.4, prior <= 0.15 (overall Spearman); H5 must track the better component: H5 >= 0.8*GP and H5 >= prior + 0.2. C2 shuffled potencies (10 within-target permutations, k in {3,5,10}): every method |mean| <= 0.05 with CI covering 0. C3 no-target control: prior at k = 0 reported (overall Spearman). C4 fit-reproduction regression (section 8).

## 8. Regression and audit already performed (before this registration)
(a) The pilot-5 prior fit reproduces pilot 4's fold-model HGB predictions exactly (max abs difference 0.0 on all compounds, folds 0 and 3). (b) Rescoring pilot 4's 86 test targets with pilot 4's own fold priors reproduces its family-equal means (all and hard) for prior, NN q3, GP, d_q6_l0.3 at k = 3, 5, 10 to <= 2.2e-5 (hard: 1e-5); 98 of 10,320 rows differ by <= 0.0097 Spearman (float32 near-ties, macOS vs Linux; not bit-exact). Files: `pilot5/results/regression_all.json`, `regression_fold0.json`. (c) Pilot 4 fold-map audit: pilot 4's `p4common.folds` is platform-dependent (unstable tie order), so its fold map cannot be rebuilt locally; the map actually used was inferred from the fold pickles (the pickle reproducing each target's saved prior numbers is, for all 141 targets, the one with the lowest in-sample fit, one pickle per family, loads 56 + 5x17) and saved as `pilot4_inferred_foldmap.json`. Conclusion: **no leakage in pilot 4** (each target scored by a model that did not see its family); the defect is reproducibility only.
