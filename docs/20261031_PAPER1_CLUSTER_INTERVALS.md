## Method (audit rulebook rules 4 and 5)
- **Unit = laboratory (source id).** Seed-level intervals are recomputed alongside for comparison and are labelled "seed-level".
- **Yield contrasts** (hits over the rounds): the cluster of a hit is the source of the picked compound. Per-source hits are recomputed from the recorded picks with the dataset (`hit = y >= thr`); every recomputed per-round count equals the recorded one (**0 mismatches over 221 rows**). Estimate = sum over laboratories of the per-laboratory mean (over seeds) paired difference, which equals the usual mean over seeds. Primary interval: t with S-1 df over laboratories (S = laboratories with a pick in either arm in any seed), scaled by S. Secondary: cluster percentile bootstrap (in the JSON).
- **Held-out-ranking contrasts**: unit = held-out laboratory with at least 15 evaluation peptides and at least 3 seeds; per-lab within-source Spearman (L7) or per-lab AUROC (ladder); t with n-1 df over laboratories.
- **Effective n** is given as "labs (non-zero)" plus the share of the effect carried by the top laboratory and the estimate without it. In regime L CHUGAI and Furukawa-2016 are held out in every seed, so seeds are not independent there; the lab-cluster interval is the one to cite.
- **Caution on the interval.** Hit counts are very unevenly spread over laboratories (Townsend and Kelly dominate), so the cluster t interval is wide by construction. It is the honest uncertainty for "does this generalise across laboratories"; the seed-level interval answers "does this replicate for the same laboratory mix".

## What changes at the laboratory level
1. **Analog retrieval (ladder R3 − R2), yield: the effect does not exclude 0 at the lab level in any of the four cells.** Seed-level and cluster intervals: regime B development +3.7 [1.6, 5.8] vs [-0.9, 8.3]; B confirmatory +3.4 [0.0, 6.8] vs [-1.0, 7.8]; L development +12.1 [6.5, 17.7] vs [-5.5, 29.7]; L confirmatory +9.5 [6.2, 12.8] vs [-4.0, 23.0]. In regime L about half the effect comes from Townsend (49%); without Townsend the estimate is +5.3 (development) and +4.2 (confirmatory). The paper should call the yield effect "replicated across seeds, not established across laboratories".
2. **Analog retrieval, held-out ranking (regime L, per-lab AUROC): the effect survives at the lab level**, with 9 laboratories: development +0.077 [0.007, 0.148], confirmatory +0.092 [0.002, 0.182] (seed-level [-0.018, 0.111] and [0.007, 0.125]). The lower bounds are close to 0 and 9 laboratories is few; call it "consistent in sign and borderline at the laboratory level".
3. **Loop minus frozen in regime L is almost entirely Townsend/Kelly.** Aware, full protocol: +47.3 with a lab-cluster interval [-53.0, 147.6]; Townsend carries 89% of the effect and the estimate without it is +0.9. Blind +13.2 [-7.3, 33.7] (top lab Kelly 54%). The matched-pool `_sub` contrasts also include 0 at the lab level in regimes A and L for all three learners. The only lab-level exclusion of 0 for loop minus frozen is the physchem learner in regime B full protocol (+9.3 [1.6, 17.0]). **The loop-minus-frozen headline of the primary table is not supported across laboratories.**
4. **K_phys minus deterministic shuffled knowledge, yield: mostly includes 0 at the lab level.** Exceptions at the lab level: blind learner development, regime A (+6.0 [0.2, 11.8]) and regime B (+4.1 [1.0, 7.2]); aware confirmatory regime A (+4.95 [0.003, 9.9], borderline). Regime L includes 0 everywhere (the effect is concentrated in Townsend, 50 to 83%).
5. **K_phys minus the plain loop, yield: robust at the lab level in regimes A and B for the aware and blind learners** (aware development A +4.7 [0.9, 8.5], B +4.8 [1.1, 8.5]; aware confirmatory A +2.75 [0.6, 4.9], B +3.65 [1.2, 6.1]; blind development A +2.4 [0.1, 4.7], B +4.9 [2.4, 7.4]); regime L and the physchem learner include 0. Read together with item 4: knowledge-based selection helps over the plain loop across laboratories in A and B, but the benefit is not distinguishable from shuffled knowledge at the lab level, so the claim "the knowledge content matters" is not supported; "a knowledge-derived prior helps" is.
6. **Other ladder modules (yield):** R1 − R0 in B development +1.7 [0.1, 3.3] at the lab level (excludes 0), B confirmatory +2.5 [-0.2, 5.2]; R2 − R1 (lab offsets) is negative in B confirmatory (-2.7 [-4.8, -0.6]); R4 − R3 includes 0 everywhere. No module yield effect other than R1 − R0 (B, development) excludes 0 at the lab level.

## Limits
- Hit counts per laboratory are small and skewed; the cluster t interval with S-1 df is conservative when a few laboratories carry most of the effect. A mixed-effects model or a wild cluster bootstrap would be the next step; the cluster percentile bootstrap is in the JSON as a secondary interval.
- 9 held-out laboratories (ranking contrasts in regime L) is a small number of clusters.
- The yield cluster is the source of the picked compound (pool laboratory); for the ranking contrasts it is the held-out laboratory. They answer different generalisation questions.
- Not recomputed here: the T2 and pilot analyses, which use other units.
