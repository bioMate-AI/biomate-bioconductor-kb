### Gate 1 — 2D Classification Junk Fraction

**Process names dispatched:** `CLASSIFY_2D_R1`, `CLASSIFY_2D_R2`, any process
matching `CLASSIFY_2D` / `CLASS_2D` / `2D_CLASSIF`.

**Parser:** `_parse_2d_classification_metrics()` — new method
- Primary: reads `class2d_report.json` (`num_classes`, `num_good_classes`)
  and computes `junk_class_fraction = 1 - (num_good / num_total)`
- Also handles real CryoSPARC direct `junk_class_fraction` / `rejected_fraction` fields

**Thresholds (CLASSIFY_2D gate block in `_check_qc_thresholds`):**

| Condition | Evidence | Action |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| `junk_class_fraction > 0.70` | [SILVER] Scheres 2012 (RELION), community SPA practice — < 30% good classes is unworkable | **Blocking gate** |
| `junk_class_fraction 0.50–0.70` | [BRONZE] community heuristic | **Advisory** (non-blocking, qc_warning SSE) |

**Rationale:** Novelty vs CryoSPARC Live — Live silently drops bad classes.
BioMate halts and surfaces the junk fraction to the operator with specific
remediation ("lower picker threshold, increase num_2d_classes, re-examine
micrograph selection").

### Gate 2 — Ice Thickness Advisory (CTF_ESTIMATION augmentation)

**Parser:** Augmented `_parse_ctf_metrics()` to also read `ctf_diagnostics.json`
- File schema: `{"diagnostics": [{"ice_thickness": "thin"|"optimal"|"thick"}, ...]}`
- Computes `ice_fraction_thick = count(thick) / total` and `ice_micrograph_count`
- Graceful: if `ctf_diagnostics.json` absent, existing CTF metrics unaffected

**Threshold:**

| Condition | Evidence | Action |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| `ice_fraction_thick > 0.50` | [BRONZE] community practice; no hard published threshold | **Advisory** (non-blocking) |

**Message:** "Re-evaluate blotting conditions: increase blot time or blot force,
or check grid hydrophilicity before next collection session."

**Mock pipeline:** `ctf_diagnostics.json` already generated by
`PATCH_CTF_ESTIMATION` step in `cryosparc_standard_spa/main.nf`.

---

### Gate 3 — Preferred Orientation (HOMOGENEOUS_REFINEMENT augmentation)

**Parser:** Augmented `_parse_refinement_metrics()` to also read
`orientation_anisotropy_ratio` (alias: `anisotropy_ratio`) from JSON.

**Mock pipeline updated:** `HOMOGENEOUS_REFINEMENT` in `main.nf` now emits
`orientation_anisotropy_ratio` (simulated 1.5–6.0×) in `refinement_report.json`.

**Thresholds (added to refinement gate block):**

| Condition | Evidence | Action |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| `orientation_anisotropy_ratio > 10.0` | [SILVER] Baldwin & Lyumkis 2020 (Curr Op Struct Biol) | **Blocking gate** |
| `orientation_anisotropy_ratio 5.0–10.0` | [BRONZE] community practice | **Advisory** |

**Blocking message:** "FSC resolution will be anisotropic and unreliable.
Collect with 40° stage tilt, use detergent additive (membrane proteins), or
consider different specimen preparation."
