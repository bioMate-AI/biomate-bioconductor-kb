## B. Findings (ranked)
**I-1 [HIGH] Regime L is not "leave labs out" for the loop; its held-out labs are nearly fixed.** setup_L (loop_retro/run_l7a.py:72-81) holds out labs only for E; the loop pool and all revealed labels are TRAINING-lab peptides, so the loop never acquires from an unseen lab. Held-out sets (results/regime_L_heldout_sets.json): CHUGAI and Furukawa-2016 are held out in 10/10 seeds, 2015_Wang 9/10, Barlow 9/10; seed 0,7,9 hold out all 18 eligible labs (34.3% of peptides, not 25%: the stop rule overshoots; range 25-34%). Seeds are therefore far from independent; seed-bootstrap CIs (n=10) understate uncertainty. The cluster CIs (11 sources) are the right ones; under them loop-minus-random E source-rho is -0.02 [-0.07,+0.03] (gbm_aware|L). Cheapest test: re-draw L with disjoint lab partitions (k-fold over the 18 labs) and report effective n=18 labs.
**I-2 [HIGH] "Leave-labs-out" groups by Source (paper), not by laboratory.** Same groups appear under several Source ids (Rezai_1/2, Bockus_1/2, Furukawa 2016/2020, Lee 2018/2021/2022, Wang 2015/2021 ...). LSO 0.22 is therefore an upper bound on true cross-lab skill. Also the paper->lab mapping for 21 multi-source peptides and the 178 repaired assignments change group membership. Test: regroup by corresponding author/lab (manual table of 41 sources), rerun GroupKFold (minutes, CPU).
**I-3 [HIGH] The 0.67 -> 0.22 gap is lab/series leakage, not stereoisomers.** random 0.711 -> flat-grouped 0.670 (only -0.04) -> LSO 0.223. Stereoisomer siblings (70.6% of peptides) are a minor leak for the fp models; any text attributing the collapse to stereo-siblings overreaches. Regime C (sibling-free) addresses the small piece; the big piece is within-lab series. Test: add a "within-lab, flat-disjoint" middle tier.
**I-4 [HIGH] A retrospective replay cannot support a claim about a generative loop.** Loop = active selection from a fixed, already-measured pool (CycPeptMPDB; PAMPA only); oracle = lookup table; no new chemistry, no synthesis/assay noise beyond a 20% noise arm, no novelty/validity/synthesizability constraint, no distribution shift created by the proposer. "Yield" (hits in N picks) rewards any ranker over random: loop-minus-random_fb +186 (gbm_aware|L) is expected for any informative model; the discriminating contrasts are loop vs frozen (+47 yield, but E-AUROC gain CI [-0.03,0.15] for plain loop) and cluster E source-rho (null). The defensible claim is: "model-guided batch selection over a finite labelled library on one assay". Hit = top 10% of within-source z, with source mean/sd estimated on ALL peptides of the source (transductive, incl. unrevealed pool and held-out E); hits are 52.8% Townsend, 22% Kelly (l5_label_audit.json) -> yield is largely composition. Test: recompute yields with z-parameters from revealed data only, and report per-lab yield.
**I-5 [HIGH] Analysis I is not a clean binding benchmark and the validity gate certifies nothing.** No benchmark is VALID (gate_A11_*). Beyond the stored verdict: (a) censoring indicator alone gives AUROC 0.794 when inverted, stronger than any chemistry control; (b) atom-LM pretraining corpus (ChEMBL) contains 72.3% of inactives vs 49.8% of positives (contamination_flags x items_I), i.e. corpus membership is itself label-predictive (item AUROC of indicator ~0.39/0.61) and 47% of all items are exact corpus members, so any LM-based arm (typicality, atom-LM) is entangled with ChEMBL provenance; (c) ~14% of rows share a compound with another fold; Tanimoto>=0.7 neighbours 26-33% even after v2/CD. Cheapest test: score every arm on I_EX-like strata (exact Ki/Kd only, matched on censoring and corpus membership) or report arm AUROC conditional on censor/corpus stratum.
**I-6 [MED] Equivalence margin [0.45,0.55] and the "excludes chance" level were set after seeing results (PREREG A11 states this, ~line 172) and the gate is near-uninformative by construction.** With 14-26 families the 90% interval half-width is 0.04-0.07, so VALID is essentially unreachable (their own finding: NOT_VALID_NOT_EQUIVALENT). Gate self-calibration is weak: test_balanced_benchmark_is_valid_at_the_expected_rate (test_gate.py:27-31) accepts as few as 5/8 VALID and up to 3/8 BIASED on pure noise with 60 families; A11 text admits ~4/8 pure-noise flags at 95%. The Bonferroni switch (98.3%) was adopted post hoc and "loosens" the bias flag. A "no-target" control above 0.5 can also be legitimate chemotype prior (RGD motifs predict integrin actives across targets), so BIASED is not always a defect. Test: run the gate on 1,000 label-permuted-within-target copies of I (null FPR) and on I with real labels reduced to exact Ki/Kd only.
**I-7 [MED] folds_v2 is protein-disjoint by accession/gene symbol/Pfam only, and is badly unbalanced.** Verified: 0 shared accessions/genes across folds. But (a) fold 0 is one family (PF00001-only Pfam component: 138 targets, 4,551 cpds), fold 1 is one family (2,438 cpds); for analysis I, folds 0 and 1 are single families holding 4,097 and 2,667 of 9,432 rows (72%); 5-fold CV is effectively 2 giant families + 21 small; (b) true paralogs/orthologs with different accessions and different Pfam sets are not linked (no sequence identity clustering; offline ortholog proxy admitted); (c) the cluster-bootstrap over 23 families is dominated by those two. Test: cluster targets at <=40% sequence identity (MMseqs2, offline if sequences are in uniprot_pfam.tsv) and compare fold map; report leave-giant-family-out results.
**I-8 [MED] C2 binding-loop evidence is internally inconsistent.** results_A_repro10: reproduces=false (learn 0.287 vs logged 0.299; per-target diff up to 0.07). results_A_full106_by_pool_size.json: for n>=300 learn_minus_fixed mean 0.061 with 95% CI [0.063,0.122] (point estimate below its own lower bound; 7 families), and results_A_ext_pool_ge300: learn-random 0.165 [0.063,0.166], 4 families -> percentile bootstrap degenerate. For n<300 (87 of 106 targets) random recall is 0.626 (pool<150: 0.724): 65 acquisitions of ~100 compounds saturates the metric. learn-nn|none = -0.091 (a cross-target nearest-neighbour baseline beats learning when family leakage is allowed), learn-nn|famdisjoint = 0.001: the loop adds nothing over NN once families are removed. Test: restrict to pools>=300 with >=10 families, use a Wilcoxon over families, and require learn>nn.
**I-9 [MED] D1 anchor-panel conclusion rests on a toy simulation.** design_sim.py: lab effect is a constant additive shift, signal is a linear direction in 200 PCs, lab peptides drawn at random from the 7,027 bank (no lab-specific chemistry). tau_hat 0.633 with CI [0.32,0.84] from 57 anchors in 8 sources (census json; file's own caveat "weakly identified"). Stored power <=0.355 even at the most favourable cell; conclusion "underpowered" is robust in direction but numbers are not calibrated to real inter-lab differences, which are compound-class dependent. Not re-run here.
**I-10 [MED] Document/lab leakage in the labelled set.** 42 sources but 15 carry >=30 peptides; Townsend 41.4%, Kelly 21.8%; 1,093 (11.6%) I-rows share a compound with another fold; M/M2 98.8%/43.9% by construction, so M/M2 are correctly retired. Document id for ChEMBL rows "not in stored table" (V5 s.2): document-level leakage (same paper's series across targets/folds) is unchecked for I. Test: join ChEMBL document ids and cluster-bootstrap by document.
**I-11 [LOW] Pretraining/comparator leakage for LLM arms.** Frontier/cheap LLMs (Haiku, Gemini-flash-lite) may have seen CycPeptMPDB and ChEMBL; memorisation probe exists but non-flagship models only (budget exhausted; Tier B not run); non-inferiority rows are 'inconclusive (power)' (n_pairs 3-4). Treat LLM comparisons as exploratory (the PREREG already says so).
**I-12 [LOW] Count hygiene.** "17,120 / 851" vs frozen 16,195 / 793 is corrected in V5 (PASS above). C4 "3.1% of positives" needs the '<' relation counted as censored for positives; '>' only gives 2.5%; state definition.
**I-13 [LOW] Regime B (scaffold-shifted)** shares stereoisomer/lab composition with A; it is shift in scaffold, not in lab or assay, and the 30-seed pool structure is again not independent. Regime C removes siblings only. None of A/B/C tests lab/assay shift; only L does, imperfectly (I-1, I-2).

## C. What a hostile referee would say
1. "Your own 0.67 -> 0.22 shows the models do not generalise; your loop claims are in-library selection." (I-3, I-4)
2. "Your leave-labs-out is leave-papers-out, with the same four labs held out in every seed." (I-1, I-2)
3. "Every binding benchmark fails your own validity gate; the gate's margin was chosen after seeing data and cannot pass." (I-5, I-6)
4. "Censoring alone beats your chemistry controls; ChEMBL-pretrained LM scores are confounded with provenance." (I-5)
5. "folds_v2 is two mega-families plus remnants; paralogs unclustered." (I-7)
6. "Bootstrap intervals that exclude their point estimates, a non-reproducing rerun, and saturated metrics in 87/106 targets." (I-8)
7. "No wet-lab or generative component; the title/abstract must not say 'closed-loop design'." (I-4)
