# SYSUCC (中山大学肿瘤防治中心) — Drug-Development Collaborations, Own Programs, and Ambitions

**Prepared:** 2026-10-30 (web research, Chinese + English sources)
**Scope:** Sun Yat-sen University Cancer Center (SYSUCC, Guangzhou) and the State Key Laboratory of Oncology in South China (华南恶性肿瘤防治全国重点实验室). This report covers drug development only: partners, modalities, the center's own originated assets, strategy, and translational platforms. The center's general research ecosystem and its AI vendors are covered in a separate report.
**Convention:** **[Fact]** means stated in a cited source (date given). **[Inference]** means my interpretation. Claims taken from the center's own publicity, or from the trade press, are marked as self-reported.

## 1. Summary — direct answers

### Who they collaborate with on drug development
SYSUCC works with almost every major Chinese oncology innovator. In 2025 the center said it had **more than 500 partner companies** [Fact, self-reported, Yangcheng Evening News 2025-11-22]. These are the collaborations I verified:

- **PD-1 trials in nasopharyngeal carcinoma (NPC).** SYSUCC investigators led or co-led all three first-line phase 3 trials in recurrent/metastatic NPC: **Junshi** (toripalimab, JUPITER-02), **Hengrui** (camrelizumab, CAPTAIN-1st) and **BeiGene** (tislelizumab, RATIONALE-309). They also ran investigator-sponsored phase 3 trials in locoregionally advanced NPC with **Innovent** (sintilimab, CONTINUUM) and **Hengrui** (adjuvant camrelizumab, published in JAMA in 2025).
- **ADCs in first-in-human and pivotal trials.**
  - **Lepu Biopharma**: becotatug vedotin (MRG003), an EGFR ADC approved in China for NPC on 2025-10-28.
  - **KeyMed**: CMG901 (AZD0901), a Claudin18.2 ADC licensed to AstraZeneca.
  - **Kelun-Biotech**: sac-TMT, a TROP2 ADC (OptiTROP-Lung04, presented at ESMO and published in NEJM in 2025).
  - **Biokin/SystImmune**: BL-B01D1, an EGFR×HER3 bispecific ADC.
  - **MediLink**: YL201, a B7-H3 ADC.
  - **Hengrui**: SHR-1826, a c-MET ADC.
  - **Alphamab**: JSKN022, a PD-L1×αvβ6 ADC; first patient dosed 2025-10.
- **Bispecific antibodies and other agents.**
  - **Simcere**: SIM0348, a TIGIT×PVRIG bispecific.
  - **Akeso**: ivonescimab, in SYSUCC investigator-initiated trials (IITs).
  - **Innovent**: IBI310 plus sintilimab as dual immunotherapy (published in Cancer Cell, 2025).
- **Formal strategic or licensing deals** (where SYSUCC is the licensor):
  - **Junshi**: strategic cooperation agreement plus an exclusive license for the gut bacterium RX-af01. Up to **RMB 500M in milestones + 5% royalty**. Signed 2022-06/08.
  - **Zhifei / Zhifei Longcom**: transfer of EBV vaccine patents for **RMB 100M + sales royalties**. Signed 2023-10-27.
  - **Lanque Bio and the GBA Precision Medicine Institute**: three-party agreement on a therapeutic EBV mRNA vaccine. Signed 2022-08-15.
  - **New Horizon Health (诺辉)**: NPC screening-marker patents (diagnostics). Signed 2022-01-06.
  - **D1 Medical (创芯国际)**: organoid patents for RMB 1M. Signed 2023-12-15.
- **Big pharma.** I found **no** standalone strategic agreement between SYSUCC and AstraZeneca, Roche, Pfizer or MSD. Big pharma appears as sponsors or licensees: AZ licensed CMG901, MSD partners on sac-TMT, and the center has run bridging trials for Keytruda and Opdivo.
- **Not verified in this pass:** RemeGen, Dizal, HUTCHMED, Vigil Neuroscience (维泰瑞隆) and CSPC as named SYSUCC-led strategic partners. HUTCHMED's fruquintinib and Akeso's drugs do appear in SYSUCC trial-recruitment notices.

**Dominant modalities** [Inference, from the trials above]:
1. PD-1/PD-L1 immunotherapy, historically and in NPC.
2. ADCs, the current wave, including bispecific ADCs.
3. Bispecific antibodies.
4. Cell therapy: TCR-T, TIL-like cells, CAR-T, neoantigen T cells.
5. EBV-targeted vaccines and antibodies, the center's own discovery niche.
6. Live biotherapeutics (gut bacterium RX-af01).

Small molecules are present (for example, the first Phase I ward project was avitinib), but they are not where SYSUCC originates drugs. I found no evidence of radiopharmaceutical or peptide-drug origination.

### What they actually do in drug development
1. **Clinical development is the core business, and they lead trials at the PI level.**
   - Phase I ward: **33 beds** across two campuses.
   - **More than 160 active Phase I projects**, over 80% of them Class 1.1 innovative drugs. Annual Phase I inpatient volume is about 4,000. [Fact, Tencent/Time Finance 2025-03-14]
   - Over the five years 2016–2020: 434 new-drug trials, 136 Phase I trials, 116 multicenter trials where SYSUCC was lead site, 16 international multicenter trials led, and **1,406 IITs**. [Fact, SYSUCC Clinical Research Dept page]
   - "Recent five years": **1,237 industry-sponsored trials and 2,117 IITs**, with more than 10,000 patients enrolled per year. [Fact, self-reported, 2025-11-22]
   - The president (2026-02-26) says SYSUCC has led about 200+ global or national multicenter studies of innovative drugs, and **helps bring 5–10 new drugs or indications to market each year — about a quarter of China's total**.
2. **They also originate drugs, but out-license early.**
   - EBV prophylactic vaccines (Zeng Musheng's lab) were transferred to Zhifei. **IND filing is expected in 2027**, according to an 2026-08-22 report.
   - Preclinical work: a therapeutic EBV mRNA vaccine; EBV neutralizing antibodies from a library of more than 50 fully human mAbs; an EBV ADC; small molecules.
   - RX-af01, the immunotherapy-boosting bacterium, was licensed to Junshi. As of the 2025 annual report it does not appear in Junshi's public pipeline.
   - Cell therapy: personalized TCR-T and TAL-T from **Fanen Bio (泛恩生物)**, a company founded by SYSUCC professor Zhou Penghui. TAL-T has an NMPA IND.
   - Most other transfers are small (RMB 10k–900k): biomarker models, devices, software.
3. **Translational validation.** This includes biomarker discovery inside pivotal trials (for example, the RATIONALE-309 gene-expression and activated-dendritic-cell signature), an organoid-guided **phase III** RCT in breast cancer, spatial-biomarker immunotherapy response predictors for NPC, and EBV-DNA-driven NPC trials.

### First-in-class originator, or not? (with confidence)
- **Mainly not.** SYSUCC is best described as China's leading **clinical originator-of-evidence**. It is the preferred lead PI site for first-in-human and registrational trials of *other companies'* first-in-class or best-in-class drugs, and it turns NPC and gastrointestinal cancers into global label indications. **Confidence: high.** The evidence is the trial volume, the lead-PI roles on the three NPC PD-1 phase 3s and the ADC first-in-human trials, the "5–10 approvals/year" claim, and project selection that explicitly prioritizes first-in-class and best-in-class drugs.
- **Real, but early-stage, first-in-class ambitions in the EBV niche.**
  - The R9AP receptor discovery (Nature, 2025-06-18) is a genuinely novel target for vaccines and antibodies.
  - The EBV vaccine and antibody pipeline is the center's credible path to first-in-class.
  - None of these assets is in the clinic as a drug yet. The prophylactic vaccine IND is expected in 2027.
  - **Confidence: medium-high** that this is the main originator thrust. **Confidence: low** that SYSUCC itself, rather than licensees, will carry any asset to approval.
- **Strategic intent to move up the value chain is explicit.**
  - The "1249" strategy, launched 2024-03, lists "自主知识产权创新药物和器械" (proprietary innovative drugs and devices) as one of its nine outcome clusters.
  - The 15th Five-Year Plan names an "IIT-IND dual-track" goal (2026-06-05).
  - Xu Ruihua said in 2025-06 that China must shift "from me-too/me-better to first-in-class". That remark was addressed to Chinese pharma companies.
  - **Confidence: high** that origination is an ambition. **Medium** that it is resourced at a level comparable to the center's clinical engine.

### 2.3 The center's own discovery programs

- **EBV franchise (Zeng Musheng, Zhong Qian; State Key Lab).**
  - Nature, 2025-06-18: **R9AP identified as the common EBV receptor** for both epithelial and B cells. A monoclonal antibody (5E9) and a peptide each block infection. The paper explicitly proposes R9AP as a vaccine and prophylaxis target. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09166-w
  - Statements at the Guangzhou Medical Expo (reported 2026-08-22, http://m-expo.net/gz/news/detail/721):
    - The prophylactic vaccine has been transferred to a "leading domestic listed vaccine company"; IND filing is expected "next year" (2027).
    - Chimeric nanoparticle vaccine published in Advanced Materials, 2025-10.
    - Therapeutic EBV latent-antigen mRNA vaccine: preclinical, preparing a trial application.
    - Phage-display library of fully human antibodies from 100 NPC patients, yielding more than 50 EBV mAbs. A dual-target neutralizing antibody aimed at post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorder.
    - An EBV ADC with "curative" results in animal models. Small-molecule and nucleic-acid programs.
    - **The ADC and therapeutic-vaccine lines are "still raising financing."** Zeng openly invited more pharma partners and said a "global top pharma HQ team" had visited.
  - Earlier: a 2018–2019 Phase I LMP2 TCR-T trial in NPC, sponsored by Sun Yat-sen University (NCT03925896). Status UNKNOWN; I found no published efficacy readout.
- **RX-af01 live bacterium** (Xu Ruihua, tumor microbiome platform). Licensed to Junshi in 2022. No public IND or listing in Junshi's 2025 annual report or pipeline page [Fact, absence of evidence]. **[Inference]** The asset is stalled or deprioritized.

### 2.4 Strategy and stated expectations

- **"1249" strategy** (2024 annual work conference, 2024-03-02): 1 goal, a world-top cancer center by 2035; 2 levers, reform and innovation; 4 breakthroughs, original basic science, clinical innovation, complex-disease care and talent; and 9 outcome clusters. The clusters include **"proprietary-IP innovative drugs and devices"** and **"helping national innovative drugs reach the market"**. Earlier five-year plans included an "原始医药国际创新基地" (international innovation base for original medicines). Source: https://netbig.top/thread-45956-1-1.html (repost of the official report); https://sysucc.org.cn/xsjw1249
- **15th FYP and 2026 messaging** (Xu Ruihua, 2026-02-26):
  - Target: a "world-top cancer center with Chinese characteristics" by 2035, benchmarked against MD Anderson.
  - Strengthen the "clinic → research → clinic" loop and accelerate translation.
  - Build a government–hospital–industry consortium with the Tianhe biomedical base and Tianhe Life Valley.
  - New Tianhe campus (中山大学肿瘤医学科学中心), alongside the Yuexiu and Huangpu campuses.
  - Source: https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260226/herald/95e81dec14077bc0fd095bdaee1108b2.html
- **Huangpu campus** (Sino-Singapore Guangzhou Knowledge City) hosts the national new-drug clinical trial center, State Key Lab animal facilities and a Phase I ward. Phase 2 is planned to add translation (成果转化) functions. https://sysucc.org.cn/hpyq
- **Leadership quote** (2025-06-09): Xu Ruihua said Chinese pharma must shift "from me-too/me-better to first-in-class, combining local disease features." He described SYSUCC's role as discovering which tumor a pan-tumor ADC works best in (NPC for MRG003) and running the registration path. https://www.lepubiopharma.com/new/218.html
- **University framing** (2022 and 2023, President Gao Song): SYSUCC is the "排头兵" (vanguard) of SYSU medical science-and-technology translation; the university wants industry co-development of "原创成果" (original research results).

## 4. Implications for BioMate

Fit is ranked by how closely each capability matches what SYSUCC actually does today. All points in this section are [Inference].

1. **Phase I / early clinical decision support — strongest fit.** SYSUCC runs 160+ Phase I projects, increasingly using seamless I/II designs and dose-optimization requirements (Zhang Li, 2023). It is the global lead PI on multinational first-in-human trials.
   - BioMate's **BOIN dose-escalation** and **PBPK/PK** workflows map directly onto the work of the Phase I ward and the Phase I lab (PK/ADA).
   - Pitch: reproducible dose-escalation simulations, model-informed dose selection and protocol-design support for the many sponsors running first-in-human trials there.
2. **IIT analytics at scale.** About 2,000+ IITs in five years, policy money tied to completed IITs (Huangpu RMB 300k per project), and IITs now counting in performance evaluations.
   - Fits BioMate's workflow execution with QC gates, plus findings and report generation.
   - Biomarker sub-studies of IITs (RNA-seq expression signatures as in RATIONALE-309, ctDNA, spatial/TLS) fit the RNA-seq, single-cell, spatial and variant-calling workflows.
3. **"IIT-IND dual track" and investigator-originated assets.** Their own assets (EBV vaccines and antibodies, TCR-T, the BMP9/NK combination) need **IND-enabling packages**, and the therapeutic lines are *financing-constrained*.
   - BioMate's drug-discovery and IND narrative capability (§2.6.1 nonclinical summaries) and ADMET for any small-molecule EBV program could lower the cost of reaching IND.
   - This is a tangible pain point Zeng Musheng himself cited on 2026-08-22.
4. **Target and biomarker discovery in EBV/NPC.**
   - R9AP, EPHA2 and gH/gL structural biology fits BioMate's **cryo-EM** and structure workflows.
   - Antibody repertoire mining from the 100-patient phage library, and EBV-genome subtype genomics (high-risk EBV subtypes), fit BioMate's genomics and variant-calling workflows.
   - TCR discovery from scRNA-seq with TCR-seq (CXCL13+ T cells) fits BioMate's single-cell pipelines.
5. **Organoid and functional drug testing.** Analysis pipelines for organoid drug-response data, and linking those responses to omics (the breast organoid phase III; Fu Liwu's micro-tissue assay).
6. **Lower fit.** De novo small-molecule medicinal chemistry for SYSUCC itself, because they do not run an in-house med-chem program at scale. Generic first-in-class discovery claims, because their origination is concentrated in EBV and immunology.

**Positioning recommendation** [Inference]: lead with **clinical-development acceleration** (Phase I and IIT design, biomarker analytics, reports usable for regulators and sponsors). Offer the **EBV/NPC origination stack** (target validation → structure → antibody/vaccine → IND package) as the second-phase collaboration, ideally co-funded under Guangzhou or Huangpu translation incentives.
