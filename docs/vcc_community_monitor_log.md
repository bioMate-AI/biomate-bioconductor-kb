## 2026-11-10

**Challenge status:** VCC 2026 CLOSED Nov 5, 2026. Winners to be announced Nov 19, 2026. No final leaderboard or disclosed methods yet. ~5,000 registrants, ~1,500 teams competed.

**New methods surfaced (from curation digest 2026-11-09 + search):**

1. **RELAY** (bioRxiv 2026-10-31): "Relational In-Context Learning for Cellular Perturbation Prediction"  
   URL: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.31.763027v1  
   Uses relational in-context learning — draws on control-vs-perturbed pairs from conditions sharing a variable with the target (e.g., same gene knockdown in a different cell type) to predict unmeasured conditions. Claims to beat in-context and supervised baselines on "unseen variable values" setting (analogous to unseen cell lines). ACTIONABLE.

2. **scDisentangle** (bioRxiv 2026-11-04): "sequential disentanglement for single-cell perturbation response prediction"  
   URL: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.04.764292  
   Notes existing methods often fail to beat simple baselines. Informational context.

3. **Foundation model benchmark** (bioRxiv 2026-11-03): "Where single-cell foundation models help: a benchmark of annotation, perturbation and regulatory inference against simple baselines"  
   URL: https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.03.763968  
   Benchmarks 5 single-cell FMs vs simple baselines on perturbation. Reinforces that simple/hybrid approaches remain competitive.

4. **CellCanon** (NeurIPS 2026 paper, arXiv ~2611.05890): agentic cellular world model for drug perturbation prediction. Strong gains on held-out-drug SciPlex3 (91.3% DE AUPRC improvement). Less directly relevant (drug perturbation, not CRISPRi zero-shot cross-line).

5. **X-Cell** (bioRxiv 2026-03): claims zero-shot generalization to entirely unseen cell lineages in larger model variant. Previously known but worth noting for unseen-epithelial-line angle.
