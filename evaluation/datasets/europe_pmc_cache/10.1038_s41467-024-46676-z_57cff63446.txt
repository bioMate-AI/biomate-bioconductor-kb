The effect of CWC15 on SE degradation promoted us to test if CWC15 is associated with the 20 S proteasome using the BiFC assay. Among four selected 20 S proteasome subunits known to interact with SE43, CWC15 interacted with PAB1, PAG1 and PBA1, but not PBE1, in the nucleus (Fig. 6a). It should be noted that CWC15-PAG1 BiFC signal could also be observed in the cytoplasm.
