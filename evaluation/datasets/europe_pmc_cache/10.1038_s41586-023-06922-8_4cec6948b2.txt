We next wanted to see whether our ability to predict causal enhancer variants could generalize to the other ETS sites within the ZRS. Seventeen SNVs cause an increase of at least 1.6-fold in ETS affinity; this is the fold change occurring in French 2, Indian 2 and Syn 0.25 (Fig. 5a). To see whether these variants drive gain of function, we analyse data from published ZRS reporter assays. In one of these assays that tests the effect of 2% sequence mutagenesis of the human ZRS enhancer on expression in mouse limb buds5, we find that enhancer variants containing affinity-optimizing ETS SNVs are significantly enriched for GOF expression.
