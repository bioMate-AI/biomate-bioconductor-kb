Some serine recombinases have evolved transposition or multi-targeting capabilities, allowing them to target many different attB sites in a given prokaryotic genome51. Efficient insertion into a defined series of pseudosites would be very useful for transgene integration relative to semi-random integrases, such as lentivirus, Piggybac or Sleeping Beauty transposase. To explore this, we tested an LSR from Clostridium perfringens, found in the multi-targeting clade (Fig. 1d,g) in our database, which we named Cp36.
