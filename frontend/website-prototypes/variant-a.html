Variant Hard-Filtering
SNV: QD<2.0, FS>60, MQ<40, MQRankSum<-12.5, ReadPosRankSum<-8. INDEL: QD<2.0, FS>200, ReadPosRankSum<-20.
Source: Van der Auwera et al., Curr Protoc Bioinformatics 2013; GATK Best Practices (2024)
DRUG DISC ADMET
hERG Cardiotoxicity Threshold
IC50 ≤1 µM flagged high-risk. IC50 1–10 µM intermediate. Compounds with IC50 <30 µM require hERG patch-clamp confirmation before progression.
Source: Redfern et al., Cardiovascular Research 2003; FDA ICHS7B guidance (2005)
