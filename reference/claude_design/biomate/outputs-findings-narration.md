## Step 1 — Let's look at what BioMate produced

Your cryo-EM run finished. BioMate assembled the After-run section automatically — outputs, findings, and report all ready.

---

## Step 2 — The After-run block

The After-run block appears at the bottom of the panel on every completed run, with two parts: output links and AI Findings.

---

## Step 3 — Five quick links

Five links at the top — each opens a different view of your run data. We'll walk through each one.

---

## Step 4 — View results: the file browser

View Results opens every output file organized by phase — images render inline, tables are sortable, MRC files open in 3D.

---

## Step 5 — Files are grouped by phase

Phase grouping solves the hundreds-of-files problem. Go to Phase E for the sharpened map, Phase A to troubleshoot preprocessing.

---

## Step 6 — Download all: the ZIP bundle

Download All packages every output — map, half-maps, particle stacks, FSC curves, and a JSON run manifest — from S3 directly.

---

## Step 7 — Methods report: paste-ready prose

The Methods Report generates a complete IMRaD methods section from the actual run record — software versions, parameters, resolution, B-factor, full citations.

---

## Step 8 — Four tabs inside the report

Four tabs: Methods prose, Parameters table, Software list with version numbers and DOIs, and Citations in BibTeX. Export as DOCX.

---

## Step 9 — QC audit log: every gate, every retry

The QC Audit Log records every gate event — metric, threshold, measured value, outcome, and action — plus every parameter change across retries.

---

## Step 10 — AI Findings card: the TL;DR

The AI Findings card is a specific assessment of this run — not generic documentation, but what actually happened with this dataset.

---

## Step 11 — Generated, not retrieved

Every sentence was generated from actual output files — the FSC curve, CTF statistics, 2D class counts. Different datasets produce different findings.

---

## Step 12 — Read the narrative

The narrative answers: should I do anything about this result? For this run: resolution, particle heterogeneity, and a recommendation on next steps.

---

## Step 13 — The three numbers that matter

Resolution by FSC 0.143, particle yield after 2D classification, and half-map agreement. If any fail the threshold, the findings flag it first.

---

## Step 14 — The metrics table: runtime, cost, files

Below the narrative: wall-clock runtime, estimated GPU-hour cost, and total output file count — useful for grant accounting and budgeting.

---

## Step 15 — Citations: what the AI relied on

Every claim links to a peer-reviewed paper or internal benchmark. Claims without a source are marked explicitly as author judgment.

---

## Step 16 — Findings vs. Methods: different audiences

Findings: written for you — short, opinionated, action-oriented. Methods: written for your reviewer — exhaustive, passive, citation-dense. Different purposes.

---

## Step 17 — Want to rerun?

Footer button changed to Run Again. To change parameters first, expand the Workflow settings strip — BioMate skips unchanged upstream steps.

---

## Step 18 — Share this run with anyone

Every completed run has a permanent shareable URL — read-only for collaborators, no BioMate account required, same result six months from now.

---

## Step 19 — This works on every pipeline

This After-run structure appears on every completed workflow — cryo-EM, GATK variant calling, ADMET, RNA-seq, or any nf-core pipeline.

---

Run the workflow. BioMate produces everything you need to understand, share, and defend your results — automatically, every time.
