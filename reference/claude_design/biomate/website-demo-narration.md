## Step 1 — This is biomate.ai

BioMate is an AI platform for pharma, biotech, and academic life science. The homepage introduces the core promise: describe your experiment in plain English, get publication-ready computational results back — no code, no specialist required.

---

## Step 2 — What BioMate covers

Scrolling down reveals the six stages of drug discovery that BioMate supports — from target discovery through clinical trial simulation. Every domain runs on validated, peer-reviewed pipelines, not generative guesswork.

---

## Step 3 — Real science, real pipelines

The platform hosts over 2,000 validated workflows across 34 biological domains — cryo-EM, RNA-seq, ADMET, PBPK modeling, variant calling, and more. These aren't wrappers. They're the same tools published research uses, executed on AWS at cloud scale.

---

## Step 4 — Simple pricing, serious capability

BioMate's pricing starts at $29 a month for individual researchers — a fraction of what a single hour of bioinformatics consulting costs. Teams and enterprise plans scale up with collaboration features, compliance layers, and dedicated compute.

---

## Step 5 — Creating an account

Registration takes 30 seconds. Name, institution, role, email. BioMate doesn't require a credit card to start — the trial gives you full platform access so you can run real workflows before you commit.

---

## Step 6 — Logging in

After signing up, you land directly in the platform. No onboarding wizard. No tutorial gate. The chat interface is immediately available — type your first scientific question and the platform responds.

---

## Step 7 — The chat interface

The platform opens to a clean two-column layout: the conversation on the left, the workflow panel on the right. This is where you describe what you need. BioMate handles the rest.

---

## Step 8 — Sending a query

Type your experiment as you'd describe it to a colleague. Here: "Run ADMET profiling on this compound — SMILES: CC(C)CC1=CC=C(C=C1)C(C)C(=O)O." No pipeline name needed, no parameter flags, no config files.

---

## Step 9 — Workflow card appears

BioMate identifies the ADMET screening workflow, confirms the SMILES input, and presents a workflow card in the chat. The card shows the pipeline name, domain, and a one-line description. Nothing runs until you click.

---

## Step 10 — Parameters are pre-filled

Opening the workflow panel shows every parameter extracted automatically from your message. The SMILES string is mapped to the right field. QC thresholds are pre-loaded from the standard ADMET profile. One button to run.

---

## Step 11 — Confirm and run

Clicking Confirm and run submits the job to AWS Batch. The status badge flips to running. Live step-by-step updates stream back via server-sent events — no page refresh, no polling, no terminal window to watch.

---

## Step 12 — Results, findings, report

When the run completes, AI Findings summarizes the key ADMET flags — hERG liability, hepatotoxicity risk, Ro5 violations — each claim sourced from the output data. One more click generates a publication-ready Methods section with full citations. That's BioMate.
