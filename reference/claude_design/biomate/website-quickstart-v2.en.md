# website-quickstart-v2 narration (EN)

## Step 1 — Home

This is biomate.ai. BioMate is a computational biology assistant that works alongside your team across the whole arc of biomedical science — from target ranking to clinical-trial planning.

---

## Step 2 — Services dropdown

The Services menu organises every capability into three product lines — Drug Discovery, Genomics and Multi-Omics, and Structural Biology. Each one wraps dozens of validated, peer-reviewed pipelines.

---

## Step 3 — Services page

The Services page lays out the full Target-to-IND path, end to end. Every box is a real BioMate workflow on the cloud — not a slide-deck capability.

---

## Step 4 — Pricing

Pricing is intentionally simple. Starter is free. Premium is twenty-nine dollars a month for individual researchers. Team is seventy-nine. Enterprise is custom. No credit card to start.

---

## Step 5 — Newsroom

The Newsroom carries the scientific articles and company news that back the platform — peer-reviewed validation, regulatory positioning, and case studies.

---

## Step 6 — About — How it works

About-How-it-works walks the lifecycle in three steps. Describe your experiment in plain English. BioMate picks a validated pipeline. The cloud runs it. Results are quality-graded and audit-ready.

---

## Step 7 — Start free

Click Start free at the top right. It routes to the platform's registration page on dev-public.biomate.ai.

---

## Step 8 — Register

Registration is quick. Name, institution, role, email. No credit card required for the trial. Your account is provisioned immediately.

---

## Step 9 — Log in

After registering you land on Log in. Existing users come straight here. One click and you're inside the platform.

---

## Step 10 — Platform landing

The platform opens to a clean two-column layout. The chat is on the left, the workflow panel is on the right. No onboarding wizard, no tutorial gate — the chat is immediately available.

---

## Step 11 — First prompt

Describe your experiment in plain English, the way you'd explain it to a colleague. For example, "Run ADMET profiling on this compound" plus a SMILES string. BioMate understands domain vocabulary.

---

## Step 12 — Workflow card

A workflow card appears directly in the chat — pipeline name, scientific domain, one-line description. Clicking it opens the Workflow Panel on the right. Parameters are pre-filled from your message.

---

## Step 13 — Confirm and run

When the parameters look right, click Confirm and run. The job is submitted to the cloud on the right compute profile — GPU or CPU. The status badge flips to running, and live step updates stream back through the panel.

---

## Step 14 — Results

When the run completes, every output file lands inside the panel — figures, tables, reports, structured JSON. Download what you need. The full audit trail — parameters, software versions, citations — is always one click away.

---
