High-resolution MS — from spectra to a defensible result

Turn mass-spec spectra into TMT quant, phospho sites, or HLA antigens.

Your Orbitrap or timsTOF records spectra as vendor raw (.raw/.d/.wiff, converted to .mzML). BioMate routes the run by assay — multiplexed TMT/iTRAQ quantification, PTM/phospho site localization, or immunopeptidomics — to the matching Bioconductor or nf-core pipeline, with no files copied by hand.

What you do with spectra depends entirely on the experimental design — and that design is exactly what decides which pipeline runs.

The problem

Raw spectra are not an answer — and the assay decides the analysis.

A .mzML or a PSM export means nothing until peptides are identified, quantified, and interpreted. And “interpreted” means something different for TMT channels, for a phospho residue, and for an HLA ligand — run the wrong pipeline and you get a confident wrong answer.

1 · SPECTRA, NOT RESULTS

A chain, not a tool

Conversion, search, summarization, and testing must be chained correctly before a single ratio, site, or antigen exists.

2 · THE ASSAY DECIDES

Same spectra, different biology

TMT → which proteins change across channels. Phospho → which residue carries the mod. HLA elution → which peptides are presented. The assay picks the path.

3 · ANNOTATION BY HAND

Design leaks

Search engines don’t emit the channel→condition annotation; a wrong annotation is the most common cause of a silently wrong TMT result.

The pipelines wrap reference packages — MSstatsTMT, isobar/PhosphoRS, nf-core/mhcquant. The goal is to route to the right one and read it honestly.

The ideas you need first

Four ideas behind spectra-to-biology.

From a finished MS run to a readable result is a short, assay-specific chain. These four ideas cover all of it.

1 · ISOBARIC LABEL

The reporter ion

TMT/iTRAQ tags let several samples pool into one run; each tag releases a distinct reporter ion, so one MS/MS spectrum carries abundance across every channel (6-, 10-, 11-, 16-, 18-plex).

2 · THE ASSAY

The hinge

TMT quant, PTM/phospho localization, or immunopeptidomics. This is what routes the run to a pipeline.

3 · THE PIPELINE

A tested chain

Not one tool: msstatstmt, isobar ptm_localization_quant, or nf-core/mhcquant — convert → summarize/localize → test/quantify.

4 · SITE & BINDING

Confidence, not just an ID

PhosphoRS localization probability says which residue carries the mod; NetMHCpan %rank says how strongly a peptide binds an HLA allele.

The assay is the hinge: an isobaric design routes to TMT quant, a phospho-enriched sample to site localization, HLA-eluted peptides to immunopeptidomics.

Step 1 · What you provide

Your MS files in BioMate, plus the one pair your assay needs.

Once your .raw/.d/.wiff are converted to .mzML (or your search-engine exports are ready) and are in BioMate (connecting the instrument is Tutorial 6’s job), each assay needs a specific pair of inputs.

1 · TMT / iTRAQ

PSM export + annotation

A search-engine PSM export (raw.pd — Proteome Discoverer / MaxQuant / SpectroMine / OpenMS / Philosopher) and a hand-built annotation.pd CSV: Run, Fraction, Channel, Condition, BioReplicate, Mixture.

2 · PTM / PHOSPHO

IDs + peaklist

The peptide identification file (identifications.id.csv) and the matching MS2 peaklist (peaklist.mgf); know your isobaric chemistry (type, e.g. TMT6plexSpectra).

3 · IMMUNOPEPTIDOMICS

mzML + FASTA

.mzML from an Orbitrap/timsTOF-class instrument and a protein FASTA; for the full chain, sequencing reads for HLA typing.

A single-run TMT experiment must set reference_norm=FALSE — reference normalization needs a Norm channel in each run.

Step 2 · How a run works

State the assay — or pick the card — then confirm.

Describe the goal the way you would to a colleague — for example, “TMT differential abundance with MSstatsTMT on this Proteome Discoverer PSM export and my annotation, treated vs. control,” or “phospho site localization with isobar on these identifications and this mgf peaklist, TMT6plex.”

Assay stated / workflow card→ Assay routes the pipeline→ Review & confirm→ Runs on cloud compute
  • The pipeline is chosen from the assay. TMT → msstatstmt, phospho → isobar ptm_localization_quant, HLA-eluted → nf-core/mhcquant — a real named workflow, never “undefined.” If the card is wrong, restate the assay.
  • You review before anything runs. Attach the inputs for your assay and set the key choices (which.proteinid, method, global_norm; or type, min.delta.score, min.prob) — for most runs the defaults are correct.
  • Then Confirm & Run. Expect a long cloud run (real MS compute) — phases move pending → running → completed.
Nothing runs until you confirm
Step 3 · The result — matched to the assay

One After-run report, shaped by what you measured.

The run returns a report matched to the assay: differential abundance for TMT, localized site ratios for phospho, ranked antigens for immunopeptidomics.

  • TMT (msstatstmt): test_pairwise_results.csv read by log2 fold change + adjusted p-value (a common cut is adj. p < 0.05); plus quant_msstats_protein_level.csv and the ProfilePlot PDF.
  • Phospho (isobar): estimated_ratios.csv (per-peptide reporter ratios), ptm_info_phosphositeplus.csv / ptm_info_nextprot.csv site annotations, and the noise-model MA-plot PDF.
  • Immunopeptidomics: FDR-controlled MHC-peptide tables from nf-core/mhcquant, then antigen_candidates.csv ranked by immunology/antigen_resolution’s fused score — class-II (DP/DQ/DR) coverage reported explicitly.
  • Downloadable tables and reports for your figures and supplement.
One report, shaped by the assay
Step 4 · Reading and trusting it

Reference packages, QC you must read, and a status you must respect.

The workflows wrap reference Bioconductor and nf-core packages. What makes a result defensible is the QC you read, the localization probability you trust, and the honest status you keep in view.

  • Read the QC first. TMT: qc_metrics.json gate checks (missing-value fraction, library-size CV, retained-feature fraction) and a ProfilePlot with no single channel wildly offset. Immunopeptidomics: a sensible yield of 8–11-mer (class I) peptides enriched for your HLA’s motif.
  • A result can be real or misleading. Trust phospho sites with high localization probability (PhosphoRS ≥ 0.9) — an ambiguous site is a hypothesis about where, not a confirmed site. Be suspicious of a TMT result with almost no missing values imputed (often a mis-specified annotation).
  • TMT and PTM are experimental. msstatstmt and isobar are real indexed workflows but thin single-package wrappers with no recorded real end-to-end run — do not report a TMT/PTM number as validated. Immunopeptidomics is best-in-class but gated on an Orbitrap/timsTOF-class instrument; confirm the model first.
  • A record you can defend. Parameters, tool versions, and outputs are kept together as a reproducibility record.
