# ind.en (compressed for 3:00)

## Step 1 — Welcome, centered

Programs are projects. Colored dots show status. Click to switch projects; work preserves.

---

## Step 3 — New project

Start an IND two ways. Use *New Project*, or BioMate chat. ADMET workflows here pre-fill steps one and two.

---

## Step 4 — Step 1 · Project Setup

Project header requires molecule, target, indication, sponsor, agencies. NMPA adds GLP declaration; PMDA, ethnic bridging block.

---

## Step 5 — Multi-region toggle

Select FDA, EMA, NMPA, PMDA. One source of truth re-renders for four agency outputs.

---

## Step 6 — Step 2 · ADMET screening

BioMate predicts ADMET properties. It evaluates 41 endpoints, feeding dossier sections §2.6.1.3 and §2.6.4.

---

## Step 7 — Per-compound triage

Click *View ADMET table* for per-compound ADMET data. Headers display hERG citations; consistent color shows liability.

---

## Step 8 — Bring in wet-lab data

Computational predictions offer partial coverage. Upload NTP GLP report PDF or FDA NDA review. BioMate extracts, prioritizing measured values.

---

## Step 9 — Step 3 · PBPK qualification

PBPK model qualifies against 30-drug reference set. EMA guideline: Cmax/AUC within two-fold for sixty-seven percent.

---

## Step 10 — The gateway badge

P-B-P-K qualification is crucial; failure blocks IND assembly. Re-run new params: adjust clearance, volume, absorption.

---

## Step 11 — Step 4 · Lead selection

Step four ranks candidates using a GP model. EHVI over Morgan fingerprints scores on five axes.

---

## Step 12 — Read the ranking

Potency is shown by filled circles; four out of five means strong. O-O-D signifies out of applicability domain, flagged in §2.6.1.

---

## Step 13 — Step 5 · IND document

Step five assembles the dossier (§2.6.1), following steps one through four. The right panel displays the assembled document.

---

## Step 14 — Section coverage

Full: measured plus computational data. Partial: computational only. Empty: not generated. §2.6.5, §2.6.6 require GLP.

---

## Step 15 — Live document viewer

The document panel is always visible on the right. Pipeline steps populate the matching section instantly, avoiding reloads.

---

## Step 16 — Jump to any section

The tab strip navigates sections directly. Dots encode coverage: green for full, amber for partial, to reveal gaps.

---

## Step 17 — Per-region view

NMPA adds GLP declaration, PMDA adds ethnic bridging table. This combined view ships to agencies.

---

## Step 18 — Edit any paragraph

Edit any paragraph. Your changes become training data, improving the BioMate regulatory model with your expertise.

---

## Step 19 — Suggest a citation

Click the plus icon to suggest missing NMPA or CDE technical guidelines. Approved suggestions update the corpus.

---

## Step 20 — Regulatory audit

The `§2.6.1` audit bar indicates zero critical, zero major, two minor, four info. The document is signable.

---

## Step 21 — Each finding has a fix

Audit drawer findings show triggers, fixes, and links. Dismiss with reason; rules refine automatically.

---

## Step 22 — Sign & freeze

Sign and freeze. 21 CFR Part 11, §11.200 captures reviewer credentials, TOTP, and signature meaning.

---

## Step 23 — Immutable versions

SHA-256 freezes signed versions. New versions supersede old. Audit trails retain signatures, enabling prior rendering.

---

## Step 24 — Wrap-up, centered

Five-step pipeline, live document, audit overlay, signature freeze. It supports TOTP signing, audit feedback, and collaborative notes.
