The problem this solves

Drug-like chemical space is far too vast to enumerate or screen.

The space of synthesizable, drug-like molecules is often estimated near 10^60 — more than any library, any screen, any amount of compute can cover. You cannot list it, and brute-force enumeration around a scaffold explodes combinatorially while mostly producing molecules that are undrug-like, hard to make, or trivial analogs. The real need is to search that space intelligently — propose only candidates that are novel, synthesizable, and aimed at your objective.

SCALE

The ideas you need first

Four plain words describe the whole method.

You don't need the neural-network internals — just these four, and the rest follows.

1 · GENERATIVE MODEL

Learns to draw molecules

A policy trained on known chemistry writes new SMILES one token at a time — a trained model, not a lookup. REINVENT4 is that generator.

2 · SCORING FUNCTION

Defines "good"

A reward that scores each proposed molecule — drug-likeness, synthetic ease, a docking score. This is your objective, made numeric.

3 · RL LOOP

Generate → score → learn

Reinforcement learning nudges the generator toward higher-scoring molecules over many rounds, so proposals improve with experience.

4 · MULTI-PARAMETER OPT

Several goals together

The reward can combine objectives — potency and drug-likeness and synthesizability — so candidates balance them, not chase one.

The whole method is a loop: a generative model proposes, a scoring function judges, and RL steers the next proposals — optimizing several objectives at once.

The reward · what you optimize toward

The scoring function is the objective — results are only as good as it is.

Reinforcement learning optimizes whatever you tell it to. The scoring_function parameter is that objective, and BioMate exposes three real choices. Pick the one that matches how confidently you can define "good" for your target.

qed_sa · DEFAULT

Drug-likeness + synthesizability

Rewards high QED (drug-likeness) and low SA score (synthetic accessibility). Fast, needs no target structure — good for general lead-like candidates.

docking_gnina

Pose score vs a receptor

Scores an AutoDock-GPU / GNINA docking pose against a receptor you supply (receptor.pdb). Target-aware — an in-silico estimate, not measured affinity.

custom_toml

Your own reward recipe

A user-supplied REINVENT4 TOML combining many components — the most flexible route to true multi-parameter optimization.

Whatever the reward rewards is what you get. A weak or mis-specified scoring function yields high-scoring molecules that are not actually better drugs.

Define the objective well — the reward bounds the result
The engine · generate, score, improve

REINVENT4 runs a loop that steers generation toward the reward.

The generator is a trained autoregressive policy — it writes SMILES, it does not enumerate. Over many rounds it proposes a batch, scores each molecule, and updates so the next batch leans toward higher reward. BioMate exposes the real controls for that loop.

  • n_steps. RL epochs — default 500 (≈15 min on GPU); 200 for a quick run.
  • batch_size. Molecules sampled per epoch — default 128; larger means more diversity, more memory.
  • sigma / learning_rate. How sharply the reward steers the policy (120) and the update step (0.0001).
  • temperature. Sampling spread — 1.0 nominal, lower is more conservative.
  • A learned policy — if the model or prior is missing, the run fails loudly; it never silently enumerates.
A trained generator steered by reward — not a filter over a list

Using it responsibly

These are hypotheses, not hits — the ranking is a starting point for the bench.

Every generated molecule is a computational estimate: a structure the model believes scores well under your objective. None of it is measured. The value is a prioritized, novel shortlist — a list of promising experiments, not a list of answers. The right next step is always the lab.

TRIAGE

Read the ranks as leads

Treat the top compounds as the molecules most worth a chemist's eye — a hypothesis queue, not a verdict.

SYNTHESIZE

Make before you trust

A predicted score is not a real compound. Synthesizability filters reduce risk; they do not guarantee a viable route.

ASSAY

Validate at the bench

Confirm activity, selectivity and safety experimentally. In-silico scores — even docking — are estimates that must be measured.

Generative design narrows an impossibly large space to a testable few. The experiment, not the model, decides which are real.

Estimates that earn trust only after synthesis and assay

REWARD-BOUND

Only as good as the score

RL optimizes the scoring function you give it, not "being a drug". qed_sa is cheap descriptors; docking is an in-silico pose, not measured affinity. Choose a good objective — the ranking is only as good as it.

LEARNED vs BASELINE

Two modes

The default is the real trained REINVENT4 policy. A brics enumeration baseline is offered as a non-RL comparator — clearly labeled as such, never dressed up as a training curve.
