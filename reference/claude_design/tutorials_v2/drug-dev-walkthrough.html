Idea · Why drug development is a loop, not a line Concept

One pass never makes a drug — the method is a loop that compounds.

Each round learns from the last — Design, Build, Test, Learn — so mistakes get cheaper every turn.

The two features that make the loop trustworthy: the QC gate (Stage 5) and the reproducibility record (Stage 7).
Stage 1 · Idea · Framing the goal Concept

Name the target, the objectives, and the constraints — before any molecule exists.

Every later stage inherits these choices — a vague goal yields a vague shortlist.

TARGET
A protein, e.g. JAK1
What you act on — a protein, not a molecule.
OBJECTIVES
Bind · Absorb · Be safe
Potent, orally absorbed, safe on the heart (hERG).
CONSTRAINTS
Novel · Makeable
Keep it new; keep it synthesizable.
These objectives become Stage 4's comparison columns; the hERG limit becomes the Stage 5 QC gate.

Stage 3 · Idea · What is ADMET — and why hERG is make-or-break Concept

ADMET asks: will the body even tolerate this molecule?

Absorbed, distributed, not destroyed too fast, cleared cleanly, and non-toxic — five questions.

A
Absorption
Does it get in?
D
Distribution
Where does it go?
M
Metabolism
Broken down how fast?
E
Excretion
How does it leave?
T
Toxicity
Any harm? incl. hERG
⚑ hERG leads block the heart's K⁺ channel and you risk a fatal arrhythmia — it has killed approved drugs.

Stage 4 · Idea · Multi-objective optimization & the Pareto front Concept

Which handful is the best all-round compromise?

Each goal keeps its own column — never blended — and the Pareto front shows the trade-offs.

You pick the trade-off; goals are never silently collapsed into one number, and rejects stay visible with their reason.
Stage 5 · Idea · The QC gate — why it must never silently pass Concept

A quality gate turns a threshold into a decision you can trust mid-run.

The gate halts or flags — it never quietly passes a failed check.

Endpoint (model estimate)ValueLimitVerdict
hERG blocker-riskLow—safe
DILI · liver injuryHigh0.5HALT
⚑ HALT · flag A gate that silently passes is worse than no gate — it looks like success.
Advisory suggestion: logp_max 5.0→4.0 — a before/after diff you choose to act on, not an auto-restart.
Stage 6 · Idea · PK / PBPK modeling Concept

What dose would a human need?

PBPK projects human exposure — Cmax, AUC, and a starting-dose scenario to reason with.

Projected exposure for evaluation — read as a projection, not a guaranteed safe dose.
Stage 7 · Idea · What lineage & provenance mean — and why they matter Concept

A result is only as good as your ability to prove how you got it.

Provenance is the chain from an output back to its exact inputs, parameters, and software.

Every run captures run id, versions, SHA-256 and citations — one click to JSON. Unreadable output → the finding is withheld, not invented.
