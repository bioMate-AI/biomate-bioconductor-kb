Step 2 · Read

A short, cited answer — every claim traceable.

BioMate's answer to our JAK1 question. Three things make it trustworthy:

  1. Numbered markers [1] [2] tie each claim to the source it came from.
  2. Real citations each one opens to the publisher.
  3. A boundary note what the answer is not evidence for — it summarises literature, it is not a verdict on your data.
