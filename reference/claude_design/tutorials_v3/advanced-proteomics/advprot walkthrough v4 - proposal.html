The question · advanced proteomics

Three experiments,
three different answers.

TMT compares protein amounts, phospho finds regulated sites, and immunopeptidomics finds peptides that bind HLA. BioMate runs each on its own path.

Idea · one result per experiment

Each experiment has its own headline.

  1. TMTMany samples are labelled and run together, so proteins can be compared across them.
  2. PhosphoSites are kept only when the phosphate is placed with confidence, PhosphoRS 0.9 or more.
  3. ImmunopeptidomicsPeptides of 8 to 11 residues are scored for HLA binding, and a rank of 2% or lower counts as a binder.
