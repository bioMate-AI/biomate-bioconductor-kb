The question · cryo-EM

How sharp is
the map?

A microscope records movies of frozen particles. BioMate corrects them, picks and sorts the particles, and builds a 3-D map with its resolution.

Idea · how a map is judged

Resolution is read from the FSC curve.

  1. Split the particles in twoTwo independent maps are built from two halves of the data.
  2. Compare the two mapsFSC measures how well they agree at each level of detail.
  3. Read the resolution at 0.143The finest detail at which FSC is still 0.143 is the reported resolution.

In BioMate · 2 Workflow

Three phases: preprocess, particles, reconstruct.

  1. Correct motion and fit the CTFBeam-induced motion is removed and the optics are modelled.
  2. Pick and classify particlesGood particles are kept and sorted into classes.
  3. Refine the mapThe kept particles are combined into one map, and resolution is measured.
