The question · episode 5 · docking

Which compound
fits the pocket best?

Docking places each molecule in the binding pocket, tries many poses and scores them. Here, BioMate runs it end to end, from structures and a receptor to a report.

Idea · what docking gives you

A pose and a score, always together.

  1. Place the ligand in the pocketA search tries many positions and twists inside a box around the pocket.
  2. Score each poseThe Vina score estimates fit; more negative means a tighter predicted fit.
  3. Trust the pose, not just the numberA reference ligand shows whether the method recovers a known crystal pose.
· the main settings

· an example
Take AutoDock Vina as the example. BioMate also offers pocket detection and diffusion-based docking. A docking score is a pose hypothesis, not a measured affinity, and the receptor is rigid by default.

In BioMate · 2 Workflow

One phase, three steps.

  1. PrepareStructures become 3D conformers; the receptor and the grid are set up.
  2. Search posesVina tries many poses per ligand inside the pocket box.
  3. Score and rank, then validateA reference ligand checks that the crystal pose is recovered.
· the box
The search is confined to a box around the pocket, so every pose is a plausible one.
· the search
Exhaustiveness 8 sets the effort. More effort finds better poses but takes longer.
· the check
Recovering the crystal pose within 2.0 Å makes new scores more credible.

Read it right · before you act on a score

A docking score is not a measurement.

  1. The score ranks, it does not measureCompare ligands with each other, not with an experiment.
  2. Validation is what makes it credible1.4 Å against the 2.0 Å criterion.
  3. The record shows the settingsExhaustiveness, pose count and box, each with its origin.
  4. Use it as a gate, not a rulerEscalate fine ranking to free-energy calculations.
