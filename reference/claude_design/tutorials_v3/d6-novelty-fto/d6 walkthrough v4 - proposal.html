The question · episode 6 · novelty

Is your shortlist
really new?

A structural screen compares each candidate with known chemistry before you spend on it. Here, BioMate runs it end to end, from a shortlist to a report.

Idea · what novelty means here

Novel is not the same as clear.

  1. Compare with what is knownEach candidate is fingerprinted and matched against a library of known molecules.
  2. The closest match decidesIf the best match is below 0.85 similarity, the candidate is called novel.
  3. Novel is not clearStructural novelty is not a legal freedom-to-operate opinion.
· the call

In BioMate · 2 Workflow

Two steps: fingerprint, then compare.

  1. FingerprintEach candidate becomes a Morgan / ECFP4 fingerprint.
  2. Find the nearest neighbourThe library is searched for the most similar known molecule.
  3. The call is the gateOccupied candidates are dropped before any money is spent.
· fingerprint
A fixed-length code for the substructures in a molecule.
· Tanimoto
Shared bits divided by total bits. 1.0 means identical.
· the gate
The novelty call is the last check before outside spend.

Read it right · before you spend

Novel does not mean clear.

  1. This is a structural screenIt compares shapes of molecules, not legal claims.
  2. A no-hit result is not proofFingerprints miss scaffold hops and bioisosteres.
  3. The record shows the settingsThe cutoff and the patent switch, each with its origin.
  4. The attorney decidesBorderline cases go to a patent attorney.
