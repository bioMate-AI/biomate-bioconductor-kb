Design, predict, rank and project exposure, with every number traceable. Here, BioMate runs the chain end to end, from a goal to a report.

Idea · what the loop adds

The advantage is the loop, not one model.

  1. Design and test are connectedCandidates flow straight into prediction, and results flow back.
  2. Each objective keeps its own columnBinding, absorption and safety are never blended into one score.
  3. Every number is traceableEach value links to the file and run that produced it.

Take JAK1 as the target, with absorption and hERG safety as objectives. BioMate offers other design, prediction and exposure tools for other goals. Exposure numbers are projections, not validated absolute values.

In BioMate · 2 Workflow

Four phases, one record.

  1. DesignCandidates are enumerated around a seed and filtered.
  2. ADMETProperties are predicted and checked against recorded criteria.
  3. Rank, then project exposureObjectives are ranked side by side, then exposure is projected.
· design
A seed becomes a list of candidates that pass the filters.
· ADMET
Fifteen properties per compound, with criteria on hERG and DILI.
· exposure
Allometric scaling, then PBPK, projects Cmax and AUC.
In BioMate · 2 Workflow · running

Watch it run, and see where it flags.

  1. Phases complete in orderDesign, ADMET, ranking, exposure.
  2. The ADMET checks run on every compoundDILI probability is checked against 0.5.
  3. Two compounds are flaggedFlags are findings on a completed run, not a stop.
In BioMate · 3 Results

  3. Criteria and limits sit togetherDILI probability against its 0.5 threshold.

Read it right · before you move on

Every number points to a file.

  1. Open the file behind a numbertoxicity_report.json holds every compound.
  2. A pass is not a safety verdictIbuprofen is below the DILI threshold; that is one check.
  3. The record shows the whole chainParameters, software and checks, each with its origin.
  4. Re-run with a change if you wishA suggested change applies only when you re-run.
