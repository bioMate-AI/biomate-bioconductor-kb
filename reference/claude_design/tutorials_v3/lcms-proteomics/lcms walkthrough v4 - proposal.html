The question · LC-MS proteomics

Which proteins differ
between conditions?

A mass spectrometer records spectra, not proteins. BioMate identifies and quantifies the proteins, then tests which ones changed.

Idea · identify, quantify, compare

Three steps turn spectra into a comparison.

  1. IdentifySpectra are matched against a protein database.
  2. QuantifyEach protein group gets an amount in every run, at 1% FDR.
  3. CompareA test says which proteins differ between the conditions.

Two phases: identify and quantify, then compare.

· the FDR

Significant means adjusted p below 0.05.
