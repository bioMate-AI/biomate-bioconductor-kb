The question · cyclic peptides

Can we design peptides
that nature never made?

Cyclic peptides use building blocks outside the twenty natural amino acids. BioMate designs them with its own generative model, checks them by physics, and learns from every assay.

Idea · what is different about peptides

The chemistry is the point, and the generator is ours.

  1. Cyclic, with unusual residuesD-amino acids, N-methylation and fluorinated or chlorinated rings are part of the design space.
  2. Structure predictors are shared toolsAnyone can run them, so they check a design. They do not create one.
  3. The generator is the advantageIt is trained on cyclic-peptide data, so it can propose chemistry that general tools never design.

Idea · how candidates are generated

A generator built in stages.

  1. Learn the language of peptidesAn autoencoder is trained first, so a peptide becomes a point in a latent space.
  2. Then learn to generateA latent diffusion model learns to produce new points, and so new peptides.
  3. Then aim it at a targetA target-conditioned version co-designs the peptide with its binding site.
  4. Cyclic variants extend itA cyclic fine-tune and a cyclic language model add ring closure and unusual residues.

Idea · three checks before anything is made

Structure, binding energy, and properties.

  1. StructureEach peptide is folded against the target as a cyclic complex, one job per peptide, taking Boltz-2 as an example. A second predictor cross-checks only the top few.
  2. Binding energyPhysics-based scoring estimates binding, and a free-energy engine handles cyclic and metal-bridged peptides.
  3. PropertiesAn availability score, permeability and stability close the gate. The availability score is informational.

· why Boltz-2 here
It is open-licensed, runs locally and also predicts affinity, so it can screen at scale. It checks designs and does not generate them.

Idea · the lab, and the close of the loop

People make them. The results retrain the generator.

  1. Making stays with peopleA CRO or a wet lab makes the peptides in batches of 48 to 96. Nothing automates synthesis.
  2. Three assays measure the resultPAMPA for permeability, fluorescence polarisation for binding, and plasma stability.
  3. Results become preference pairsThe chosen peptides are paired with the rejected ones.
  4. The generator is retrainedDiffusion-DPO shifts the generator toward what worked, and Round 2 proposes tighter analogues.

Read it right · where the loop is used

The same loop works for other targets.

  1. Each round is keptThe designs, the checks, the assays and the pairs stay on record.
  2. The generator improvesEach retrain starts from the last one, so the proposals get tighter.
  3. The same loop is used for several targetsαvβ6 in pancreatic cancer, GRPR, and PD-L1 blockade.
  4. A re-run is your clickNothing restarts by itself.
