The question · small-molecule campaign

How does a lead series
improve, round by round?

A campaign is one object that lives across rounds. Each round proposes molecules, the lab measures them, and the results refit the model that proposes the next round.

Idea · one object across rounds

A campaign remembers every round.

  1. It is created onceA campaign names a target, an indication and an objective, here JAK1, rheumatoid arthritis and balance.
  2. Each round is a recordRound number, the proposed structures, scores with confidence intervals, domain status, and what triggered the round.
  3. Nothing is overwrittenRound 2 sits beside Round 1, so you can always see how a proposal came about.

Idea · what a candidate must satisfy

Every objective keeps its own number and its own rule.

  1. Potency is what you wantpIC50 is shown with a confidence interval, because a prediction is never exact.
  2. Drug-likeness and makeability have limitsQED must stay at or above 0.4 and the synthetic-accessibility score at or below 5.0, the workflow defaults.
  3. Safety is a gate, not a trade-offA DILI probability above 0.5 flags a compound, whatever its potency. hERG is shown beside it.
  4. The model knows its limitsA compound far from the training data is marked as outside the domain, and its score is treated with caution.

In BioMate · round 1

Three seeds, scored objective by objective.

  1. The campaign headerTarget, indication and objective sit at the top, and a selector moves between rounds.
  2. One row per compoundEach objective has its own column, with the pass marks beside DILI and hERG.
  3. No blended scoreThe page says so, because one number would hide the trade-offs.
  4. All three start in domainSo their scores can be trusted as a starting point.
In BioMate · assay results come back

Measured values sit beside predicted ones.

  1. The lab’s numbers are ingestedAn ingest event records C3 at 6.3 and C1 at 5.6, each tagged measured.
  2. Predicted values keep their tagC3 was predicted at 6.1 and C1 at 5.8, so you can see how far the model was off.
  3. The ingest starts the refitThe audit log records the ingest, and the retrain job picks it up and refits the surrogate.
  4. Every value says where it came fromReference, species, study type and a confidence level travel with it.
Idea · how round 2 is chosen

A surrogate model, and a rule for what to try next.

  1. A surrogate predicts each objectiveA Gaussian-process model learns from the measured values and says how sure it is.
  2. A rule picks what to tryExpected hypervolume improvement favours molecules that would push the Pareto front outward.
  3. The library is re-scoredThe cached virtual library is scored again with the refit model.
  4. Round 2 is writtenOptionally, the proposal is exported as an order for a contract research lab.

Read it right · who does what

The loop is BioMate and the bench, taking turns.

  1. BioMate proposes and refitsScoring, choosing and refitting happen without manual steps.
  2. People make the moleculesA chemist or a contract lab does the synthesis. BioMate never automates it.
  3. A re-run is your clickNothing restarts by itself.
  4. The record staysEach round keeps its measurements, its model and its lineage.
