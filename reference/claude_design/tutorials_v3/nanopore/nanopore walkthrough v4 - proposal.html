The question · nanopore sequencing

Was it a good run,
and what did it find?

A nanopore run produces long reads. BioMate checks how long and how accurate they are, then aligns them and calls variants.

Idea · N50 and quality

Two numbers say whether the run is good.

  1. Read N50Half of all the sequence is in reads at least this long.
  2. Mean qualityA Q value is the confidence in each base; higher is better.
  3. Then mapping and variantsThe share of reads that map, and the variants found against the reference.
