The question · sample QC

Is this RNA good
enough to sequence?

One number from an instrument decides whether a sample goes on. BioMate reads it against a recorded requirement and writes down the verdict.

Idea · integrity and purity

Integrity says if it is intact. Purity says if it is clean.

  1. RIN and DV200RIN scores RNA integrity from 1 to 10; DV200 is the share of fragments longer than 200 nucleotides.
  2. A260/280 and A260/230Ratios that flag protein or solvent carry-over.
  3. A requirement, then a verdictThe number is compared with what the application needs: RIN 7 or more for RNA-seq.
