The question · episode T2 · models and the closed loop

How far should you
trust a prediction?

A model replaces a slow experiment with a prediction. BioMate shows every model as an object you can inspect, and feeds your real results back in.

Idea · learned once, reused

A model turns an input into a prediction.

  1. Input in, prediction outA molecule or a sequence goes in; a score or a structure comes out.
  2. Pre-trained, or customLarge models arrive pre-trained. Custom ones are trained on your data.
  3. Inference changes nothingRunning a model does not train anything of yours.

In BioMate · 2 Object

Open a model and read what you would be trusting.

  1. A model is an objectName, version and family.
  2. Evidence: a floor, not a rankA measured score against a required floor, on held-out data.
  3. “Not evaluated”The honest reading when no score exists. No invented number.
  4. No leaderboardClearing the floor by more earns no higher rank.
  5. Use it in a runOne click puts this model into an analysis.

In BioMate · 4 Loop

Design, make, test, analyse: recorded round by round.

  1. A surrogate proposes, the bench measuresThe refit runs as a tracked training run.
  2. Every proposal is accounted forOne that produced no number still returns a reason.
  3. Censored stays censoredIC50 > 30 µM is fitted as an inequality, never as 30.
