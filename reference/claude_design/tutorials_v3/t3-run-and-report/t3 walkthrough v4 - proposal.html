The question · episode 3 · run a workflow

How do you go from a question
to a report you can trust?

A workflow is a tested pipeline that runs on cloud computers and hands back one report. Follow one real run, which genes change when airway cells are treated with dexamethasone, from set-up to the three-tab report.

Idea · the words you need first

Five words make the whole report readable.

  1. A workflow is a ready-made pipelineYou run it, you do not assemble it. It is organised into phases, and each phase into steps.
  2. Parameters show before anything runsThe settings are visible with sensible defaults, each marked with where it came from.
  3. Checks and the report come at the endChecks compare results with recorded criteria. The report comes back in three tabs.

· what tested means
Each step uses an established, published method. That makes the pipeline dependable. It does not make a result clinically proven.
In BioMate · 1 Data

Describe the goal, review the set-up, then confirm.

  1. Describe the goal in plain wordsWhich comparison you want: here, dexamethasone against untreated.
  2. BioMate picks the workflowIt opens the panel on the right and binds your inputs: a count matrix and the group labels. Other workflows attach FASTQ, CSV or SMILES files here.
  3. Every parameter shows its originThe comparison comes from your message, the method is recommended, the cutoffs are defaults.
In BioMate · 2 Workflow

One phase, two steps, and the check beside them.

  1. Differential analysisDESeq2 tests every gene for a difference between the groups.
  2. Write resultsThe results table and the volcano plot are saved.
  3. The check runs when step 2 finishesThe number of significant genes is compared with the recorded cutoffs.
· every gene
34,371 genes are tested at once, so the p-values are corrected for that.
· the results
One row per gene, with its fold-change and adjusted p-value.
· the check
Significance is padj below 0.05 and a fold-change of at least 2.
In BioMate · 2 Workflow · running

Watch each step move from queued to done.

  1. Steps complete in orderThe panel updates live while you keep chatting.
  2. The check is listed beside the stepsSignificant genes, against padj below 0.05 and a fold-change of at least 2.
  3. A check result is a finding, not a stopThe run completes and reports what it found.
In BioMate · 3 Results

The answer first, then the evidence.

  1. The answer arrives in chat1,242 of 34,371 genes change significantly with dexamethasone.
  2. Open the run for the evidenceThe panel holds the counts, the strongest genes and the plot.
  3. Each gene shows direction and confidenceFold-change tells you the direction and size; the adjusted p-value tells you how sure to be.
  4. The volcano plot is the same result as a pictureUp on the right, down on the left, more certain higher up.
Read it right · the three tabs

Behind the summary: every file, and the full record.

  1. The file behind the numbersdeseq_results_top200.csv holds the genes with their fold-change and adjusted p-value.
  2. Every file is filed by the step that made itSix outputs, each with a type, so you can trace the pipeline end to end.
  3. The record pins down the runParameters with origins, software, and the check and its outcome.
  4. One click downloads itThe whole record leaves as JSON, for a paper, a patent or a review.
