The question · target identification

Which targets are worth
going after in psoriasis?

Give BioMate a disease. Three kinds of evidence are gathered in parallel and fused into one ranked list, and every target keeps the evidence behind it.

Idea · evidence channels

Each channel asks a different question.

  1. GeneticsIs the gene associated with the disease in human data?
  2. NetworkIs it close, in the protein-interaction network, to genes already known for the disease?
  3. LLM-reasonedWhat does a frontier model conclude from the literature, with its reasoning shown?

· fusing them
The three scores are fused by a harmonic sum, so a target needs support from more than one channel to rank high.

  3. Every parameter shows its originEach setting says whether it came from your message, a default, or a recommendation.
In BioMate · 2 Workflow

Two phases: gather evidence, then fuse and rank.

  1. Three channels in parallelGenetics, network and LLM-reasoned evidence are gathered together.
  2. Fuse into one rankingThe three scores become one, and each target keeps its per-channel evidence.
  3. Tier and routeA target is corroborated when at least two strong channels agree, and gets a modality.
· the sources
Each channel names its source.
· the fusion
A harmonic sum, as in Open Targets.
· the tier
Corroborated needs two strong channels.
