                About BioMate

                The AI platform for biomedical research and drug discovery.

                BioMate takes biomedical work from a scientific question to a reproducible, audit-ready result — across genomics, structural biology, drug discovery and more, in one environment. It is open to any team doing hard science. And we use it ourselves, end to end, to run a focused drug-development program: identifying targets from human biology, then designing cyclic-peptide medicines for immune-mediated disease.

                How it fits together

                A platform that integrates our own techniques — and the program we run on it.

                The platform

                Not connected tools. Integrated techniques.

                Plenty of products wire existing tools together. BioMate is different in kind: it brings our own techniques into one environment — a self-hosted model trained for scientific work, an advanced closed-loop system with a verifiable reward, and reproducible execution across 4,000+ catalog workflows in 36 biomedical domains.

                It is the foundation we build on, the one we always use to do what we need — and it is open to any team doing hard science.

                Our program — on the platform

                First the target, then the molecule.

                We run our own drug development on that platform. First we identify the target — the choice that most often decides whether a drug works — from human genetics and patient data. Then we design the molecule: an AI-designed cyclic peptide, optimized in silico against every constraint and proven in the wet lab, round after round.

                Our focus is immune-mediated disease — autoimmune, chronic infection, and immuno-oncology. Pre-clinical today.

                Our story

                Built for the organizations doing the hardest science.

                BioMate was founded in late 2025 by a team with deep roots in biomedical informatics and NLP. The founding insight was simple: the bottleneck in computational life science is not capability — it is access. The tools exist. The methods are published. What is missing is a platform that makes them usable by the scientists who need them, without requiring a computational specialist at every step.

                We built the platform the way the science demanded: evidence-grounded, auditable, and broad enough to cover the full span of computationally demanding biomedical work — from genomics and structural biology to drug discovery, clinical pharmacology, and regulatory documentation. Where we go deepest is immune-mediated disorders: nominating and de-risking targets, designing peptide and macrocycle candidates, and closing the design–make–test loop with wet-lab feedback.

                Every result on BioMate is defensible — by design, not by documentation.

                Today BioMate is in production with pilot teams across pharma, biotech, CROs, and academic medical centers — 4,000+ indexed workflows, 36 domains, and a quality framework that regulatory reviewers and journal editors can inspect at the parameter level.

                Scientific coverage

                36 domains. 4,000+ workflows. One interface.

                BioMate spans the full breadth of modern life science research — from raw sequencing data to IND-ready documents. Each domain draws on the community's gold-standard open-source tools, accessible through a plain-English request.

                Genomics & Variant Calling
                GATK HaplotypeCaller, DeepVariant, nf-core/sarek — germline and somatic variant calling, CNV, SV detection.
                Transcriptomics
                STAR, Salmon, DESeq2, edgeR, clusterProfiler — bulk RNA-seq from raw reads to differential expression and pathway enrichment.
                Single-Cell Analysis
                Seurat, Scanpy, SingleR, Cell Ranger — clustering, cell-type annotation, trajectory inference, and condition-level comparisons.
                Structural Biology
                AlphaFold 2 & 3, ESMFold, RoseTTAFold, AutoDock Vina, GROMACS — structure prediction, docking, and molecular dynamics.
                Cryo-EM
                CryoSPARC, RELION — GPU-accelerated single-particle analysis, 2D classification, 3D refinement, and resolution estimation.
                Drug Discovery & ADMET
                Virtual screening, ADMET profiling, PBPK simulation, hERG liability, IND document assembly — full preclinical pipeline.
                Epigenomics
                minfi, DMRcate, bismark — bisulfite sequencing, differentially methylated region calling, and chromatin accessibility analysis.
                Proteomics & Metabolomics
                MaxQuant, MSstats, XCMS — label-free quantification, PTM analysis, and metabolic network modeling.
                Metagenomics & Microbiome
                QIIME 2, Kraken2, MetaPhlAn — amplicon and shotgun metagenomics, taxonomy profiling, and diversity analysis.
                Bioconductor & R Methods
                1,818 Bioconductor packages available through plain-English requests — the full peer-reviewed R ecosystem, no coding required.
                Community Databases
                gnomAD, ClinVar, GTEx, Reactome, KEGG, STRING, OpenTargets, UniProt, PubMed — queryable directly within workflows.
                And 25 more domains…
                Immunology, synthetic biology, imaging, pharmacology, rare disease, cancer biology, and more.
