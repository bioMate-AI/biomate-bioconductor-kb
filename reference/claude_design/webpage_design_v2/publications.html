                Zhang, Y. (2026) · Zenodo

                Structure Grounding Is Not Enough: Real Execution as the Ground Truth for LLM-Generated Bioinformatics Workflows

                Zhang, Y. (2026) · Zenodo

                Bioconductor's formal structure (NAMESPACE, S4 types, BiocViews, vignettes) suppresses LLM hallucination — raising function-citation accuracy from 71.4% to 88.2% — but does not guarantee execution. Introduces execution-grounded validation as the strongest tier of a lexical ⊂ structural ⊂ executional grounding hierarchy.
