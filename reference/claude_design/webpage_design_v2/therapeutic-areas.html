                Therapeutic Areas · 中文

                Immune-mediated disease —
                where we focus.

                An immune system that attacks the body, fails to clear a pathogen, or is evaded by a tumor.
                One engine, built for the immune system, is designed to address all three.

                Immunity that attacks the self

                Autoimmune

                We design therapeutics against the validated drivers of autoimmune inflammation — the deepest part of our target map and data.

                Rheumatoid arthritis · Systemic lupus erythematosus · Sjögren’s · Systemic sclerosis · Vasculitis · Inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn’s, ulcerative colitis) · Psoriasis & psoriatic arthritis · Ankylosing spondylitis · Multiple sclerosis · Myasthenia gravis · Type 1 diabetes

                Immunity that fails to clear

                Chronic infection

                The same immune-design engine, turned to infections the immune system cannot resolve on its own — restoring or redirecting the response.

                Chronic viral hepatitis (HBV) · HIV · Persistent / latent viral infection · Infection-driven immune exhaustion

                Immunity that is evaded

                Immuno-oncology

                Making immunologically “cold” tumors visible and targetable. Our lead program is a cyclic-peptide radioligand for pancreatic cancer.

                Lead: Pancreatic cancer · Immunologically cold solid tumors · Checkpoint-resistant cancers

                How to read this

                One engine. Many targets.
                Honest about stage.

                Autoimmune and immuno-oncology are where our engine and data are strongest today; chronic infection is an extension of the same engine. Disease areas reflect where our design engine and proprietary data are deepest; target mechanisms are drug-blind rediscoveries from a human-genetics-anchored cross-disease analysis. Programs are discovery-stage unless marked as lead.
