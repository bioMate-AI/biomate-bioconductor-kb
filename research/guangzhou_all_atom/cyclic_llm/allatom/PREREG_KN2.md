# PRE-REGISTRATION KN-2: simulated metal-aware co-folding assay along the knowledge relaxation chain (committed 2026-10-27 BEFORE any candidate is folded)

Purpose. KN-1 characterised the design space (mechanism features, freedom to operate, novelty) without activity. KN-2 asks whether relaxing knowledge keeps the candidate able to ENGAGE the target mechanism in a simulated assay. This is a simulated assay, NOT affinity: earlier internal results show co-folding ipTM saturates and is not an affinity discriminator, so no potency, ranking or affinity claim is made.
Assay. Boltz-2 co-folding of the curated metal-complete αvβ6 headpiece (derived from PDB 9XMM; MIDAS, ADMIDAS and SyMBS Mn and Ca supplied as ion entities, receptor and metals as in the validated setup) with one cyclic peptide; 5 diffusion samples, seed 0; samples ranked by model confidence; the top-ranked sample is scored. ENGAGEMENT E(x) = 1 iff ligand-ipTM >= 0.80 AND the minimum distance from either carboxylate oxygen of the Asp of the RGD motif to the MIDAS Mn is <= 2.6 Å. Peptides without an RGD motif have no such Asp; for them E = 0 by definition (the assay is a mechanism-engagement assay for an RGD-dependent target).
Controls run in every batch (3 seeds each): positive = Trivehexin monomer and avebehexin (known binders, coordinate MIDAS in the validated setup); negative = the four non-active peptides listed in the target knowledge base.
CALIBRATION GATE (must pass before any candidate is interpreted): both positive controls engage (E = 1) in >= 2 of 3 seeds, and at least 3 of the 4 negative controls do not engage in any seed. If the gate fails, KN-2 reports "assay not admissible" and makes no claim; the assay is not retuned after seeing candidate results.
Candidates (seed 20261027, unique valid HELM from the KN-1 samplers): L0 fixed scaffold n = 100 (of its 180); L1 pharmacophore restricted to the claim's own nine-residue form n = 100; L1 other ring sizes (7, 8, 10, 11) n = 100; L2 RGD only n = 100 drawn from candidates that contain R-G-D; L3 unconstrained n = 100.
Outcome. Engagement rate e(s) per group with 95% Wilson intervals. Pre-stated expectations (so they can be wrong): e(L0) high; e(L1, nine-residue) lower than e(L0); e(L3) near 0 (no RGD).
CLAIM_KN2 ("relaxing to the pharmacophore keeps engagement"): the lower 95% bound of e(L1, nine-residue) exceeds 0.5 times the point estimate e(L0). Otherwise the result is reported as relaxation losing engagement. Per-candidate results are saved; no candidate is dropped.
Disclosure. The assay inherits the limitations of co-folding (single receptor conformation, canonical-backbone approximation for non-canonical residues, over-close metal distances in raw samples); engagement is pose feasibility, not binding free energy. Wet-laboratory confirmation is planned for the next version.

Clarification (same day, before any candidate was folded; no result involved). Implementation of "the MIDAS Mn": in the stored validated control complexes (run cofold-v2-9xmm-20261014T164613Z) the three Mn ions are named M1-M3; the RGD Asp coordinates M3 (0.81 A and 1.91 A for the two controls) and M3 is the only ion coordinated by the beta6 chain (Ser125 OG, Glu223); M1 and M2 are coordinated only by alphaV residues. The scorer therefore identifies the MIDAS ion by rule as "the Mn nearest to a beta6 serine OG" (not by ion order). The thresholds (ligand-ipTM >= 0.80, Asp carboxylate O to MIDAS Mn <= 2.6 A) are unchanged. Scorer: kn2_assay.py, tested on trimmed real control complexes (fixtures/) and synthetic negatives.
