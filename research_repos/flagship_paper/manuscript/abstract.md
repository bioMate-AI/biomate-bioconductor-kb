# Abstract

**Interactive Evidence-Graded QC Governance and Auto-Loop Convergence for Drug Discovery Workflows**

Drug discovery workflows in genomics, proteomics, cryo-EM, ADMET, and pharmacokinetics depend on hundreds of threshold-dependent quality-control (QC) decisions per run, yet current automated platforms either silently filter borderline data or report metrics without explaining their provenance or scientific weight. No existing system pairs interactive, evidence-graded QC governance with autonomous parameter convergence inside a single regulatory-traceable architecture. We present BioMate, a 25-domain Nextflow platform whose 57 QC gates are each annotated GOLD (citation-anchored consensus), SILVER (method-specific best practice), or BRONZE (community heuristic), and whose five wired auto-loop domains (ADMET, CYP-DDI, NCA-PK, docking, PBPK) iteratively tune parameters under those gates. Across four drug-development benchmarks, auto-loops moved gate-pass rates from 20–50% to 85.7–100% within three iterations; protocol-aware profiles eliminated 100% of false-rejections of biology-valid marginal samples observed under default thresholds (HTS, LNP, cryo-EM); per-domain gate-pass tracked provenance grade with descriptive Pearson r=0.977 (N=4) and an omnibus per-scenario chi-squared p=0.010, while the pre-registered Fisher's exact GOLD-vs-BRONZE contrast did not reach the conventional p<0.05 threshold (p=0.106) and a sensitivity analysis excluding adversarial scenarios yielded Fisher p=0.0044; and audit completeness reached 1.0 against 0.0 for baseline pipelines (Mann-Whitney U p=1.57×10⁻⁴). BioMate enables regulatory-traceable AI-assisted drug discovery. Auto-loop coverage is currently five domains; remaining twenty are gate-only, and benchmarks are synthetic-but-anchored to published datasets.
