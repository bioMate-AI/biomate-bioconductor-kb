## 1. Purpose
Define acceptable use of BioMate systems, devices, data, and accounts by personnel. Supports CC6.7.

## 2. Scope
All BioMate personnel (employees, contractors) and any device or account used to access BioMate systems or data.

## 3. Policy statements
1. **Authorized use.** BioMate systems and data are used only for legitimate business purposes and only with the access granted.
2. **Credentials.** Personnel protect their credentials, do not share accounts, and use the company IdP and `[CONFIRM: password manager / MFA]` where required.
3. **Devices.** Devices accessing Restricted/Confidential data must have `[CONFIRM: disk encryption, screen lock, current OS/patches; MDM enrollment if/when deployed]`. Do not claim MDM enrollment unless a real MDM is in place.
4. **Data handling.** Restricted/Confidential data is not copied to unauthorized locations, personal accounts, or unencrypted media. Production data is not used in non-production without authorization and de-identification.
5. **Secrets.** Secrets are never committed to source control, pasted into third-party tools, or shared over insecure channels.
6. **Prohibited.** No attempts to bypass security controls, no unauthorized software that handles company data, no use of company systems for unlawful activity.
7. **Third-party & AI tools.** Use of external tools (including AI/LLM tools) with company or customer data is governed by the AI Acceptable Use Policy.
8. **Reporting.** Suspected security incidents or policy violations are reported promptly per the Incident Response Policy.

## 4. Roles & responsibilities
- **All personnel** — comply and acknowledge annually.
- **Security owner** — maintains the policy and acknowledgment records.

## 5. Enforcement & 6. Review
Per the Information Security Policy. Acknowledged on hire and annually; reviewed annually.
