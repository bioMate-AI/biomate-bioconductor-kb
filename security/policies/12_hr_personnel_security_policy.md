## 1. Purpose
Define security requirements across the personnel lifecycle — hiring, onboarding, ongoing, and offboarding. Supports CC1.4 and CC6.2.

## 2. Scope
All BioMate employees and contractors.

## 3. Policy statements
1. **Background checks.** Background checks are performed where required and legally permissible prior to or shortly after hire, and documented. `[CONFIRM: current process/provider — if none, this is a gap to close.]`
2. **Confidentiality & policy acknowledgment.** Personnel sign confidentiality/IP agreements and acknowledge the Acceptable Use, AI Acceptable Use, and Information Security policies on hire and annually.
3. **Onboarding.** Access is provisioned on documented approval, least-privilege, through the IdP `[CONFIRM]`; security training is assigned (see Security Awareness Policy). Onboarding is evidenced in `[CONFIRM: HRIS/ticketing]`.
4. **Roster / source of truth.** The current list of employees and contractors is maintained in `[CONFIRM: HRIS]` and used as the population source for audits.
5. **Role changes.** Access is adjusted to match new responsibilities and re-reviewed.
6. **Offboarding.** On termination, access to all systems and data is revoked within `[CONFIRM: e.g., 24 hours]`, devices/credentials are recovered, and the action is evidenced in `[CONFIRM: ticketing]`.
7. **Contractors.** Contractors are subject to equivalent access, confidentiality, and offboarding requirements.

## 4. Roles & responsibilities
- **CEO/HR** — owns the lifecycle process and records.
- **Security owner / Engineering** — provision/deprovision access.
- **Managers** — initiate onboarding/offboarding, notify of terminations promptly.

## 5. Enforcement & 6. Review
Per the Information Security Policy. Reviewed annually.
