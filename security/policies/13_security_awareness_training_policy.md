## 1. Purpose
Ensure personnel understand their security responsibilities and current threats. Supports CC1.4 and CC2.2.

## 2. Scope
All BioMate employees and contractors.

## 3. Policy statements
1. **Training on hire.** Security awareness training is completed within `[CONFIRM: e.g., 30 days]` of hire and covers acceptable use, data handling, phishing, incident reporting, and — given BioMate's stack — AI/LLM data-handling.
2. **Annual training.** All personnel complete refresher training at least annually.
3. **Tracking.** Assignment and completion are tracked and retained as evidence in `[CONFIRM: training tool / Vanta]`. (No training tool was found in the repo — selecting and operating one is a gap to close before fieldwork.)
4. **Role-based training.** Engineers with production access receive additional secure-development and access-handling guidance.
5. **HIPAA training.** Where PHI is in scope, HIPAA workforce training is included.
6. **Awareness.** Timely notices are issued for significant new threats or incidents.

## 4. Roles & responsibilities
- **Security owner** — assigns, tracks, and retains training records.
- **All personnel** — complete assigned training on time.

## 5. Enforcement & 6. Review
Per the Information Security Policy. Reviewed annually.
