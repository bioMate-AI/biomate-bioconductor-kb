Evidence-Graded QC

GOLD / SILVER / BRONZE — so you know what you can publish.

Every run is evaluated against 32 domain-specific QC profiles. BioMate grades outputs with GOLD, SILVER, or BRONZE classification — and explains exactly which metrics passed or failed.

  • 32 protocol-aware QC profiles across all domains
  • Thresholds sourced from published community guidelines (ENCODE and equivalent)
  • Inline QC report embedded in every run
  • BRONZE runs flagged with specific remediation steps

Reproducibility & Audit

A complete record of every decision — forever.

BioMate maintains an immutable audit trail for every run: which parameters were set, which were changed from defaults, what the AI reasoned, and what the QC outcome was. Export to PDF or JSON at any time.

  • Full parameter provenance with changedFrom history
  • AI decision reasoning captured per workflow
  • Draft Methods section generated automatically
  • One-click export for peer review or regulatory submission
