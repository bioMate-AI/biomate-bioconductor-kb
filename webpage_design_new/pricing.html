FAQ

Common questions

How is compute billed?
Premium and Enterprise plans include a monthly compute allowance. GPU-intensive workflows (cryo-EM, MD simulation) consume more compute credits than standard genomics runs. You see an estimated cost before every run — no surprise bills.
Can I use BioMate with my own AWS account?
Yes, Enterprise plans support private / VPC deployment where BioMate runs entirely within your institution's AWS account. Your data never leaves your infrastructure.
Is there a free trial for the Premium plan?
Yes — all new accounts start with a 14-day Premium trial at no cost. No credit card required to start. If you are in the pilot program, your access continues until the pilot concludes.
Does BioMate support HIPAA-compliant workflows?
SOC 2 Type II certification is in progress. Enterprise customers can deploy BioMate within their own HIPAA-compliant AWS environment today using the private deployment option. We provide the compliance documentation package to support your institution's review process.
Can core facilities use BioMate for multiple PI labs?
Yes — Enterprise plans include per-lab isolation and billing, making BioMate suitable for core facilities that serve 10–40+ PI labs from a single account. Usage reports are exportable per PI for chargeback purposes.
What workflows are included?
2,000+ validated workflows across drug discovery, genomics, cryo-EM, multi-omics, structural biology, preclinical, and systems biology. Enterprise plans can include custom workflow development for domain-specific pipelines.

Running a core facility or pharma team?

We offer private deployment, multi-lab management, and custom workflow development. Let's talk about what BioMate looks like at your institution.
